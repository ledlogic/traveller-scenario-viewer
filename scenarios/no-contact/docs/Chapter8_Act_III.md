## Chapter 8: Act III: Morning, "Dig Them Out"

## 8.1: ACT III: MORNING, "DIG THEM OUT" (~75 MINUTES)

Dust storm Stage 3. The surface patrols have been pulled in. The only quiet way to reach the closed Areas 12–14 is **under** the ground.

**Ambiance:** the Feed Line pump hum from earlier in the night (Chapter 2, §2.29) is gone if the Reserve has gone to Black. Let the quiet land before anyone says anything.

### The scenes

1. **The Maintenance Yard (Area 9).** Mole driver Gus Teller and the Reserve's two moles (Chapter 5, §5.4). Persuade him, steal a mole, or bring your own driver (Drive (mole)). See §8.4.
2. **Where are they?** The location depends on Chapter 7, §7.6: roll the team's fate or choose. Captured crew are sedated in the Orchard (Area 13); trapped crew are in a Terrace void (Area 12); lost crew are in the meadows or the Sink; held crew are in the tower's Incident Room (Area 7).
3. **Digging.** The mole's route is soil under Area 12 and rock beneath the Orchard (Chapter 5, §5.4). Drilling the Mass raises the Alert track and calls the Waders. See §8.5.
4. **The rescue.** Open an Egg-bloom, free a trapped team member, find a lost one, or break a held one out of the Incident Room (Chapter 7, §7.6). See §8.6 (the Terrace void) and §8.7 (the Orchard).
5. **The ship.** Reach the *Soft Landing* (Chapter 5, §5.5), 11 km north of Area 13. Any **two fobs** or Okafor-Brandt's codeword unlock it. See §8.8.
6. **Lift before dawn.** At about 05:45 the storm passes. If Voss has signalled (Chapter 4, §4.6), an Imperial cutter arrives.

**Voss's endgame plays out here** (Chapter 4, §4.6): he has either signalled an Imperial cutter, or the party stopped him earlier (dead, bound, aboard, or his transmitter destroyed). Resolve with an escape, a firefight, a confrontation at the ship's hatch, or a tense stand-down, depending on how play went.

**What success looks like.** Best case: all four of the pickup are out and aboard. Middle case: some are out; the others are in the Mass. Floor: the **fobs and the codeword** get at least two PCs off Jorjor. The Mass keeps the rest (Chapter 9, §9.3).

## 8.2: WHAT THEY KNOW (IF FREED)

- **Why they were early.** Wei told them to "check that nothing has gone bad." They went to look at the one odd site in an hour's drive.
- **What they saw.** The Reserve's staff were calm *too fast*. They noted one tour group member who never came back from the Terrace.
- **What the Mass did.** Hale will describe the sensation of "being counted." She believes it is one huge mind.
- **About Wei.** Okafor-Brandt received the same order the PCs got ("no loose threads"). She doesn't know whether Wei burned the six, is compromised, or is running a test; she was told to extract **whoever is there**. Her loyalties are to Wei; she will want to know who the Imperial plant is.
- **What to do now.** Get to the ship, lift before dawn.

## 8.3: GETTING OFF PLANET

The *Soft Landing* (Chapter 5, §5.5) sits in a dry wash **11 km north of Area 13**, under sensor-shadow. Reaching it, unlocking it, and lifting off is the final scene.

- **To unlock the ship:** any **two** of the four **key fobs** (Chapter 5, §5.6). If the crew are lost, the ship's cache can be opened with the captain's **spoken codeword**, found on a rescued crew member or in her tent.
- **To lift:** the ship can fly itself with a PC at the controls. A pilot is better (Pilot (spacecraft)); an autopilot hop out of the storm is possible.
- **The Imperium.** If Voss reached his transmitter and was not stopped (Chapter 4, §4.6), an ISS cutter arrives at **dawn (≈05:45)**. The ship must be airborne before then.

## 8.4: SET PIECE: THE YARD AND THE MOLE

**Time:** about 02:00 to 02:30. **Mood:** quiet, mechanical, a little desperate. The Maintenance Yard (Area 9) is a gravel lot, two long sheds and a light on a pole, all humming in the storm. Gus Teller (Chapter 4, §4.11) is alone with **Mole-1 "Maggie"** (running) and **Mole-2 "Lazarus"** (needs a fan belt, Chapter 5, §5.4). Quill's orders on his clipboard say "no vehicle leaves."

- **Persuade Teller** (Average, 8+). He lost a nephew in Y1113 and wants to help but is afraid of the paperwork. DM +2 if the party shows him Pell's whiteboard or admits what is in the Orchard. Success: he drives Maggie himself, or hands over the keys on a signed work order. Failure: he stalls, and every ten minutes adds a ranger patrol.
- **Take a mole without him.** Drive (mole) check (Average, 8+) to start and run one. Dex is the only PC trained, but any PC can try with Drive 0 at a DM of -2. Failure stalls the engine for 1D minutes.
- **Mole-2.** A fan belt, Mechanic (Average, 8+), 30 minutes, DM +1 if two PCs work together. A second mole doubles the party's options: one team under Area 12, one team on the surface.
- **The cost of being seen.** A ranger pair arrives if the table is slow: a Persuade or Deception check (Average, 8+), or a firefight (+1 AP per burst).

## 8.5: SET PIECE: THE DIG

**Time:** about 02:30 to 03:45. **Mood:** claustrophobic. Every kilometre is a different kind of ground, and the Mass is somewhere on the other side of the hull.

The route is **soil from Area 9 to Area 12**, 3.5 km: about 55 minutes in Maggie (4 km/h) or 70 minutes in Lazarus (3 km/h). From there the Terrace tunnels run toward the Sink. The roots of the Orchard are another kilometre of **rock** (1 km/h, about an hour), or the table can go through the Mass itself at 5 to 6 km/h and pay **+1 AP per ten minutes** while Waders converge on the vibration.

**Lost crew will not wait.** Lost pickup members reach the Sink at about 03:00 (Chapter 7, §7.6). A table with one mole should choose: dig for the trapped and captured, or split a surface party to sweep the Drift Meadows. The Mass has the same clock the party does.

Roll **1D6 every 30 minutes underground** (or choose):

| 1D6 | What happens | Who rolls |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Quiet.** The mole hums, the dust falls past the lamps, nothing happens. Let the table breathe. | none |
| 2 | **Warm pocket.** The Mass has heated a void ahead of the drill. The cabin fills with damp heat. | END Average (8+) or 1D damage; Survival gives DM +1 |
| 3 | **Soft ground.** Roots snag the cutting head; the whole mole shudders. | Drive (mole) Average (8+) or +1 AP and Waders turn toward the sound |
| 4 | **Old tunnel.** The Y1113 collapse breaks into the path. | Recon or Drive (mole) Average (8+) to see it first, otherwise 2D damage to the mole |
| 5 | **The hum.** Through the hull comes a sound like many sleepers breathing, and the lamps pulse in time with it. | END Average (8+) for each PC, or DM -1 on their next check |
| 6 | **Gas seep.** A vent of stale spore air bleeds into the cab. | Survival Routine (6+) or the filters wear out an hour early |

## 8.6: SET PIECE: THE TERRACE VOID

**Time:** about 03:15 and later. **Mood:** a cramped, warm hole, slow dread, the clock counting down. If the **Trapped (T)** result applies (Chapter 7, §7.6), the pickup is in a void under Area 12 with **12 hours of air** (from 16:20, so about 04:20) and a leg wound that deals roughly 1D per hour untreated.

- **Finding the void.** Pell's whiteboard says "shouting from the tunnel, 17:40." A Recon or Investigate check (Average, 8+) in the right tunnel finds the voice. Electronics (sensors) shortens the search.
- **Breaching.** Mole into the shelf from below (Drive (mole) Average, 8+) or cut through from the tunnel (Mechanic or Explosives, Difficult, 10+, **Explosives +2 AP** and a risk of collapsing the shelf onto the party).
- **Stabilising Delacroix.** Medic (Average, 8+), 1D minutes. Success stops the damage; a failure costs another 1D damage and a second try. Yeva is the table's trained medic.
- **The tell.** The survivors have not been alone. Something has been **tapping on the rock** for hours, in time with their pulses.

## 8.7: SET PIECE: THE ORCHARD

**Time:** about 03:45 to 04:45. **Mood:** horror. This is the scene the scenario is built for. Dust storm Stage 3: every Egg-bloom is **sealed tight**, so the Orchard is the quietest place in the Reserve. The party arrives from below, through a hole in the roots, into a grove of more than thirty egg-shaped plants, six to ten metres tall, wet, warm and faintly glowing. Through the petals, if a lamp is held close, there are **shapes standing upright**, eyes closed, mouths a little open.

**What the party can see and hear.**
- The grove is not silent. It is a slow, even sound, like a very large room full of people asleep.
- The blooms with living occupants are **warmer and brighter** than the empty ones. Electronics (sensors) or any thermal viewer halves the search time; otherwise it is a Recon or Investigate check (Average, 8+) per row.
- **Sedated sleepers murmur** fragments of their last conversations, in their own voices. If Okafor-Brandt is in one, she says "no loose threads" in her own voice, the order Wei sent.
- A **Wader** is in the grove, motionless, blind in the storm. It hunts by smell and vibration (Chapter 2, §2.30). It does not like the mole, and it does not like gunfire.

**Who else is in there.** The pickup are not the only ones. One bloom holds a member of the tour group lost **12 days ago** (§2.22), thin and warm. Another holds a ranger Quill "relocated" last winter. Every bloom that is cut or lamped open raises the question of whether the table can carry them out. Leave nobody behind, or choose, and live with it.

**Getting someone out** (Chapter 2, §2.30):
- **Warm lamp:** Difficult (10+) during Stage 3, 1D minutes, silent, no AP.
- **Cut the calyx:** Hits 25, no AP, the bloom empties. Vibro-blades are fast; slugs are slow.
- **Blast it open:** Explosives, **+2 AP**, and the shriek carries a kilometre. Fast, and it will bring the Waders.
- **Cut the petals:** Armour 5 in the storm. A bad idea unless the grove is already awake.

**When someone comes out.** A freed sleeper falls limp and must be carried. Medic (Average, 8+) brings them round in 1D rounds, coughing spores, disoriented, and asking where the others are. Do not let the party rush this scene. Give each PC a decision about who they carry.

**If the grove wakes.** A pollen burst, a shriek, a gunshot, or the second bloom cut raises AP and the Wader turns. A Wader grapples (Melee 2, STR 12): STR check to break, six metres a round toward the nearest open bloom. The **escape** is the horror: the whole grove begins to open at once, petals sliding apart, the sleepers inside turning their heads.

## 8.8: SET PIECE: THE RUN AND THE HATCH

**Time:** about 04:45 to 05:45. **Mood:** a race against the weather and the clock. The storm passes at about **05:00** (Stage 4), the sky lightens, and an Imperial cutter may arrive at **05:45** if Voss has signalled.

**Getting to the wash** (11 km north of Area 13, Chapter 5, §5.5). Options: the **Gravy Train** along the service track (about 30 minutes in clear air, Drive Average, 8+, Difficult in the last of the storm), on foot (about 3 hours at 4 km/h, longer with the injured), or **Maggie** through soil, which is too slow. A table that left the rig at the Terminal has a choice to make about what they will carry and what they will leave.

**The hatch.** Any two fobs (Chapter 5, §5.6) open it, and the cockpit log and fob link (Chapter 5, §5.5) tell the table where the team went and who is alive, or Okafor-Brandt's codeword *("Bluebird, no reply")* from inside. If Voss is alive and has followed the party here, this is where it plays out (Chapter 4, §4.6):

- **The signal.** If Voss already sent one, a TL10+ emission is on record (+1 AP, Electronics (comms) Difficult, 10+, to notice it was sent). The cutter arrives at 05:45.
- **The confrontation.** Voss wants a seat or the ship. He has a pistol and whatever the party left him. Gun Combat, Melee, or Persuade (Difficult, 10+) to talk him down.
- **The stand-down.** Voss stays behind, the table lets him walk, or he asks for a deal.
- **Wei.** If Okafor-Brandt is alive she wants to know who the plant is (§8.2). Persuade or Deception (Average, 8+) decides what she is told.

**Lift-off.** If Okafor-Brandt is at the controls, there is no roll. Otherwise Pilot (spacecraft) 1+ needs Routine (6+); no Pilot skill needs Average (8+) with DM -1 (autopilot). Corrin is the only PC with Pilot (spacecraft). **The ship must be airborne before 05:45.**
