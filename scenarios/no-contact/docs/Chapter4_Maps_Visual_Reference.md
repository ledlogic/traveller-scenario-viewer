## 4.1 — LAYOUT & ADJACENCY

Six spaces, single-story, roadside: **Office**, **Lobby**, **Room 1**, **Room 2**,
**Room 3**, **Room 4**, arranged in that order along the building's face, with a
gravel parking lot in front and the highway beyond it.

| Space | Occupants | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Office | Motel owner (NPC) | Appendix A |
| Lobby | Shared — vending, jukebox, sometimes food | Appendix C |
| Room 1 | The Cultist / The Bulb Man (SolSec pair) | Chapter 6, §6.1–6.2 |
| Room 2 | Psychic Medium / The Runaway (SolSec pair) | Chapter 6, §6.3–6.4 |
| Room 3 | Tabloid Reporter / Mob Enforcer (SolSec pair) | Chapter 6, §6.5–6.6 |
| Room 4 | Del & Priya Ashworth (civilian NPCs) | Appendix C |
| Restaurant | Semi-closed, separate structure across the lot | Appendix D |

**Adjacency:** every neighboring pair in the sequence above (Office/Lobby,
Lobby/Room 1, Room 1/Room 2, Room 2/Room 3, Room 3/Room 4) shares a thin wall —
raised voices, a struggle, or a gunshot next door are plausibly overheard by whoever's
on either side.

**The lobby is the only route** from any guest room to the parking lot and highway.
Every guest, PC or civilian, passes through it to come or go, which makes it the
natural social crossroads of the night — see Appendix C for its random event table.

The **restaurant** is a separate structure across the gravel lot, connected by a
covered walkway rather than sharing any wall with the main building — it sits
outside the thin-wall adjacency chain above, and outside the lobby's normal foot
traffic. Reaching it means a deliberate choice to step outside. See Appendix D.
