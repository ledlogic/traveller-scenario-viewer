# TRAVELLER: NO CONTACT
## Chapter 1: Introduction

---

## CONVENTION BLURB (Con of the North)

Six strangers. One roadside motel on the edge of a dying desert world. Each of you is a Solomani Security agent told to lie low at Motel Yermo and wait for extraction back across the border. The ride never comes. The storm has cut the phones, the diner is far too quiet, and something out on the flats is being fed. Includes material integrated from Mystery Flesh Pit National Park. Mongoose Traveller 2e, pre-generated characters, new players welcome.

*(452 characters)*

---

## 1.1: PREMISE

**When:** day 245 of Y1116 (Imperial date 245-1116), a single night. The history behind Jorjor and the Rim is in Chapter 2, §2.20.

All six player characters are **Solomani Security (SolSec) assets** who have each been told, separately, to go to ground at a specific three-room motel, **Motel Yermo** *(Spanish, "wasteland/barren place"; matches the hellworld texture of Jorjor itself)*.  They were told to wait for expected extraction back across the border to Solomani Confederation space. Each was also told they'd be paired with a partner for this job (sharing a room, a cover story, and the wait for extraction) but each received that instruction independently, and most pairs have never actually met their partner in person before tonight. Verifying that the person who shows up matching your partner's description really is who they say they are is part of the job, not a given. No pair was told anything about the other two rooms; as far as any given pair knows, they may be the only SolSec presence at the motel tonight. Each pair has a cover identity, a matched pair of motel-guest personas, they believe is unique to them.

Every PC received some form of this last transmission, days before tonight, as a relayed telegram or coded HAM message:

> *"By now I hope you've completed your mission. Now meet up with your partner, identified by the codename provided, at ¿wasteland hotel. Extraction is at 2140 standard on 245-1116. Do not leave lingering traces to this location; we intend to use it again. That means no loaner vehicle, no police charges on record, nothing that puts a name or a transaction anywhere near this address. Understand: no loose threads. - WeiVern"*

Extraction is set for **21:40 standard time (9:40 PM) on day 245 of Y1116**, at the motel. Over one night, in three locales, **The Motel / The Highway / The Reserve**, the six agents have to figure out why. Their orders arrive as relayed messages, telegrams and coded HAM traffic signed *WeiVern*, a name every PC knows to be **Baldwin Wei** (canon SolSec officer, *Signal GK*, Chapter 4, §4.13). Communications with SolSec have been kept deliberately minimal in case Imperial Security is listening, and old physical channels, telegrams and HAM radio carrying encoded messages, leave less exposure for SolSec on this side of the Imperial border than any modern link. Wei does not need to appear in person; his silence denotes the unconcerned oversight he is providing to the mission.

The extraction team (Chapter 4, §4.8), which consists of four SolSec agents aboard the courier-sloop *Soft Landing* (Chapter 5, §5.5), landed a day early, walked into the nearby Halvorsen Basin Biological Reserve (HBBR) (Chapter 2, §2.16–2.31) on a day-tour cover, carrying biological and psionic test equipment they never mentioned to anyone, and got trapped underground. The game is in three acts (Chapters 6–8): **Act I**, notice that something big has happened at the Reserve, survive the Mass's raiders (the gutwings, Chapter 2, §2.30) and decide to go and look; **Act II**, take a stranded feed hauler's big rig through the Reserve's gate; **Act III**, use the Reserve's own mole vehicles to drill to the pickup, or at least to their ship's key fobs, and get off planet before dawn.

## 1.2: SESSION STRUCTURE

The session runs as three acts. Total actual runtime target: **4 hours**, convention slot length.

| Act | Locale | Time at table | Question | Ends when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I: Roll Call** | The Motel | ~75 min | Who's in the group, and who isn't? | The table has a crew for the road, the interlopers are dealt with, and they know where the pickup went. |
| **II: The Big Rig** | The Highway | ~90 min | What happened to our contact? | The rig is running and through the Reserve's gate (or another way in). |
| **III: Dig Them Out** | The Reserve | ~75 min | Can we get them (or their codes) off this planet? | The *Soft Landing* lifts, or doesn't. |

The *Soft Landing* sits in a dry wash just outside the Halvorsen Basin Biological Reserve, and the pickup crew used their spare day to go in, posing as tourists. Whatever else the table decides, the only way off Jorjor runs through the Reserve.

**The dust storm clock** (Chapter 2, §2.31) runs through all three acts: a distant wall in Act I, first gusts in Act II, full storm in Act III, dawn at the end.

## 1.4: PACING NOTES

- Don't let any single room's thread eat more than ~20 minutes uninterrupted; cut between rooms (in Act I) and between the cab and the trailer (in Act II) to keep all six players engaged.
- If the table is moving faster than expected, the Act II complications (§7.1) can be introduced early. If slower, cut them, or reveal them as a false alarm to buy time. In Act III, skip the Maintenance Yard haggling and go to the drill.
- Every action in the Reserve raises the **Alert track** (Chapter 2, §2.28). Use it to keep pressure on without a hard clock.
- For a clock-time reference across all three acts (when the complications land and when the dust storm advances) see Chapter 1, §1.3.

## 1.5: DISCOVERABLE THROUGH PLAY

What the table can find out, and where it lives. Use this mid-session instead of reconstructing the knowledge landscape from memory across the other documents.

- **The TL gear gap** (§5.1), the TL12 receiver in Voss's car and the military kit in his trunk, surfaced through Recon, Electronics checks or sharp in-fiction observation, never handed over for free.
- **The Reserve armoury** (Chapter 2, §2.29, Area 7): known beforehand to Dabney, confirmed by Voss, and a later stop for the whole party.
- **The gutwings** (Chapter 2, §2.30; Chapter 6, §6.4): proof the Reserve has changed, and a torn strip of the pickup's jacket.
- **Highway traffic table results** (Chapter 2, §2.8) and **lobby random events** (Chapter 2, §2.12), mostly ambient texture, occasionally real signal buried in it.
- **The Fence-Line Hum** and **the Big Queue** (Chapter 2, §2.9; Chapter 2, §2.10): rumor-tier local color, deliberately never confirmed one way or the other.
- **Del's broken rig and the Ashworths' CB gossip** (Chapter 4, §4.2), mundane, intentionally ruled-out red herrings.
- **The pickup's day** (Chapter 6, §6.2; Chapter 4, §4.2): the cook, the washboy's Solomani coin, and Del's CB gossip about shots and a bang at the Reserve. Any two point at the Gut (Chapter 6, §6.1).
- **The Reserve's cover-up** (Chapter 2, §2.23; Chapter 4, §4.11): Pruitt's Form 11-C count and her list of missing names; the Incident Room; the Gate ledger entry for the pickup's permit #0614.
- **The paper trail** (Chapter 7, §7.7); the Gate's false "departed" stamp, the Clinic's "S. Hall," Pell's whiteboard, Teller's work orders; each office has a slice, spelled differently, and the contradictions are the clue to where the pickup really is.
- **What the Mass is and does** (Chapter 2, §2.30), Drifters, Waders and Egg-blooms; the way it reacts to psionics, gunfire and explosives.
- **The hang-up call** (Chapter 7, §7.2): a pulse and layered voices on the front-desk line at 1:35 AM. It is the Mass tapping the comms through the Feed Line's telemetry cable, traceable with Electronics (comms).
- **Voss's observations** (Chapter 4, §4.6): the pickup walking into the Reserve at 11:30, the closed gate, the covered stretcher at dusk, and the sealed cases carried into the Tower last week.
