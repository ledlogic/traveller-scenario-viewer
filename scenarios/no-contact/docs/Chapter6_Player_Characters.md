## Chapter 6 — Player Characters

*Referee Reference Document*

## 6.0 — HOW TO USE THIS CHAPTER

All six guests — three room pairs — are here in one place. Each guest has two
full versions: **Version: Confederation** (a genuine SolSec agent) and
**Version: Imperium** (the Imperial mole). Exactly one PC across the whole
table is secretly Version: Imperium; the assignment mechanic is in Chapter 2.

A player's Page 1 is identical in shape (though not in content) whether
they're later handed a Confederation or Imperium Page 2, so nobody can infer
their own assignment — or anyone else's — from which packet they got first.

**Characteristics:** each version has a full UPP (STR/DEX/END/INT/EDU/SOC) and
standard MGT2e DMs, matched in total between a guest's two versions the same
way skills are — only the distribution shifts to fit that version's emphasis.
Standard DM table: 2 or less −2, 3–5 −1, 6–8 +0, 9–11 +1, 12–14 +2, 15 +3.

## 6.1 — ROOM 1, GUEST 1: "THE CULTIST"

**Surface bio:** All harsh angles and severe clothes. Never seems to blink. Speaks
quietly, but perfectly audible from yards away. Whatever they're proselytizing, it's
nothing you'd take your mother to.

### "Corvin Danh" — Version: Confederation

SolSec sleeper, three years deep-cover running a fringe religious front as an
intelligence-gathering and money-laundering operation.

**UPP:** 677899 *(STR 6 (0), DEX 7 (0), END 7 (0), INT 8 (0), EDU 9 (+1), SOC 9 (+1))*

**Recent history — how they got here:** Arrived two days ago via a hired local
driver, paid in advance in cash and given no reason to return. The driver dropped
Corvin at the highway turnoff and continued on toward the regional town without
looking back.

**Skills:** Persuade 2, Deception 1, Streetwise 1, Religion 0, Religion (SolSec front) 2,
Melee 0, Melee (unarmed) 1

**Gear:** TL10 short-range burst transmitter disguised as a prayer pendant; TL11
chemical truth-suppressant disguised as "sacrament" wine.

**Neural.AI image prompt:** A gaunt figure in severe, dark, high-collared clothing, all
harsh angles, unblinking pale eyes fixed calmly on the viewer. Standing in a dim,
low-budget 1970s-style motel room lit by a single bare bulb, cheap wood paneling
behind them. Quiet, unsettling stillness — hands folded, no visible threat, but
utterly wrong for the room. Photorealistic, science fiction character portrait, no
text, no logos.

---

**Secret objective:** Holds the emergency rendezvous protocol — the *real* fallback
extraction point, memorized, never written down. **Format is the owning player's
call, not the referee's:** before or at the top of the session, ask whoever's
playing Corvin (Confederation) whether they want this represented as a physical prop
(a card, a written cipher, a folded note) they can actually hand to another
player at the table, or kept purely verbal/in-fiction. Whichever they pick,
honor it consistently for the rest of the session — this is their character's
detail to own.

**Carrying:** Nothing physical beyond the rendezvous protocol itself, held purely in
memory unless the owning player has chosen to make it a physical prop (see above)
— either way, the single most valuable piece of knowledge in the building tonight,
and the reason Corvin is the character other true agents should protect above all
else.

**Tell (honest roleplay):** Fixates on ritual and routine even under stress; the
strange calm cracks only when the rendezvous protocol itself is threatened.

---

### "Corvin Danh" — Version: Imperium

Same name, same cult-front cover — but the front was actually stood up by Imperial
Intelligence eighteen months ago as bait, and Corvin was slotted in to run it.

**UPP:** 686998 *(STR 6 (0), DEX 8 (0), END 6 (0), INT 9 (+1), EDU 9 (+1), SOC 8 (0))*

**Recent history — how they got here:** Arrived the same way, on the same kind of
hired-driver drop-off — Imperial Intelligence deliberately mirrored SolSec's own
arrival tradecraft so nothing about the trip itself would read as unusual.

**Skills:** Persuade 1, Deception 2, Streetwise 1, Religion 0, Religion (front) 2,
Investigate 1

**Gear:** Identical-looking pendant, but it's a TL12 passive recorder/locator, not a
transmitter — it's been *listening*, not calling home.

**Neural.AI image prompt:** A gaunt figure in severe, dark, high-collared clothing, all
harsh angles, unblinking pale eyes fixed calmly on the viewer. Standing in a dim,
low-budget 1970s-style motel room lit by a single bare bulb, cheap wood paneling
behind them. Quiet, unsettling stillness, but with a faint, almost clinical
watchfulness in the eyes rather than devotion. Photorealistic, science fiction
character portrait, no text, no logos.

---

**Secret objective:** Confirm whether SolSec still trusts this front, and burn it from
the inside if so.

**Carrying:** A TL12 audio log covering two nights of "confessional" recordings from
cult-front visitors — unreported to his own Imperial handlers, kept as a private
insurance policy in case his cover here is ever questioned from either direction.

**Tell (honest roleplay):** Oddly incurious about the "sacrament" ingredients — a
detail a real true-believer-adjacent operator would obsess over.

## 6.2 — ROOM 1, GUEST 2: "THE BULB MAN"

**Surface bio:** Kind of lives here, kind of lives everywhere. Amicable and outgoing,
no concept of "inside voice." No one calls him anything else; no one knows why he's
called that.

### "Dabney Kroll" — Version: Confederation

Low-level courier and lookout, useful specifically because he's dismissed as harmless
local color. Has run message drops through this motel for over a year.

**UPP:** 878654 *(STR 8 (0), DEX 7 (0), END 8 (0), INT 6 (0), EDU 5 (-1), SOC 4 (-1))*

**Recent history — how they got here:** Not a one-way ride tonight — Dabney has lived
motel-adjacent, doing local odd jobs, for over a year. He was folded into SolSec's
courier network without ever having to travel anywhere new; tonight he's simply on
shift, the same as any other night.

**Skills:** Streetwise 2, Recon 2, Stealth 1, Electronics 0, Electronics (comms) 1

**Gear:** TL10 audio bug kit disguised as spare radio tubes; knows every physical
hiding spot in and around the motel grounds.

**Neural.AI image prompt:** A stocky, weathered man in his 50s with a booming,
open-mouthed grin, wearing a stained undershirt and suspenders, holding a coil of wire
and an old vacuum-tube radio chassis like he's mid-repair. Standing in a cluttered
motel utility closet, warm incandescent light, clutter of spare parts around him.
Friendly, guileless energy. Photorealistic, science fiction character portrait, no
text, no logos.

---

**Secret objective:** Physically watches the highway approach all night — he's the
early-warning system, and his silence about *not* seeing extraction traffic is itself a
clue.

**Carrying:** A year's worth of memorized traffic patterns for this stretch of
highway — who normally passes, when, and how often. He's the only PC equipped to
recognize when tonight's traffic (Appendix B, §B.2) is genuinely anomalous rather than
just unfamiliar.

**Tell (honest roleplay):** Genuinely, guilelessly friendly. Talks too much about
mundane motel trivia — which is cover, but also just who he is.

---

### "Dabney Kroll" — Version: Imperium

Same harmless-local cover, but he was placed here first, over a year ago, specifically
to become "furniture" before any SolSec operation used this motel.

**UPP:** 787754 *(STR 7 (0), DEX 8 (0), END 7 (0), INT 7 (0), EDU 5 (-1), SOC 4 (-1))*

**Recent history — how they got here:** Same long-term placement — settled in over a
year ago specifically to be already trusted and unremarkable before any operation
ever arrived, rather than showing up alongside one.

**Skills:** Streetwise 1, Recon 1, Stealth 2, Investigate 2

**Gear:** TL12 miniature directional receiver hidden in a transistor radio he
"tinkers with" constantly — plausible cover for holding it up and pointing it at
rooms.

**Neural.AI image prompt:** A stocky, weathered man in his 50s with a booming,
open-mouthed grin, wearing a stained undershirt and suspenders, holding a coil of wire
and an old vacuum-tube radio chassis like he's mid-repair. Standing in a cluttered
motel utility closet, warm incandescent light, clutter of spare parts around him. Same
friendly surface, but eyes a half-second too focused, too aware. Photorealistic,
science fiction character portrait, no text, no logos.

---

**Secret objective:** Has been quietly logging every guest who's passed through this
motel for a year. Tonight, he confirms which are active.

**Carrying:** A written log, hidden inside the "spare radio tubes," identifying which
guests over the past year showed SolSec tradecraft patterns — cross-referenced
against tonight's arrivals. If recovered by the true agents, this is the single most
damaging physical object in the building to their whole regional network, not just
tonight's cell.

**Tell (honest roleplay):** A little too good at appearing to not be listening — a
beat of a pause before he "notices" you've spoken to him.

## 6.3 — ROOM 2, GUEST 1: "PSYCHIC MEDIUM"

**Surface bio:** Palms read, cards laid, mirrors scried, and — so rumors say — other,
stranger services provided. Probably a liar, maybe a believer, possibly touched. Looks
at you like she knows something.

### "Yeva Kessler" — Version: Confederation

Runs a genuine cold-reading operation as cover for debriefing nervous assets — people
talk to psychics. Skilled interrogator disguised as a mystic.

**UPP:** 566899 *(STR 5 (-1), DEX 6 (0), END 6 (0), INT 8 (0), EDU 9 (+1), SOC 9 (+1))*

**Recent history — how they got here:** Arrived three nights ago by regional bus,
ticket paid one-way in cash, claiming to be "between engagements" as a traveling
performer — true enough as a cover story to hold up to casual questioning.

**Skills:** Persuade 2, Deception 2, Investigate 1, Insight 2

**Gear:** TL10 micro-recorder in a "scrying" pendant; genuinely excellent at reading
people, which she attributes to "the gift" as cover for tradecraft.

**Neural.AI image prompt:** A striking woman in her 40s with dark, heavily-lined eyes
and layered scarves, seated at a small round table draped in velvet, tarot cards laid
out before her, candlelight from a cheap motel-room table lamp. Calm, penetrating gaze
directed straight at the viewer, as if she already knows the answer to a question not
yet asked. Photorealistic, science fiction character portrait, no text, no logos.

---

**Secret objective:** Has already partially debriefed one other guest tonight without
either of them realizing the other was SolSec — a thread the players can pull.

**Carrying:** Partial debrief notes (referee's choice which other guest they concern)
recorded on her micro-recorder pendant — incomplete, but enough to confirm at least
one other person in the building is "more than they appear," without naming who.

**Tell (honest roleplay):** Asks pointed, specific follow-up questions that a real
fortune teller wouldn't need.

---

### "Yeva Kessler" — Version: Imperium

## 6.4 — ROOM 2, GUEST 2: "THE RUNAWAY"

**Surface bio:** Gangly frame, big eyes, doesn't belong here but you can't imagine
where they do. Demeanor like a feral cat and about as skittish. Pays in fistfuls of
sweaty dollar bills.

### "Dex" — Version: Confederation

Youngest asset in the cell, a genuine defector-in-training being smuggled out — not a
hardened agent, an asset in the truest sense: someone SolSec is retrieving.

**UPP:** 697654 *(STR 6 (0), DEX 9 (+1), END 7 (0), INT 6 (0), EDU 5 (-1), SOC 4 (-1))*

**Recent history — how they got here:** Hitched the last fifty kilometers in the back
of a produce truck, dropped off at the highway turnoff with nothing but a jacket and
a rehearsed story about running from a bad home — a story that happens to be mostly
true.

**Skills:** Streetwise 1, Stealth 2, Athletics 0, Athletics (dexterity) 1, Deception 1

**Gear:** TL10 concealed distress beacon sewn into a jacket lining — meant to be
triggered only in genuine extremis.

**Neural.AI image prompt:** A gangly teenager or young adult with wide, wary eyes and
an oversized secondhand jacket, sitting cross-legged on a threadbare motel bed,
knuckles white around a fistful of crumpled dollar bills. Skittish, feral-cat posture,
ready to bolt. Dim, cheap motel lighting. Photorealistic, science fiction character
portrait, no text, no logos.

---

**Secret objective:** Doesn't know the rendezvous protocol or any operational details —
genuinely just wants to get out. The most sympathetic, least suspicious PC, and the
one with the least actual information.

**Carrying:** The distress beacon sewn into the jacket lining — given by a previous
handler with no explanation beyond "only if things go very wrong." Dex has no idea
what it actually does or who it calls, only that it's precious and not to be shown to
anyone.

**Tell (honest roleplay):** Real fear, not performed fear. Flinches at things a
hardened agent wouldn't.

---

---

**Carrying:** The same jacket-lining beacon, identical in appearance — but it's

**Tell (honest roleplay):** Fear is a half-second too controlled — flinches land
exactly when it's useful to be noticed flinching.

## 6.6 — ROOM 3, GUEST 2: "MOB ENFORCER"

**Surface bio:** The perfect intersection of big money and low taste, packaged in a
too-tight suit. To hear them tell it, Popes and Kings quiver when "the boss" sends
them around. True or not, those knuckles stay bruised.

### "Big Tomas Reyes" — Version: Confederation

Muscle and enforcement for the cell — genuinely capable of violence, kept in reserve
unless the extraction goes wrong on the ground.

**UPP:** A79655 *(STR 10 (+1), DEX 7 (0), END 9 (+1), INT 6 (0), EDU 5 (-1), SOC 5 (-1))*

**Recent history — how they got here:** Rode in as hired "muscle" on a legitimate-
looking cargo delivery, the truck continuing on toward the starport after dropping
Tomas at the motel with a single duffel bag — paperwork and all, a completely
ordinary freight run to anyone who checked it.

**Skills:** Melee 0, Melee (unarmed) 2, Gun Combat 0, Gun Combat (slug pistol) 1,
Persuade 2, Streetwise 1

**Gear:** TL10 subdermal reinforcement in the forearms (unarmed strikes hit harder
than they should); revolver is a genuine TL7 period piece, legally carried and
deliberately unremarkable under Jorjor's High Law/controlled-blades regime.

**Neural.AI image prompt:** A hulking man in his 40s squeezed into an ill-fitting,
loud pinstripe suit, thick bruised knuckles resting on a motel dresser, gold rings on
several fingers. Standing with deliberate stillness rather than menace, a faint,
almost tired watchfulness in his expression. Dim motel room, cheap lamp light.
Photorealistic, science fiction character portrait, no text, no logos.

---

**Secret objective:** Under orders to eliminate any asset who breaks under pressure
and threatens the cell, including a fellow "guest."

**Carrying:** A verbal kill-order — no physical evidence, just memorized authorization
for exactly this contingency. The single most dangerous piece of "knowledge" in the
building if the night goes badly, precisely because it leaves no paper trail to
discover.

**Tell (honest roleplay):** The violence is a last resort, not a first instinct — a
real thug postures more than he acts.
