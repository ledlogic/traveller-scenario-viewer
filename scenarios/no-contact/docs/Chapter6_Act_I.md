## 6.1: ACT I: NIGHT, "ROLL CALL" (~75 MINUTES)

Guests arrive and settle in. Small talk, cover stories, first hints something is off; a car that doesn't stop at the motel sign, a payphone call that doesn't connect, a light that shouldn't be on in an empty room. Players start informally sounding each other out without revealing themselves.

**Establish the trap early, quietly:** every PC took a one-way ride to get here, assuming extraction would be their ride out; nobody arranged their own return transport. Layer in two concrete complications rather than vague dread: a **dust storm front is blocking regional highway traffic** (Stage 1: an orange wall on the south-west horizon), and it has also knocked out **local commercial comms** (payphones, the motel's own line). Neither is supernatural or plot-generated; both are checkable, ordinary facts a PC can confirm. Full detail, including a random table for passing traffic that never stops, local flora/fauna texture, and optional unrest/bandit rumors, is in Chapter 2, §2.7–2.9.

**Tech discipline matters from the first scene.** Jorjor runs passive detection at its own TL7 baseline, escalating to the Imperial Security Service if something anomalous is flagged, see Chapter 2, §2.6 for the house rule on checks required when a PC risks using above-TL7 gear.

### The question: who's in the group, and who isn't?

Act I's real work is sorting people.

- **In the group:** the six SolSec guests in Rooms 1–3 (Chapter 3). They each know their *room partner* by codename; they do not know the others.
- **Not in the group (bystanders):** the motel owner (Chapter 4, §4.1), the Ashworths (Chapter 4, §4.2), the restaurant staff (Chapter 4, §4.3). Ordinary people, and genuine bystanders if the night turns violent.
- **The interlopers.** Anyone who should not be riding along, watching, or asking questions: **Deputy Marsh** (Chapter 4, §4.5), the **idling stranger** (Chapter 4, §4.6), a curious Priya, and, the hard one, **the Version: Imperium mole** (Chapter 1, §1.5) among the six.

**Get the interlopers out** means the table makes a decision about each:

| Interloper | Options |
|---|---|
| Deputy Marsh / the stranger | Talk him away (boring, plausible story). Mislead him. Take him out of the picture. The Watcher (if used) can't be fully shaken; he only stops reporting if the party is gone. |
| The Ashworths | Keep them out of it. Del becomes essential in Act II; Priya stays at the motel. |
| The mole | Cut them loose, leave them restrained at the motel, take them along under watch, or *use* them. This is the table's call, and the mole's player decides how loudly to resist. |

**The Roll Call scene.** Around 40–50 minutes in, put the six in a room: Wei's order (Chapter 1, §1.9) in each hand. They compare notes on "no loose threads" and the one-way rides, and someone says the sentence the scenario exists for: *"If they're not coming, why not?"* Do not resolve it. Let the three theories (Wei burned us / Wei's compromised / it's a test) be argued out.

### Act I closes with the first clue to the pickup

By the end of Act I the table should have **two of these three** pieces of information, enough to point at the Reserve:

| Clue | Source | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| The cook | Chapter 4, §4.3 | Four off-worlder-looking hikers ate breakfast at 10:30, asked the road to "the Gut," left a large tip. |
| The washboy | Chapter 4, §4.3 | Saw the four walk the spur road toward the Reserve at 11:30. One woman gave him a coin: **a silver Solomani "Terran" coin** (TL tell). |
| Del's CB | Chapter 4, §4.2 | Heard on the CB: the Reserve "closed up tight" at five o'clock; **shots, then a bang** around four-twenty; areas 12 through 14 are shut. |

If the table is slow, have Del or the cook volunteer it. If fast, add the Reserve's own signage on the highway.

## 6.2: WHAT THEY SAW TODAY: THE PICKUP

Earlier today, four off-worlder-looking hikers passed through the restaurant (Chapter 4, §4.8). The cook and the washboy are the table's best early pointers to the Reserve (Chapter 6, §6.1).

- **The cook** (Act I clue): served four strangers (two women and two men in good boots and field jackets, one younger man carrying a pack too heavy for a tourist) at about 10:30. They ordered strong coffee and pie, asked the road to "the Gut," and left a large cash tip in unfamiliar notes. He tells the story if asked about strangers, or volunteers it if the table is stuck.
- **The washboy** (Act I clue): saw the four walk off down the spur road at about 11:30. A woman handed him a coin as thanks: a **silver Solomani "Terran" coin**, TL10-minted, plainly not Jorjor currency. He is proud of it. A PC with Streetwise, Electronics, or the right Science check can tell it is not Imperial.
