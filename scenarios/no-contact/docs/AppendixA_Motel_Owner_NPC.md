## A.1 — THE MOTEL OWNER

A civilian anchor NPC — a non-player exposition and stakes source who is utterly
unaware any of tonight's events are happening. Useful as:

- A "why should we care if this goes wrong" civilian who could get hurt.
- A red herring PCs suspect first, since they can investigate the owner without
  blowing their own cover.
- A source of mundane setting texture — front-desk gossip, who's stayed here before,
  what the highway traffic normally looks like.

**Name:** Earlene Vance

**Age:** 61

**UPP:** 578986 *(STR 5 (-1), DEX 7 (0), END 8 (0), INT 9 (+1), EDU 8 (0), SOC 6 (0))*

**Career history:** Two terms **Drifter** (Scavenger) out of her teens and
twenties, chasing salvage and odd jobs across a couple of subsectors before the
money ran out and the road did too. Bought into the motel with a partner
twenty-nine years ago; been running it alone since the partner passed. Never
left Jorjor again.

**Skills:** Streetwise 1, Persuade 1, Mechanic 0, Mechanic (repair) 1, Recon 1,
Gambler 1, Carouse 1 *(the drifter years and three decades of running a roadside
business, respectively — no specialization above 0 outside what's listed)*

**Gear:** None of consequence — cash box, a shotgun kept under the front desk (legal,
unremarkable, TL7), a rotary phone.

**Role at the table:** Cash-only, asks-no-questions. Genuinely doesn't know what any
of tonight's guests are. Should never be given secret SolSec/Imperial information by
the referee — the whole point is that they're the one truly innocent presence in the
building.

**Neural.AI image prompt:** A weathered, heavyset woman in her early 60s wearing
a stained cardigan, standing behind a cluttered front desk lit by a flickering
neon "VACANCY" sign visible through the window behind them. Bored, incurious
expression, thumbing through a ledger. 1970s roadside Americana, warm and grimy
lighting. Photorealistic, science fiction character portrait, no text, no logos.
