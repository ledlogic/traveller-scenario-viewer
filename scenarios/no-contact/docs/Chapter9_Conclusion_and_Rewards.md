## 9.1: HOW THE NIGHT ENDS

The session ends when the *Soft Landing* lifts, when dawn arrives with the ship still on the ground, or when the table decides it is done. Score the night on four tracks, then read the matching epilogue in §9.2.

| Track | Best | Middle | Worst |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The pickup** (§4.9, §7.6) | All four freed and aboard | Some freed, the rest left in the Reserve | None freed; only the fobs or the codeword got out |
| **The ship** (§5.5, §8.3) | Lifted before dawn with every PC who survived | Lifted with fewer PCs than arrived | Still on the ground at dawn |
| **The spy** (§4.6) | Contained, turned or traded with before the ship lifted | Alive but not stopped; the cutter is a close call | Free, transmitter intact, cutter at dawn |
| **The Reserve** (§2.28) | Alert never reached Black | Reached Black, but the PCs got out | Black, with staff casualties or a full lockdown |

**Rating the night.**

| Result | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Full success** | Pickup all freed, ship lifted, Voss dealt with, Alert below Black |
| **Success** | Ship lifted with at least two of the pickup, or every surviving PC aboard |
| **Partial** | Ship lifted on the fobs or the codeword alone |
| **Failure** | Ship not lifted by dawn |

## 9.2: EPILOGUES

Read the one that fits, then add the Voss line that fits.

**Full success.** The *Soft Landing* climbs out of the dust with every seat full. Okafor-Brandt does not speak until the planet is a brown disc behind them, and then only to ask who the man in the lot was. Wei's silence has an answer now, and none of them like it.

**Success.** The ship lifts with empty seats. The names left on the Reserve's forms are the kind that stay "departed without notice" for years, and a few of the PCs will think of them whenever they see a steaming field.

**Partial.** The fobs open the hatch, the ship lifts, and the people who carried the fobs are somewhere under the Terrace. SolSec will call it a clean extraction of an asset and a regrettable loss. The PCs will know better.

**Failure.** Dawn clears the dust. The ship is a grey boulder in a wash, and a ranger truck is already coming up the track. Pruitt's forms will file them as visitors who wandered off. The Bureau is very good at forms.

**If Voss signalled the Imperium.** An Imperial cutter shadows the sky at dawn. Whether the PCs have their ship or not, the Imperium now knows about Motel Yermo, the Reserve, and a SolSec team that was working a world it was not supposed to be on.

**If Voss was traded with or turned.** SolSec gets a quiet line into Imperial counter-intelligence, and Wei gets a favour he did not ask for.

**If Voss got away.** He is on the highway with photographs of every face and every cover name. The next scenario starts with that.

## 9.3: IF THE TABLE FREES NO ONE

The fobs alone get two PCs aboard. The ship lifts. The pickup stays in the Reserve, in the cradle or the dark, and the final image of the scenario is the *Soft Landing* rising out of a dust storm with fewer people than it should have. The Mass does not release its dead.

## 9.4: REWARDS

Convention play does not usually carry characters forward, but if the table wants rewards, these fit Mongoose Traveller 2e.

**Credits.** SolSec pays a completion bonus per PC: Cr10,000 for a ship that lifts, plus Cr5,000 for each of the pickup freed and aboard. Add Cr5,000 to the PC who dealt with Voss before the ship lifted. These are Cash benefits, not Ship Shares.

**Allies, Contacts and Enemies.** A PC may take one of these, per the table's agreement:
- **Ally:** Captain Okafor-Brandt, if freed (§4.10).
- **Contacts:** Captain Pell (§4.12), Compliance Officer Pruitt (§4.11), Gus Teller (§4.11).
- **Enemies:** Ranger-Supervisor Quill, if exposed (§4.11); Imperial counter-intelligence, if Voss signalled; the Reserve's paperwork, which never forgets a name.

**Skill advancement.** At the end of the session, each PC may raise one skill they used in a meaningful scene by one level, to a maximum of 3, or gain a new skill at level 1. This stands in for training and experience between sessions.

**Table awards (optional).** A short vote at the end: best in-character moment, best use of the armoury, best use of the paper trail, most memorable escape.

**Equipment.** A PC who brings out a pickup member's TL11 gear may keep one item, at the referee's discretion. SolSec will ask for the rest back.

## 9.5: CAMPAIGN HOOKS

If the referee wants to carry the scenario forward:
- **The Rebellion.** Strephon was assassinated on day 132 of Y1116, and word has not reached the Rim (Chapter 2, §2.20). The next scenario opens as the news arrives and the Solomani fleets move.
- **Wei.** Whether he burned the cell, was compromised, or ran a test is never confirmed in the scenario (§1.10). A follow-up can answer it.
- **The Shutter Project and the satellite** (§2.25, §2.16). Who built the platform, and who now knows how to command it?
- **The Mass** (§2.21, §2.30). Is it one mind, and what is it waiting for? The Imperium's thousand years of Concession 9 (§2.18) may not be over.
- **Osei and the Bureaucracy** (§4.7). A compromised official with the keys to a smuggling corridor is a seed for a heist or a hunt.
- **The body bank** (§2.24). The missing 10% leave accounts open, and someone is spending the float.
