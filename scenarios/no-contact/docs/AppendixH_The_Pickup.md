## H.0 — WHAT THIS COVERS

The extraction the six guests are waiting for is real. A four-person SolSec
team came for them. They landed **a day early**, in a dry wash just outside the Reserve fence,
because their controller (Baldwin Wei) told them only to "check that
nothing has gone bad," and being good at their jobs they went to look.
They used the spare day to walk into the park on a day tour, as any
tourist would. They were intrigued by the one thing
within an hour's drive of the motel that appears on no chart of normal
worlds: the Halvorsen Basin Biological Reserve (Chapter 5, §5.3; Chapter 7).
They took a tour. There was an accident.

This appendix gives you the team, their ship, the key fobs that unlock it,
what they did that provoked the Mass, and what happened to them.

## H.2 — THE SHIP: *SOFT LANDING*

A **TL11 SolSec courier-sloop**, 100 dTon, Jump-1, with a cargo hold with
four cots, and a quiet, matte-grey hull that looks, from the air, like a
dry-wash boulder. Passive sensor-shadow field (TL11) hides it from Jorjor's
TL7 surveillance unless something draws attention to it.

- **Location:** a dry wash **11 km north of Area 13**, beyond the Reserve
  fence line. Reachable on foot across the flats (Route C, Chapter 8, §8.4).
- **Fuel:** enough for one lift and one jump.
- **Lock:** TL11 biometric-and-key. **Any two of the four key fobs**
  (H.3) open the hatch and wake the flight computer. A fob alone gets the
  door open and nothing else.
- **Backup:** Okafor-Brandt's spoken codeword *("Bluebird, no reply")*
  overrides the lock from inside the hatch comm panel. Anyone who hears it
  can use it.
- **Flight computer:** will lift on autopilot for a PC at the controls.
  Skilled pilot not required; Pilot (spacecraft) 1+ is easier.

## H.3 — THE KEY FOBS

Each team member wears a **bone-conduction key fob** (TL11, a slim black
disc on a chain, passes as a dog tag). Under the **2-of-4 rule**, even a
partial rescue pays off — two fobs, two living people, or two bodies with
their gear. The fobs stay with the person.

| Fob | Wearer | If captured | If trapped | If lost | If held |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Okafor-Brandt | On her, in the cradle | On her | On her | Locked in the Incident Room evidence cage |
| 2 | Mbeki | On him | On him | On him | Same cage |
| 3 | Hale | On her | On her | On her | Same cage |
| 4 | Delacroix | On him | On him | On him | Same cage |

**Referee note:** if the table recovers the fobs but not the team, they can
leave. The scenario's third Act ends on that choice — everyone out, or only
the codes out.

## H.6 — WHAT THEY DID: THE AGGRAVATION (d6)

Something the pickup did woke the Mass. Roll d6, or choose.

| d6 | What they did | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | **They fought back.** A Wader grabbed Hale on the Terrace. Rook shot it eight times. | Gunfire (+1 AP). Wader herd *hunting* in Area 12. Drifters converge. |
| 3–4 | **Psionics.** Hale ran a Telepathy: Life Detection pulse across the Sink to count the minds. The Mass counted her back. | Psionic use (+1 AP). Drifters turned to follow her. Hale was *noticed*. |
| 5–6 | **Explosives.** Mbeki set a shaped charge to open a void for a sample. The Terrace shelf collapsed into the tunnels. | Explosives (+2 AP). New collapse in Area 12. Egg-blooms in Area 13 *screamed*. |

**Whichever you pick, the Reserve goes to Red at 17:10, closes Areas 12–14
to camping at 18:00, and starts the cover-up.** Roll another result on top
if you want a compounded disaster.

## H.7 — WHAT HAPPENED TO THEM: RANDOM CHOICE (d6)

Roll once for the team (or choose). **C** = Captured (inside an Egg-bloom,
Area 13). **T** = Trapped (in a Terrace void, Area 12). **L** = Lost (wandering
the Drift Meadows or the Sink; Areas 11 and 14). **H** = Held (in the
Incident Room under the tower; Area 7). **X** = dead.

| d6 | Outcome | Okafor-Brandt | Mbeki | Hale | Delacroix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **All captured** | C | C | C | C |
| 2 | **Split** | C | T | C | T |
| 3 | **All trapped** | T | T | T | T |
| 4 | **Scattered** | T | L | C | L |
| 5 | **Officials got to some** | H | C | H | T |
| 6 | **The worst night** | C | X | H | T |

**Where the paper says they are** is a different matter. The Reserve's
offices disagree with one another and with the truth: the Gate says all four
"departed without notice," the Clinic may hold a "Hall," the Rescue log says
a response was stood down. The table works it out in Chapter 8, §8.9. Whatever
you roll here is the truth that the paper trail is hiding.

### Condition by state

- **Captured (C).** Sedated and upright in a cradle. Alive and unharmed.
  After **12 hours** a captive begins to lose 1 END per hour. The
  crew can be talked to only once freed. The Egg-bloom is sealed in
  the dust storm (Chapter 8, §8.3); open it with a warm lamp or cut the calyx.
- **Trapped (T).** Conscious in a warm void. **12 hours** of air, injured
  (Delacroix: leg wound, ~1D damage per hour untreated). The best
  rescue route is Teller's mole through the soil under Area 12.
- **Lost (L).** Wandering, dosed with spores, hallucinating (the team
  members are *trying* to reach the wash). Waders are following. **Must
  be found by about 03:00** or they walk into the Sink.
- **Held (H).** Awake, in the Incident Room cells beneath Area 7, with
  Quill's rangers watching. Quill intends to "relocate" them to the Sink at
  **Black** (AP 8+). They can be freed by force, by **Pruitt's form**,
  or by deal.
- **Dead (X).** Body at the site. Their fob and gear are recoverable.

**Fobs:** per H.3, the fob stays with the wearer. Two fobs open the ship.

## H.8 — WHAT THEY KNOW (IF FREED)

- **Why they were early.** Wei told them to "check that nothing has gone
  bad." They went to look at the one odd site in an hour's drive.
- **What they saw.** The Reserve's staff were calm *too fast*. They
  noted one tour group member who never came back from the Terrace.
- **What the Mass did.** Hale will describe the sensation of "being
  counted." She believes it is one huge mind.
- **About Wei.** Okafor-Brandt received the same order the PCs got
  ("no loose threads"). She doesn't know whether Wei burned the six, is
  compromised, or is running a test (Chapter 2, §2.3); she was told to
  extract **whoever is there**. Her loyalties are to Wei; she will
  want to know who the Imperial plant is.
- **What to do now.** Get to the ship, lift before dawn.

## H.9 — IF THE TABLE FREES NO ONE

The fobs alone get two PCs aboard. The ship lifts. The pickup stays in
the Reserve, in the cradle or the dark, and the final image of the
scenario is the *Soft Landing* rising out of a dust storm with fewer
people than it should have. The Mass does not release its dead.
