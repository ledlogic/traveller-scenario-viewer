## 7.1: ACT II: GRAVEYARD, "THE BIG RIG" (~90 MINUTES)

The extraction window comes and goes. Nothing. Panic and suspicion rise. The table now has a bearing: the pickup went into the Reserve. Act II is about **finding out what happened** and **getting there**.

**Pacing is free-form, not a hard clock.** There is no countdown mechanic; the referee paces this act by feel, using the complications below and §1.11's notes.

### The scenes

1. **Confirming the contact.** The table pieces together the pickup's day: the Gate ledger entry and permit #0614 (Chapter 2, §2.30 Area 1) can be learned from Del, a rumour, or by phone-to-the-Reserve if comms return. The pickup team is real and is missing.
2. **The paper trail.** Inside the Gate, the table learns the pickup is recorded as "departed without notice," and that no one office has the whole story. Gate, Property, Clinic, Rescue log, Incident file and Maintenance orders each hold a slice, spelled five different ways (§7.7). Captain Pell of the Rescue Service (§4.12) is the best ally: his whiteboard is the only honest record, and Policy RS-9 has stopped his people from going in.
3. **Del's rig.** Del Ashworth's long-haul tanker, *the Gravy Train* (Chapter 5, §5.3; Chapter 4, §4.2) is the only vehicle that can get through a Red-alert Gate (Chapter 7, §7.3, Route A). It is broken: a fouled fuel line, about **2D hours** to fix. The party can speed it up (Mechanic, Engineer (power), or Del's own Mechanic (repair)), or work other routes (the owner's pickup is a Route C crossing on foot; Form 14-R is Route B).
4. **Persuading Del.** He is possessive of the rig, tired, and has a load of 28 tonnes of offal and a stamped manifest. What changes his mind: a fair price, a true story, a threatened wife, or a ranger's badge.
5. **The Gate.** At Red (Chapter 2, §2.29) the Gate checks manifests. A stamped manifest gets a feed truck through to Area 8. Tolliver and Pruitt are here; this is the Act's best social scene.
6. **The drive.** Dust storm Stage 2: visibility 50 m, a Drive check, and the first Drifters.

### Complications

Use these to force decisions under pressure; full NPC stat blocks are in Chapter 4:

- Local police drawn by noise or a nosy neighbor's call, §4.5.
- A stranger's car idling too long outside, engine running, §4.6, built as two interchangeable versions (mundane or a Shutter Project Watcher).
- A phone call to the motel's front desk that hangs up the moment anyone but the owner answers, §7.2, **still deliberately unresolved**, even now.

**Act II ends** when the rig is past the Gate (or the table has found another way in) and they are at the Feed Line Terminal (Area 8) with the storm rising. If the table is out of time, skip to the Terminal.

## 7.2: THE HANG-UP CALL

No NPC or stat block needed; this complication is a phone call to the motel's front desk that goes dead the instant anyone other than the owner (Chapter 4, §4.1) answers. Deliberately ambiguous: could be a wrong number, could be the extraction contact attempting one last check-in and losing nerve or connection, could be Imperial surveillance confirming who's on-site. **Never resolve this one concretely.** Its value is entirely in the players not knowing which of the three it was, resist the urge to have it call back or explain itself later in the session.

## 7.3: THREE WAYS IN

The Reserve is 11 km from the motel by road, on a spur most maps leave off. At Red, the Gate (Area 1) checks everything. Three routes in: plan for different Acts, and expect the table to invent a fourth.

**Route A: The Big Rig.** Del Ashworth's rig, *the Gravy Train* (Chapter 5, §5.3; Chapter 4, §4.2), is a **contract Feed Line hauler** carrying **28 tonnes of slaughterhouse offal** on a **stamped manifest (Consignment 9-4471)**. Even at Red, a stamped manifest gets a feed truck through the Gate to Area 8: "you can't question a properly stamped form." Once at the Terminal, the rig can follow the service track to Area 9. **Cost:** Del needs the rig fixed (Act II), and he needs to be persuaded, or hired, bribed, or forced. He will not abandon his truck. A character who rides in the cab or sleeper is clean; one on the catwalk or tank hatch is **coated in feed**, which Drifters and Waders ignore (§2.31).

**Route B: The Form.** **Form 14-R (Request to Retrieve Property from a Closed Area)** is the legitimate way to ask for a lost bag. A PC with **Admin or Bureaucracy** can file one at the Gate: Average (8+) check, DM +2 if they have the pickup's permit number (Area 13 walk-in #0614) or a Jorjor Civil Service seal. Success: a ranger escort to Area 7, with a reduction of AP by 1. Failure: +1 AP and a suspicious Compliance Officer Pruitt. Pruitt can be an ally (§4.11).

**Route C: On Foot.** Over the fence-line, across the dry wash, and into the closed Areas from the north (the same line the pickup's ship sits behind). Eleven kilometres with the dust front arriving. Effects: no Gate-check, but Drifters and Waders, rangers on patrol, and the weather. The ship lies along this route (Chapter 5, §5.5).

## 7.5: WHAT THEY DID, THE AGGRAVATION (d6)

Something the pickup did woke the Mass. Roll d6, or choose.

| d6 | What they did | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | **They fought back.** A Wader grabbed Hale on the Terrace. Rook shot it eight times. | Gunfire (+1 AP). Wader herd *hunting* in Area 12. Drifters converge. |
| 3–4 | **Psionics.** Hale ran a Telepathy: Life Detection pulse across the Sink to count the minds. The Mass counted her back. | Psionic use (+1 AP). Drifters turned to follow her. Hale was *noticed*. |
| 5–6 | **Explosives.** Mbeki set a shaped charge to open a void for a sample. The Terrace shelf collapsed into the tunnels. | Explosives (+2 AP). New collapse in Area 12. Egg-blooms in Area 13 *screamed*. |

**Whichever you pick, the Reserve goes to Red at 17:10, closes Areas 12–14 to camping at 18:00, and starts the cover-up.** Roll another result on top if you want a compounded disaster.

## 7.6: WHAT HAPPENED TO THEM, RANDOM CHOICE (d6)

Roll once for the team (or choose). **C** = Captured (inside an Egg-bloom, Area 13). **T** = Trapped (in a Terrace void, Area 12). **L** = Lost (wandering the Drift Meadows or the Sink; Areas 11 and 14). **H** = Held (in the Incident Room under the tower; Area 7). **X** = dead.

| d6 | Outcome | Okafor-Brandt | Mbeki | Hale | Delacroix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **All captured** | C | C | C | C |
| 2 | **Split** | C | T | C | T |
| 3 | **All trapped** | T | T | T | T |
| 4 | **Scattered** | T | L | C | L |
| 5 | **Officials got to some** | H | C | H | T |
| 6 | **The worst night** | C | X | H | T |

**Where the paper says they are** is a different matter. The Reserve's offices disagree with one another and with the truth: the Gate says all four "departed without notice," the Clinic may hold a "Hall," the Rescue log says a response was stood down. The table works it out in Chapter 7, §7.7. Whatever you roll here is the truth that the paper trail is hiding.

### Condition by state

- **Captured (C).** Sedated and upright in a cradle. Alive and unharmed. After **12 hours** a captive begins to lose 1 END per hour. The crew can be talked to only once freed. The Egg-bloom is sealed in the dust storm (Chapter 2, §2.31); open it with a warm lamp or cut the calyx.
- **Trapped (T).** Conscious in a warm void. **12 hours** of air, injured (Delacroix: leg wound, ~1D damage per hour untreated). The best rescue route is Teller's mole through the soil under Area 12.
- **Lost (L).** Wandering, dosed with spores, hallucinating (the team members are *trying* to reach the wash). Waders are following. **Must be found by about 03:00** or they walk into the Sink.
- **Held (H).** Awake, in the Incident Room cells beneath Area 7, with Quill's rangers watching. Quill intends to "relocate" them to the Sink at **Black** (AP 8+). They can be freed by force, by **Pruitt's form**, or by deal.
- **Dead (X).** Body at the site. Their fob and gear are recoverable.

**Fobs:** per §5.6, the fob stays with the wearer. Two fobs open the ship.

## 7.7: THE PAPER TRAIL, WHERE IS THE PICKUP NOW?

**The park is not optional.** The *Soft Landing* sits in a dry wash just outside the Reserve fence, and its crew used their spare day to walk into the park on a tour. The only way off Jorjor is through the closed areas.

**The problem is finding them.** The Reserve handles hundreds of cases a year, and each office keeps its own paper, in its own order, with its own idea of how to spell a name. Nobody has the whole picture. Four people in trouble have become five or six partial records that disagree with one another.

### The cover names, as spelled by each office

| Office | How the four are written |
|---|---|
| Gate ledger | I. Brandt, T. Mbeki, S. Hale, R. Dela |
| Rescue log | Brant, Mbeke, Hall, "Rook D." |
| Clinic | "S. Hall" (if she was admitted) |
| Incident Room | "Four visitors" (no names) |
| Compliance | "Brand, I." and three more she has not matched |

A search by exact name in the wrong office returns nothing. **Admin (records) check, Average (8+)**, finds the right entry; DM +2 with the spelling variants above; DM -2 if the searcher has no name at all.

### The offices and what each one has

| Office (Area) | Keeper | What the record says | Fate it is hiding |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gate ledger** (1) | Tolliver | Signed in 13:00 as four. The out column was auto-stamped at 18:00 closing: **"OUT, Form 7-D (departed without notice)."** Permit #0614 for Area 13. | All. Nobody signed out. |
| **Property / Lost & Found** (1) | Tolliver | Four daypacks collected from the Terrace rim at 17:20. One is tagged "camping gear/tripod." It holds a disassembled TL10 rifle. | Packs only; the owners are elsewhere. |
| **Clinic annex** (1) | RRS volunteer | **"S. Hall"**, female, 50s, admitted 17:55 with "nausea, disorientation," then **transferred to Incident Room** at 18:40. | Held (H). |
| **Rescue log** (5) | Pell | Call 16:34. Response **stood down 17:12 per Policy RS-9.** Pell's whiteboard adds: "shouting from the tunnel 17:40" and "Orchard screamed 16:25." | Trapped (T) and Captured (C). |
| **Incident Room file R-1116-0245** (7) | Quill | "Minor subsidence. Four visitors **departed without notice.** Closed." A separate **custody sheet** under it lists anyone actually held. | Held (H), plus the cover story for all. |
| **Maintenance work orders** (9) | Teller | **WO-9-0245:** Mole-2 sent to Area 12 at 17:25 on Quill's quiet instruction, **recalled 17:50** with a failed belt. A second slip: **Form 22-M, unclaimed remains, Area 12** (only if someone is dead). | Trapped (T), or dead (X). |
| **Compliance notebook** (1, back desk) | Pruitt | Her forty Form 11-Cs and a list of names. Today's "Brand, I." is on it, matched to the Gate ledger by luck. | Confirms the pickup is the missing. |

### How the table works it out (the Trace)

Treat the investigation as a scene with **three kinds of check**. Each success narrows the answer; none is automatic.

| Check | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Admin (records) or Bureaucracy,** matching names across offices | Finds the entries above. Each success gives one record. |
| **Bureaucracy,** knowing which paper wins | In the Bureau's eyes the Incident file outranks the Gate ledger, which outranks the Rescue log. The true story is mostly in the *lowest-ranked* paper. A success tells the table to trust Pell's whiteboard over Quill's file. |
| **Persuade or Streetwise** with Pell, Teller or the clinic volunteer | Gets the human memory behind the paper: the shouting, the scream, the mole slip. |

| Successes | What the table learns |
|---|---|
| 0 | They are somewhere in the Reserve. The Gate says they left, which is a lie. |
| 1 | The **kind** of trouble: held by staff, trapped, lost, or taken. |
| 2 | **Which Area** each is probably in (7, 12, 11 or 14, 13). |
| 3 | Area plus **how long they have** (§7.6, "Condition by state") and who else is looking. |

**Contradictions are the clue.** The Gate says four left; the Property room still holds four packs; the clinic has a "Hall"; Pell heard shouting at 17:40. Four who "left" do not leave their packs, and do not shout from a tunnel.

**Paper also gets people out.** A **Form 31-T (Transfer of Custody)** moves a person from the Incident Room to a named carer. A **Form 14-R** (§7.3) fetches property. A **Policy RS-9 waiver** signed by Quill releases Pell's team. All three are weapons in a bureaucracy that does not question a stamped form.
