The ten character portrait prompts are in each character's entry in Chapter 3. The prompts below cover the other thirteen images. They share one look so the set matches: hard-science-fiction realism, red-toned light from two red dwarf suns, dust, worn industrial hardware.

## Setting (Chapter 2)

- **palin-orbit.webp:** *A small cold desert world seen from orbit, red-brown and iron-oxide regolith with pale polar caps, thin hazy atmosphere, two small red dwarf suns low in the black sky behind it, one slightly brighter than the other, a single tiny starport station in orbit, grounded hard-sci-fi space illustration, wide cinematic framing.*
- **halcourt.webp:** *A domed frontier town on a high red desert plateau, a cluster of low pressure domes and a utilitarian downport with a few landed traders, patchwork additions to a decades-old military depot, dust storm on the horizon, thin dark sky with two red suns, grounded hard-sci-fi establishing shot.*
- **meridian-field.webp:** *Cutaway view of a vast linear airport and bazaar built into a natural trench, shops and offices cantilevered off a hard rock cliff face, a long central corridor of gardens, biohabs and zoos running down the middle, winged atmospheric flyers parked on the surface apron above, warm lamplight below against red desert light above, bustling and prosperous, pre-collapse, detailed hard-sci-fi architectural illustration.*
