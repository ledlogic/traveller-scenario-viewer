# APPENDIX C, SOURCES AND CANON NOTES

This appendix marks every claim in the package as **[CANON]** (verifiably in one of your uploaded books, with the citation), **[TABLE]** (from the So Skire world list in Appendix D), **[WIKI]** (from the Traveller Wiki pages for the So Skire Subsector, Hell and Digriz, read while building this), **[SHEET]** (the Traveller Map world data sheet for Palin, transcribed in Appendix D), or **[HOMEBREW]** (invented for this campaign). Nothing is lifted from a published product except where flagged.

---

## CORRECTIONS TO EARLIER VERSIONS

- **Ashkeep never existed.** The first versions set the campaign on a world called Ashkeep (hex 1608), which I invented. It was not in any of your books or on your So Skire list. It has been replaced everywhere by **Palin (2234)**, a real entry in the So Skire list, and its invented UWP, Law Level, Tech Level and Government are gone.
- **So Skire is not an unrelated sector.** An earlier version of this appendix said the Old Expanses So Skire Subsector was geographically unrelated to your Alpha Crucis and Solomani Rim campaign region. That was wrong. The Dagir Subsector map in Solomani Front carries a label reading "To So Skire," and the table you supplied (with the four Confederation worlds on its southern edge) is that neighbouring subsector.
- **Hell was not shown to be a Rim War prison.** An earlier version said Hell (2237) was established as an Imperial prison during the Solomani Rim War. The wiki page I read this session says only that Hell hosts a prison world, exile camp or prison camp, and it does not mention the Rim War. The Px trade code in your table agrees with the prison function. The Rim War link was unsupported and has been removed.
- **Gravity was wrong.** An earlier Chapter 0 said Palin's gravity was roughly a third of a G. The data sheet gives 0.50 g, and the chapter now says so.

## CHAPTER 0, THE SETTING

- **Palin's UWP (B430489-A), trade codes, nobility, bases, extensions, PBG, number of worlds, stars, and every neighbour** [TABLE], all confirmed by the data sheet for Palin [SHEET], which also supplies the 0.50 g gravity, the Law Level 9 wording, and the plain-language readings of the Economic and Cultural extensions. The distances are my calculations from the hex coordinates, treating even-numbered columns as shifted half a hex down and sanity-checked against known adjacent pairs.
- **So Skire is subsector O of the Old Expanses sector with 37 worlds, capital Peart, 33 Imperial and 4 Confederation worlds, defended by the 308th Fleet, in the Domain of Sol** [WIKI]. The wiki's background and history sections for the subsector are empty, and it gives Palin no description at all. The Hell page records an Imperial Landed Nobility member as the Imperial representative, which agrees with reading the nobility code B as a Knight.
- **Hell is a prison or exile camp** [WIKI], consistent with the Px code [TABLE]. The page does not mention Palin or Di Meola.
- **Digriz is a corporate polity of the Confederation and a frontier world bordering Imperial space, with a class B starport and shipyard** [WIKI, TABLE].
- **Starport B means refined fuel and a spacecraft shipyard, Atmosphere 3 means a respirator, Government 8 is Civil Service Bureaucracy, and the last row of the Law Level table bans all weapons and all armour** [CANON, Core Rulebook Update 2022, World Creation chapter]. Size 4 matches the Core Rulebook's own Mars example. The extracted text of these tables lost some numerals, including the size diameters and gravities and the law level row numbers. The data sheet now supplies the size figures (6,400 km and 0.50 g) [SHEET]. "Law Level 9 is the last row" still rests on the row order, and the sheet's wording (weapons outside the home prohibited) is consistent with it.
- **The Law and Travellers procedure** (the authorities take an interest when a 2D roll comes in at or below the Law Level) is from the same chapter [CANON]. The dice digit was lost in the extracted text, and 2D is the standard roll. "Five rolls in six" at Law 9 is my arithmetic.
- **The nobility, base, zone, PBG and extension columns** are Traveller5 Second Survey notation. The Core Rulebook does not define them [general Traveller convention]. The data sheet confirms Palin's reading as a Knight with no travel zone [SHEET].
- **The Dagir cluster material** (26 worlds, the narrow Imperial strip, the Treaty of Gemstar, the Dagir Border Fleet, Cloudia's 1103 merchant fleet programme, SolSec's limits in the Cloudia belt) is **[CANON, Solomani Front, Dagir Subsector chapter]**.
- **The hex-grid join** (row 40 of So Skire meeting row 01 of Dagir, giving Palin about seven parsecs from Dagir) is my reading of the "To So Skire" label and the shared column range. The book does not state it.
- **Everything else is [HOMEBREW]:** the names Palin A and Palin B, the Cinders, the plateau ruling, Halcourt and its history, Sir Teodor Brandt, the Palin Civil Registry, the Hazard Site Charter, the Autonomous Systems Ordinance, the timeline entries outside the Solomani Front items, and the four patron options.

## APPENDIX A, ARBITER AND COUNTERSIGN

- Both mainframes as characters, the standing protocol and the ways to turn one against the other are **[HOMEBREW]**.
- **Intrusion and Security mechanics** are **[CANON, Core Rulebook Update 2022, p.110]**. Security software is rated 0 to 3, with each level naming a difficulty band for the Electronics (computers) check needed to bypass it: Security/0 Average, /1 Difficult, /2 Very Difficult, /3 (TL12) Formidable. ARBITER is Security/3 with a referee-discretion extra, and COUNTERSIGN is Security/2.
- The mainframes' other skills, INT and EDU equivalents and drone limits are **[HOMEBREW]** referee guidance, not a verified build.
