# APPENDIX C — SOURCES & CANON NOTES

You asked directly: **is Ashkeep in any of your uploaded books?** No. It isn't. Nothing in this campaign package is lifted from a published Traveller product except where flagged below — everything else is referee invention, written to sit consistently alongside what your books actually say. This appendix goes through every chapter and marks each claim as **[CANON]** (verifiably in one of your uploaded PDFs, with the citation) or **[HOMEBREW]** (invented for this campaign). Where I got a mechanic slightly wrong against the actual rule text, that's corrected here too.

---

## CHAPTER 1 — SITUATION & ASHKEEP

- **"So Skire" as the subsector bordering Dagir** — **[CANON, thin]**. Your *Solomani_Front_Aug23.md* upload labels it directly on the Dagir Subsector border map: *"To So Skire"* (appears once, no further detail). That's the entire extent of what your book says about it — no world list, no UWPs, no description. Everything past the name is homebrew.
- **Ashkeep** (world name, hex 1608, UWP D640468-8, population, government, history) — **[HOMEBREW]**, invented whole. I did not use the real-world Traveller wiki's "So Skire Subsector" (which is canonically part of the *Old Expanses* sector — a different, non-adjacent sector from your Alpha Crucis/Solomani Rim campaign region) because importing it would have put your campaign in two unconnected parts of the setting at once. I deliberately treated "So Skire" as your own book uses it: the unnamed Imperial subsector next door to Dagir.
- **Halcourt** (the settlement) — **[HOMEBREW]**, invented.
- **UWP format itself** (Starport/Size/Atmo/Hydro/Pop/Gov/Law-TL, trade codes De/Po/Ni) — **[CANON]** notation, Core Rulebook Update 2022 world-generation rules. The specific digits for Ashkeep are homebrew; the code *system* they're written in is the real one.
- **Government code 6 = "Captive Government"** — **[CANON]** term (standard Traveller government-type table); Ashkeep's specific backstory for having that government is homebrew.
- **"Solomani Rim War"** as a historical period — **[CANON]** real Traveller/Solomani Rim setting history, referenced generically; no specific event from that war is drawn from your books — I did not invent a "prison world" backstory for Ashkeep (see note below on why not).

**A note on something I looked into and deliberately didn't use:** there's a real, published Traveller world literally named "Hell" (Old Expanses 2237), established as an Imperial prison during the Solomani Rim War, in a subsector also called "So Skire" — I found this searching the web mid-campaign-build. I didn't fold it in, because that "So Skire" is Old Expanses sector canon, geographically unrelated to your Alpha Crucis/Solomani Rim campaign, and using it would have been a false "citation" — it would have looked like I'd pulled real setting data into your game when I'd actually pulled it from an unconnected part of the wider Traveller multiverse. Flagging it here in case the prison-world flavor (minimal civilian oversight, everyone watching the wrong thing) is something you want to lean into for Ashkeep anyway — it's a good idea, just not one I sourced from your books.

## APPENDIX A — ARBITER & COUNTERSIGN

- Both mainframes as characters, the standing protocol, "turning one against the other" — **[HOMEBREW]**.
- **Intrusion/Security mechanics** — **[CANON, corrected]**. I originally wrote flat "Security 6" / "Security 5" target numbers, which isn't how the actual rule works. Core Rulebook Update 2022, p.110, defines **Security software** as a Bandwidth 0–3 package where each level names a difficulty band for the Electronics (computers) check needed to bypass it: Security/0 = Average, /1 = Difficult, /2 = Very Difficult, /3 (TL12) = Formidable. I've corrected both mainframes' stat blocks to use this real system — ARBITER at Security/3 (Formidable, with referee-discretion extra hardening on top, since the book's scale tops out below what 25 years of self-improvement should represent) and COUNTERSIGN at Security/2 (Very Difficult), consistent with Chapter 2's "faster-moving, less rigorous" characterization of K-S engineering.
- Both mainframes' other listed skills, INT/EDU equivalents, and drone-commitment numbers — **[HOMEBREW]**, not drawn from any specific NPC-building formula in your books; treat them as referee guidance rather than a verified build.
