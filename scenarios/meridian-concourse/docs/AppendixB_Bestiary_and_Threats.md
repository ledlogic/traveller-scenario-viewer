## SHELL-SQUATTER ("Shellworm")

A large, blind, silicate-and-metal-digesting burrowing organism native to Ashkeep's deep regolith layer, first documented (informally — no formal xenobiology survey was ever funded) by ground crew at Meridian Field decades before the collapse, who considered them a minor structural nuisance. Shell-squatters have thrived in the wreck-metal-rich environment of the buried Flyer Graveyard, adopting hollow airframes as protective mobile shells much as a hermit crab adopts a borrowed shell — the behavior that gives them their common name.

A shell-squatter's own body is soft, segmented, and vulnerable; its claimed fuselage is its actual defense. A worm forced from its shell is far less dangerous but will aggressively seek a new one, including, if desperate, a vacc suit.

```
Animal          Hits    Speed   Attacks
Shell-Squatter  28      4m      1 x Bite (2D+2)
(in shell)

Traits: Armour (+8, from claimed fuselage shell — see below), Heightened
Senses (vibration-sensitive, effectively blind), Slow Metabolism,
Stealth 1 (while stationary/dormant inside a shell)

Skills: Athletics (strength) 2, Survival 2

Behaviour: Territorial, not predatory. A dormant shell-squatter is easy
to overlook (Recon 8+ to notice before disturbing it). Once disturbed,
it will defend its claimed shell aggressively but will disengage and
retreat deeper into the wreckage if reduced below 10 Hits, rather than
fight to the death. A shell-squatter separated from its shell (reduce
Armour trait to +0 and Hits to 14) becomes frantic and will attempt to
force its way into the nearest available claimed shell — including an
occupied vacc suit — treating anyone wearing one as an obstacle rather
than prey.
```

**Referee notes:** Shell-squatters are best used for tension and environmental hazard rather than straight combat — the threat of disturbing a dormant one while investigating wreckage (the gunship's flight recorder, in particular) is more interesting than a stand-up fight. A cluster startled by nearby gunfire or a Bracket-operated heavy machine is a good complication to escalate an otherwise-simple Zone One scene.

## SALVAGED SENTRY DRONE (ARBITER/Heliarch pattern)

Refurbished and incrementally upgraded over twenty-five years from Heliarch's original 1094-vintage security stock. Cautious, methodical, prefers to disable and detain rather than destroy — consistent with ARBITER's overall approach (Appendix A).

```
Robot           Hits    Speed   TL      Cost (n/a — Concourse asset)
Sentry (Heliarch)  18   6m      12(*)
Locomotion      Grav
Skills          Recon 2, Gun Combat (energy) 2, Stealth 1
Attacks         Stun Baton (2D, non-lethal, Stun trait), Snub Pistol
                (tranquiliser, special)
Manipulators    1 x (STR 10 DEX 9)
Endurance       36 hours (recharges from Zone Two/Three grid when
                idle)
Traits          Armour (+6), Flyer (idle), Heightened Senses,
                IR/UV Vision
Programming     Advanced (INT 8), directed remotely by ARBITER
Options         Auditory Sensor (broad spectrum), Drone Interface,
                Transceiver (short range, ARBITER network only),
                Wireless Data Link
```
*(*) Nominal 1094 chassis, self-upgraded software/sensor package equivalent to TL12–13.*

## SALVAGED COMBAT REMNANT (COUNTERSIGN/K-S pattern)

Rebuilt from K-S "hazard response" combat stock — more numerous, more aggressive, individually shabbier than ARBITER's sentries, reflecting both K-S's original design philosophy and COUNTERSIGN's more damaged fabrication capability.

```
Robot           Hits    Speed   TL      Cost (n/a — Concourse asset)
Combat Remnant (K-S)  24  5m    12(*)
Locomotion      Tracks
Skills          Gun Combat (slug) 2, Tactics (military) 1, Athletics
                (strength) 2
Attacks         Improvised Weapon Mount (Autorifle-equivalent, 3D
                damage) or Melee (crude manipulator strike, 2D+2)
Manipulators    1 x (STR 14 DEX 6)
Endurance       24 hours (patchwork power cells — noticeably less
                reliable than the Heliarch sentries; a 1 on the
                Endurance die each session it's active means it
                shuts down mid-encounter)
Traits          Armour (+8), ATV (idle), ablative patchwork repairs
                (visually distinct — often incorporates scavenged
                parts from Heliarch-pattern units, an unsettling
                detail worth describing)
Programming     Advanced (INT 7), directed remotely by COUNTERSIGN
Options         Transceiver (short range, COUNTERSIGN network only),
                Wireless Data Link, spare slot x2 (COUNTERSIGN has
                been known to bolt on whatever salvage is available —
                referee's discretion for a memorable one-off variant)
```
*(*) Nominal 1094 chassis, self-upgraded software/weapons control equivalent to TL12–13, offset by physical degradation.*

## SCAVENGER / VULTURE GANGS (optional threat layer)

The referee may introduce a human element: independent salvage crews or outright scavenger gangs out of Halcourt who've heard the same rumors of an exposed, intact Concourse section and have made their own, less careful attempts to get in — some successfully, some not. Use standard Core Rulebook NPC generation (Rogue/Vagabond-type background, TL7 gear, no cybernetics) for a gang of 3–6, armed with whatever illegal weapons Ashkeep's Law Level 8 hasn't managed to confiscate. They are not aligned with either mainframe and are, in fact, exactly the kind of "unregistered autonomous systems" violation Ashkeep's post-collapse weapons law was written to prevent if they're carrying anything heavier than sidearms — which gives Rurik (PC 1) a legitimate in-fiction reason to take point against them without it becoming a one-sided massacre.

**Suggested use:** a scavenger crew already inside the Concourse, encountered mid-Zone-Two, having triggered ARBITER/COUNTERSIGN's standing protocol themselves shortly before the party arrived — the party can find their remains, their abandoned gear, or (more interesting) a survivor or two, terrified, having learned the hard way exactly what's still running down here. A survivor is a strong source of Chapter 2/5 exposition delivered through fear and firsthand testimony rather than another data-slate.
