# CHAPTER 3, CHARACTERS

Ten pregenerated characters, built for a full-table run of *The Meridian Concourse*. The group took the **Mercenary skills package** (section 3.1), and eight of the ten characters draw one of its skills. Characters 8–10 (Engineer, Pilot, Astrogator) are the expedition's junior/backup ship specialists, brought along as redundancy for the senior professional crew.

The **Miner** (#2) and **Safety Officer** (#3) are deliberately split into two roles rather than one, because underground they have two different jobs. The Miner's job is the structure: is the rock and the built environment around you going to hold. The Safety Officer's job is the air and everything in it: is what you're breathing actually safe, and is it going to stay that way.

## 3.1: MERCENARY SKILLS PACKAGE

As the Core Rulebook describes [CR p.50], the group chose one skills package and the Travellers took turns selecting an item from it until all were taken. This group took the **Mercenary skills package**, which suits a combat-heavy delve into a hostile site. Its skills are Electronics 1, Medic 1, Leadership 1, Heavy Weapons 1, Gun Combat 1 (twice), Stealth 1 and Recon 1.

There are ten Travellers and eight skills, so two take none. Remi and Solt sit out, because both are already strong in the skills they would have taken.
