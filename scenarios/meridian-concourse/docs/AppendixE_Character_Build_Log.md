# APPENDIX E, CHARACTER BUILD LOG

This appendix rebuilds each pregen the way a Mongoose Traveller 2e Traveller is actually generated, from background to current age, so every skill and characteristic on the Chapter 3 sheet can be traced to a term. Rurik (Character 1) is first. The others follow one at a time, using the same layout.

Source tags: **[CR]** is the Core Rulebook Update 2022 (page numbers are the printed ones), **[SF]** is Solomani Front, **[TABLE]** is the So Skire list, **[HOMEBREW]** is invented for this campaign, **[PROPOSED]** is a build decision waiting for the table to approve it.

---

## THE PROCESS IN ONE PAGE

1. **Roll characteristics** [CR p.9]. 2D each, in order STR, DEX, END, INT, EDU, SOC, or assign the six scores in that order. The DM comes from the score: 0 is -3, 1 to 2 is -2, 3 to 5 is -1, 6 to 8 is +0, 9 to 11 is +1, 12 to 14 is +2, 15 or more is +3. Human maximum is 15 for an unaugmented Traveller.
2. **Homeworld and background skills** [CR p.9, p.10]. Pick a number of skills at level 0 equal to 3 plus the EDU DM, from: Admin, Animals, Art, Athletics, Carouse, Drive, Electronics, Flyer, Language, Mechanic, Medic, Profession, Science, Seafarer, Streetwise, Survival, Vacc Suit. The homeworld should suggest the picks. The Traveller is 18 years old at this point.
3. **Qualify for a career** [CR p.18]. Marine is END 6+, with DM-1 for each previous career and DM-2 if aged 30 or more [CR p.32]. A failed roll means the Draft or the Drifter career.
4. **Basic training** [CR p.18]. In the first career, every skill on the Service Skills table at level 0. For Marines that is Athletics, Vacc Suit, Tactics, Heavy Weapons, Gun Combat and Stealth.
5. **Each four-year term** [CR p.18, p.19]. Choose one skill table and roll 1D for a skill or characteristic. Then roll Survival (a natural 2 is always a failure, and a failure means a Mishap and leaving the career, and the Benefit roll for that term is lost). If still serving, roll once on the Events table. Then, if eligible, roll for Commission (SOC 8+ for Marines, only once the first term has passed unless SOC is 9 or more, DM-1 each term after the first), and roll for Advancement. A promotion grants an extra skill roll and any rank bonus. If the Advancement roll is equal to or less than the number of terms served, the Traveller cannot continue in the career after this term. A natural 12 means they must continue.
6. **Ageing** [CR p.48, p.49]. Starts at the end of the fourth term, at age 34. At the end of each term from then on, roll 2D, subtract the total number of terms, and read the Ageing table.
7. **Mustering out** [CR p.46, p.47]. One Benefit roll per full term served, plus bonus rolls for the highest rank reached (the printed table has lost its numerals in extraction, so the exact values need checking against the book: ranks 1 to 2 are believed to add one roll, ranks 3 to 4 two, ranks 5 to 6 three plus DM+1 to benefit rolls). Cash rolls are limited to three across all careers, and the Gambler skill adds DM+1 to Cash rolls.
8. **Skill limits** [CR p.18]. No skill above level 4 during creation, and total skill levels cannot exceed three times INT plus EDU.

Marine Events table, 2D [CR p.33]: 2 Disaster (roll on Mishap, but not ejected), 3 trapped behind enemy lines (Survival 1, Stealth 1, Deception 1 or Streetwise 1), 4 space station security (Vacc Suit or Athletics (dexterity) +1), 5 specialist training (EDU 8+ for any skill at 1), 6 assault on a fortress (Melee or Gun Combat 8+ for Tactics (military), failure is injury and -1 to a physical characteristic), 7 Life Event, 8 planetary assault (Recon 1, Gun Combat 1, Leadership 1 or Electronics (comms) 1), 9 commander's error (see book, may create an Enemy), 10 black ops (DM+2 to Advancement), 11 commanding officer's interest (Tactics 1 or DM+4 to Advancement), 12 heroism (automatic promotion or commission).

Marine Mishaps, 1D [CR p.33]: 1 severely injured, 2 captured and discharged early with an Enemy (jailer), STR and DEX reduced by one level, 3 stranded behind lines (Stealth or Survival +1, ejected), 4 black ops against your conscience (ejected if refused, otherwise gain an Enemy), 5 quarrel with an officer or fellow marine (Rival, driven out), 6 injured, roll on the Injury table.

Ageing table, 2D minus total terms [CR p.49]: 1 or more is no effect, 0 reduces one physical characteristic by 1, -1 reduces two physical by 1, -2 reduces three physical by 1, -3 reduces one physical by 2 and two physical by 1, -4 reduces two physical by 2 and one physical by 1, -5 reduces three physical by 2, -6 reduces three physical by 2 and one mental by 1.

## CALENDAR CONVENTION [HOMEBREW]

The campaign year is Imperial Year 1119. A term is four years. A Traveller who enters at age 18 and serves **n** terms is aged 18 + 4n at the end of term n. To place any pregen in time, count back from the current age at the campaign year, and the career years follow.

A term-by-term tracker uses these columns:

| Term | Years | Age start | Age end | Career and assignment | Rank | Qualification or survival | Skill roll or rolls | Event | Commission and advancement | Characteristic changes | Ageing roll | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
