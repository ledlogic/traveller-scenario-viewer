# TRAVELLER: NOVEM PORTUS SPATIALES
## Chapter 4 — Act Two: The Rival

*Schaut → Otha → Vaderwall → Otha. Someone is tidying up behind them.*

---

## 4.1 — SCENE 1: SCHAUT, THE SECOND TIME

A brief transit, and it is different.

- The apron lights are off. Fuel is still sold, by a man who does not look up.
- The crawler track is busy. Whatever the Cut is, they are working it double shifts.
- The radio noise from the flats is louder and has a rhythm now. **Corr, Average (8+) Electronics (computers) check (INT):** the rhythm matches the header of Port I.
- **Irena Sabel** finds one of them alone and says, quietly: "Don't leave it switched on here. It can hear itself." She will not explain. She seems surprised to have said it.

---

## 4.2 — SCENE 2: OTHA

**Otha** (E434522-9) is Aguilar's homeworld: a few hundred thousand people in pressure-roofed towns, a frontier field and no questions.

Before the job, Marguerite left a **backup image of the Pankow build** with an old friend here, the ship broker **Oda Renke**, as insurance.

**What they find**

- Renke's office is sealed by the town proctors. She was killed three days ago.
- Her body was found seated at her desk with her hands flat on the table and her head turned to the left, exactly as the figure sits in the line drawing that Port IV displays when run.
- Her safe is open. The backup image is gone.
- A message cylinder is waiting at the field office, routed from Drang: **Fenn is dead, and his machine has been gutted.**

**Checks**

- **Average (8+) Investigate check (1D hours, INT):** Renke let her killer in. Two cups on the desk. She knew him, or he was expected.
- **Difficult (10+) Streetwise check (INT), DM+2 for Aguilar:** a yacht made planetfall the same day and left within hours. Two men. One was "the kind you'd lend money to".
- **Average (8+) Medic check (EDU):** a single contact stunner burn at the neck, then asphyxiation. Careful work.

**If they confront Balkan later about Renke:** he says "How dreadful," and returns to the subject of the Ports. He is not bothered at all. Let the players notice.

---

## 4.3 — SCENE 3: DAME KESH

**Vaderwall** (C652453-A) is a feudal technocracy: rank follows technical mastery, and old systems are heirlooms. Corr was born here and knows the etiquette.

**Dame Ottoline Kesh**, seventy-four, uses a powered chair, has one working hand and no patience. She keeps the **Vaderwall build** in a vault under her manor at Tessel Hold, and has spent thirty years writing a monograph about Torvald.

**She receives them, until she learns who sent them.**

- "Balkan. That *annotator*. He has never made anything in his life." The interview ends. Her secretary shows them out.
- **Formidable (14+) Persuade check (SOC)** to change her mind while they still carry his charter. **Average (8+)** if they openly renounce him and tell her about Fenn and Renke.

**What she knows, if won over**

- Torvald's last posting was Schaut. Kesh was her doctoral supervisor.
- The tower in the boot image is real. It is the first-landing survey tower on Schaut.
- Torvald wrote to her once in 1094: *"I have kept the ninth to myself. If the other eight are ever read aloud without it, I am sorry for whoever is listening."*

**Getting to the build**

| Approach | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Invited | Win Kesh over. She supervises; all checks at DM+1. |
| The fasting hour | Kesh dines alone for one hour each evening and the secretary leaves. **Average (8+) Stealth check (DEX)** for the group, then **Difficult (10+) Electronics (computers) check (1D minutes, INT)** on the vault's TL10 lock. |
| Force | Two house retainers (Appendix C). Law level 3. The proctors arrive in 2D minutes. |

**The comparison:** **Difficult (10+) Electronics (computers) check (1D × 10 minutes, INT).** The odd Ports here are **III, VI and VIII.** The theory is confirmed. Eight of nine, or so it seems.

---

## 4.4 — SCENE 4: FIRE

As the comparison finishes:

- Whoever is nearest the door is hit from behind with a stunner (**Cade: Gun Combat (energy) 2, DEX +1; 2D+3, Stun**). If the group is too alert for this, the vault's fire suppression is triggered instead and the door seals.
- When they come round or get out, the manor is burning. Kesh is dead in her chair, strangled. Her monograph is in the fire. Ports III, VI and VIII are gone from the rack.
- **Escaping the fire:** **Average (8+) Athletics (dexterity) check**, or take 2D damage and try again. Anyone carrying another person takes DM–2.

**Saving something:** a traveller who goes back for Kesh's papers (**Difficult (10+) Athletics check (END)**, 1D damage either way) recovers one charred page: Torvald's letter, quoted above, and a hand-drawn plan of the Schaut tower with a room marked **"IX"**.

**At the port:** a note in a good hand is waiting with the field warden:

> *My dear friends — the charter is concluded, with my thanks. The completion fee is lodged at Pankow. You have been exactly as good as I hoped. Mr. Cade will call for my property. Do not follow. — A.B.*

Cade does call, an hour later, and finds what the travellers have just found: the *Marginalia* has been entered and **the sealed core holding the Pankow build is already gone.** Someone else took it. Cade is as surprised as they are, and for one moment both sides know it.

- **Average (8+) Investigate check (INT):** the lock was opened with a bought port master key, not forced. A hired local, paid well.
- **Cade** will not start a fight on a law level 3 world with the manor still burning. He leaves to tell Balkan.

**Why the core matters:** Balkan already holds an image of his own build, taken from Renke's safe. An image is not enough. The native Ports only behave on the Schaut-grade hardware they have lived in for twenty-five years, which is why he took boards, not copies, on Drang and Vaderwall. He needs the physical core.

The travellers have their notes, their filled-in grid and a charred page.

---

## 4.5 — SCENE 5: THE RECENSION

Back on **Otha**, the trail has one more turn.

**Liana Telver**, Andros Telver's widow, did not sell willingly and wants the build back. She is the patroness of the **Silver Recension**, a society of rich Veneet dilettantes who believe the Torvald Builds are a recovered scripture. It was her hired hand who took the core from the *Marginalia* on Vaderwall, and her yacht that carried it here.

She has chartered a pressure-hall on the edge of town and is staging a recital: thirty robed members, a great deal of incense, and the Pankow build running on a lectern.

**Finding it:** **Average (8+) Streetwise check (INT)**: thirty wealthy off-worlders are hard to hide on Otha.

**What happens, unless the travellers change it**

1. The Recension chants Port headers. Nothing occurs. They have one build and no idea what it is.
2. Balkan walks in with Cade, in ordinary clothes, and tells them so, wittily and at length.
3. Liana goes for him. Cade kills her.
4. Balkan lifts the core from the lectern and leaves. The Recension scatters.

**Player options**

- **Take the core first:** an opposed **Stealth** or **Deception** check against the Recension's ushers (skill 0), before Balkan arrives. If they succeed, Balkan reaches Schaut with six native Ports and only an image of the other three. He will run it anyway, and it will go worse (Appendix D, §D.5).
- **Confront Balkan here:** he will talk. He is still friendly. He explains that the builds are "a salutation" and that he intends to be the one who delivers it. Cade is armed with an autopistol and a stunner. Balkan will not fight; he will leave.
- **Follow him:** the *Annotated* lifts for Schaut. Jump-2 or Jump-1, from Otha it is the same single parsec. He will be there first only by the hours it takes them to refuel.

**This scene is a red herring by design.** The Recension knows nothing. If time is short, cut it.

---

## 4.6 — END OF ACT TWO

The travellers know:

- The nine Ports are a handshake, spread three to a build.
- The ninth was "kept back".
- The tower is on Schaut.
- Balkan is going to read the other eight aloud.
