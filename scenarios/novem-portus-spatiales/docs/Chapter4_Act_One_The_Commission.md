# TRAVELLER: NOVEM PORTUS SPATIALES
## Chapter 4 — Act One: The Commission

*Pankow Highport, then the moon Kell. The job is a pleasure. Let it be one.*

> **Travel in this act.** Every move is inside the Pankow system and takes hours, not weeks. Narrate it as a scene cut, with no piloting or astrogation rolls, unless the players want to do something unusual on the way.

---

## 4.1 — SCENE 1: DINNER ON PANKOW HIGHPORT

**Where:** The *Lithic Club*, the only good restaurant on Pankow Highport, with a view of a brown, dry world and the company's orbital yards.

Baltic hosts. He is fifty-eight, silver-haired, immaculately and slightly eccentrically dressed, and he is delighted by every one of them. He has read Corr's appraisal of the Henneker navigation tables. He knows the *Marginalia*'s tonnage. He orders for the table and is right.

**What he tells them**

- There are thousands of Torvald Builds, and he dismisses nearly all of them as "splash screens with delusions". He means the original fork, **the Big Splash**: a nine-Port control program from about 1090. Three copies are known, and by an odd chance all three are now in this system.
- His copy came from **Andros Telver**, a collector on Veneet, who sold it to him last year and took his own life a week later. "A melancholy man. I think he sold it to be rid of it."
- The second is held by **Tobiah Fenn**, caretaker of a closed *Ostrander Lithic* research station on **Kell**, Pankow's moon.
- The third is held by **Dame Ottoline Kesh**, Vaderwall's consul on Pankow, who keeps it in the consulate vault at the downport and dislikes him.
- He wants to know which build is genuine. He suspects his own is not.

**What he hands over:** a sealed, armoured portable core holding the **Baltic build**, a letter of credit, and the signed charter (handout, Appendix F, §F.2).

**What he does not say:** that his yacht, the *Annotated*, is docked two rings away, and that he means to be wherever they are.

**Checks**

- **Routine (6+) Persuade or Broker check (INT):** he raises the completion fee to Cr25,000 a head. He does not haggle; he enjoys being asked.
- **Average (8+) Investigate check (INT), by Corr:** nobody has ever published the builds' purpose. Torvald worked for *Ostrander Lithic*. Her last posting is redacted.
- **Difficult (10+) Streetwise check (INT):** Telver did not seem melancholy to anyone who knew him. He died in his yacht's airlock, and the port called it suicide. See §3.10.

> **Play Baltic warm.** The scenario's ending depends on the players liking him now.

---

## 4.2 — SCENE 2: A FIRST LOOK AT THE BALTIC BUILD

Aboard the *Marginalia* at her berth, Corr and anyone helping can examine the core.

- **Corr, first look:** he has appraised a dozen Torvald forks and every one was junk. **Average (8+) Electronics (computers) check (1D hours, EDU), DM+2 for Corr:** this one isn't. Its lineage runs straight back to 1094. It is the Big Splash.
- **Average (8+) Electronics (computers) check (1D hours, EDU):** nine Ports, numbered I to IX, each with a ref hash ID in its header. The registry attributes all nine to Torvald, under three identities. Six Ports carry one of two human identities, one recorded as male and one as female. Three (I, IV and IX) carry an identity with no gender recorded at all.
- **Difficult (10+) Electronics (computers) check (1D hours, INT):** those three neuter Ports are *different* underneath. Their ref hash validates, but the code inside is not written the way a person writes. No comments, no structure, no wasted instructions.
- **Halvard, Routine (6+) Mechanic check:** the core's processors are Schaut-grade substrate, the expensive kind. "Somebody paid a lot to run an old program."

Hand out the blank grid from Appendix A and let the players mark the Baltic column. If anyone tries to debug a single Port, or asks why nobody has, see Appendix A, §A.6 and §A.7.

**Boot splash.** Like nearly every program written in the 1090s, the build opens with a splash screen, and each Port draws its own when run. The first is a line drawing of a slender tower on a flat plain under two small moons. In 1116 it reads as ordinary period design. Nobody recognises the place yet.

## 4.3 — SCENE 3: THE ORE HAULER

This scene replaces a visit to Schaut. It should take ten minutes of table time and leave a mark.

While the crew is aboard, the *Ostrander Lithic* ore hauler ***Cutwater*** docks at the next berth, in from Schaut with a hold of white crystal block for the orbital yards.

**What is odd**

- The sealed core in Corr's cabin gets warm the moment the *Cutwater* is made fast. It cools again when she undocks.
- **Average (8+) Electronics (sensors) check (INT):** faint, regular radio noise is coming from the hauler's hold. It is structured, and it is not on any band a ship uses.
- A young shuttle technician off the *Cutwater*, **Irena Sabel**, wanders over to the *Marginalia*'s airlock and looks at the ship for a long time. If approached she is friendly and vague and asks what they are carrying. See Chapter 3.
- **Petrosyan-Wu** recognises the hauler's livery: Schaut, the posting they were refused in 1109.

**Average (8+) Broker or Streetwise check (INT), at the dock office:** the *Cutwater* runs to Schaut and back every three weeks, and nobody on the highport has ever been asked to work there.

## 4.4 — SCENE 4: THE STATION ON KELL

**Kell** is Pankow's single moon: airless grey rock a few hours out at the *Marginalia*'s Thrust 1. Vacc suits outside; the station itself is pressurised and has a docking tube.

**Research Outstation 3**, run by *Ostrander Lithic*, closed in 1095. Fenn, once Torvald's junior technician, stayed on as caretaker and never left. The station is a half-buried run of laboratory units, most of them dark. He keeps one room powered: the machine room holding the **Kell build**.

**Fenn** is sixty-one, courteous, frightened and lonely. He will not sell. He will let them look, because nobody has asked him about the work in twenty years and he badly wants to talk.

**What Fenn will say, a little at a time**

- Torvald did not write the builds. "She took dictation."
- She made three copies deliberately, "so that it would take three people who trusted each other. She did not think there would ever be three."
- She did not die in a laboratory fire. He does not know how she died.
- The machine room has been noisier this past week. Since about the day the *Marginalia* took the charter.

**Checks**

- **Average (8+) Persuade or Diplomat check (SOC):** Fenn allows a full comparison rather than a supervised look.
- **Difficult (10+) Electronics (computers) check (1D hours, INT):** in the Kell build the neuter ref hash is on Ports **II, V and VII**: different ones from the Baltic build. Every other Port carries one of Torvald's two human identities.
- **Average (8+) Science (cybernetics) or Science (xenology) check (EDU):** the odd Ports are not programs so much as a recorded conversation. One side of one.

**The theory arrives.** Two builds, six odd Ports, no overlap. If the third build holds III, VI and VIII, then no copy is "genuine". The real thing is spread across all three. Let the players reach this themselves.

---

## 4.5 — SCENE 5: THE PATRON AT THE PAD

When they come back through the docking tube, the *Annotated* is on Kell's second pad. Baltic is waiting inside the station's airlock lobby with a flask of tea, reading.

He is charming about it: he could not bear to wait. He asks what they found. **This is the hinge of the scenario.**

- If they tell him the three-per-build theory, he goes very still, then laughs and doubles the completion fee. He writes the addendum into the charter there and then, in his own hand (handout F.2, addendum): Cr40,000 a head, or Cr50,000 if it was already raised at dinner. The signing fee does not change. He asks whether Fenn would sell. If told Fenn would sooner die, he says, "Really?"
- If they hold it back, he knows (**Deception opposed by his Investigate 2, INT +2**). He is just as pleasant.

Either way he says he will "pay his respects" to Fenn and follow on.

**Aguilar, or anyone else watching, Average (8+) Recon check (INT):** a second man came off the yacht and has not been introduced. This is **Cade** (Chapter 3).

---

## 4.6 — END OF ACT ONE

The *Marginalia* lifts for the highport. Behind them, within hours, Tobiah Fenn is found face down in the station's coolant sump. Ports II, V and VII have been cut out of the Kell machine: the boards themselves, pulled from the rack. The news reaches the highport the next morning, by the company's own traffic channel.
