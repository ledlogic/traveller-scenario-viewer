## D.3 — THE LIFEFORM

From *Signal GK*: natural chips form where volcanic gases and bacteria etch crystalline silicon (SGK:35–36); a powered chip signals to find companions and interferes with nearby equipment; its code is a binary language a computer can emulate; intelligent chips are strong in mathematics and weak at reading people (SGK:42–44).

Invented here: a chip organism on Schaut, its planetary scale, its quarrying, its bitterness, and its ability to drive hardware made from itself.

## D.4 — THE WORLDS

- **UWPs and hex numbers** come from the Voyager subsector map for Magyar, 1116. The map also shows gas giants and the borders the route crosses.
- ***Solomani Front*** does not describe these worlds; it covers the Solomani Rim sector. It supplies context only: the Imperium holds a small territory in Magyar reinforced from the Rim (SF23:21); Ultima subsector is the gateway into it (SF23:125); Magyar was long a source of reavers and pirates (SF23:121) and of Aslan migration (SF23:148).
- **Allegiance (invented for this scenario):** Pankow is Imperial and has stayed so. Schaut was Imperial until 1112, was drawn economically toward the Solomani Confederation by corporate pressure, and then changed its governance to match.
- **Invented:** *Ostrander Lithic*, every named person, the moon Kell, Pankow Highport and Downport detail, Glasshouse Station, Outstation 3, Tessel Hold and Tessel House, the *Lithic Club*, the *Cutwater*, and all local detail.
- **"Hellworld"** is a survey description, not a CR22 trade code.

## D.5 — SCENARIO RULINGS

- **Schaut's air.** Very thin and tainted: a breather mask (combined respirator and filter) is required. The unmasked check in Chapter 6 (Average END each minute or 1D damage) is a ruling for this scenario, not a rulebook value.
- **Pregens** are designed, not rolled. Term counts and ages are consistent (age 18 plus four years per term) and skill totals are modest for the terms served. A full term-by-term rebuild is the obvious next pass.
- **Comms.** Acts One and Two stay inside the Pankow system, where ordinary comms work, so Balkan can call and turn up within hours. Only the final leg crosses a parsec, and both ships spend the same week in jump.
