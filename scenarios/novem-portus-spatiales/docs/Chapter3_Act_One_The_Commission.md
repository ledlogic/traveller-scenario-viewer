# TRAVELLER: NOVEM PORTUS SPATIALES
## Chapter 3 — Act One: The Commission

*Pankow → Schaut → Drang. The job is a pleasure. Let it be one.*

---

## 3.1 — SCENE 1: DINNER ON PANKOW

**Where:** The Lithic Club, the only good restaurant at Pankow Highport, with a view of a brown, dry world and the company's orbital yards.

Balkan hosts. He is fifty-eight, silver-haired, immaculately and slightly eccentrically dressed, and he is delighted by every one of them. He has read Corr's appraisal of the Henneker navigation tables. He knows the *Marginalia*'s tonnage. He orders for the table and is right.

**What he tells them**

- The Torvald Builds are a nine-Port control program from about 1090. Three copies survive.
- His copy came from **Andros Telver**, a collector on Veneet, who sold it to him last year and took his own life a week later. "A melancholy man. I think he sold it to be rid of it."
- The second is held by **Tobiah Fenn**, caretaker of a closed research station on Drang.
- The third is held by **Dame Ottoline Kesh**, a technarch of Vaderwall, who dislikes him.
- He wants to know which build is genuine. He suspects his own is not.

**What he hands over:** a sealed, armoured portable core holding the **Pankow build**, and a letter of credit.

**What he does not say:** that he will be following in his yacht, the *Annotated*, which makes Jump-2.

**Checks**

- **Routine (6+) Persuade or Broker check (INT):** he raises the completion fee to Cr25,000 a head. He does not haggle; he enjoys being asked.
- **Average (8+) Investigate check (INT), by Corr:** nobody has ever published the builds' purpose. Torvald worked for Ostrander Lithic. Her last posting is redacted.
- **Difficult (10+) Streetwise check (INT):** Telver did not seem melancholy to anyone who knew him.

> **Play Balkan warm.** The scenario's ending depends on the players liking him now.

## 3.2 — SCENE 2: A FIRST LOOK AT THE PANKOW BUILD

In jump, Corr and anyone helping can examine the core.

- **Average (8+) Electronics (computers) check (1D hours, EDU):** nine Ports, numbered I–IX, each with a header signature. All nine signatures appear to be Torvald's.
- **Difficult (10+) Electronics (computers) check (1D hours, INT):** three of the nine (I, IV and IX) are *different* underneath. Their signature validates, but the code inside is not written the way a person writes. No comments, no structure, no wasted instructions.
- **Halvard, Routine (6+) Mechanic check:** the core's processors are Schaut-grade substrate, the expensive kind. "Somebody paid a lot to run an old program."

Hand out the blank grid from Appendix A and let the players mark Build 1.

**Boot splash.** When the build is started it draws a line image: a slender tower on a flat plain under two small moons. Nobody recognises it yet.

## 3.3 — SCENE 3: SCHAUT, THE FIRST TIME

The *Marginalia* comes out of jump at **Schaut** (C720115-9) to refuel. This should take ten minutes of table time and leave a mark.

**What they see**

- A bone-dry, tan and white world. Enormous pale flats that throw the sunlight back like a salt pan.
- **Glasshouse Station:** a landing apron, a fuel shed, an admin dome and one hab block. Population twenty-seven, all Ostrander Lithic employees.
- A crawler track running north out of sight toward something the staff call **the Cut**.
- Unrefined fuel, sold without conversation. Law level 5: sidearms stay aboard.

**What is odd**

- The port has far more comms and processing hardware than twenty-seven people need.
- On approach, the ship's sensors pick up faint, regular radio noise from the flats. **Average (8+) Electronics (sensors) check (INT):** it is structured, and it is coming from everywhere at once.
- While the *Marginalia* is on the apron, the sealed core in Corr's cabin gets warm.
- A young port technician, **Irena Sabel**, watches the ship the whole time it is down. If approached she is friendly and vague and asks what they are carrying. See Appendix C.

**Petrosyan-Wu** will recognise the port: this is the posting they were refused in 1109.

## 3.4 — SCENE 4: THE STATION ON DRANG

**Drang** (E651124-7) has a frontier landing field, thin air and about forty people. One of them is **Tobiah Fenn**.

**Ostrander Research Outstation 3** closed in 1095. Fenn, once Torvald's junior technician, stayed on as caretaker and never left. The station is a half-buried run of laboratory units, most of them dark. He keeps one room powered: the machine room holding the **Drang build**.

**Fenn** is sixty-one, courteous, frightened and lonely. He will not sell. He will let them look, because nobody has asked him about the work in twenty years and he badly wants to talk.

**What Fenn will say, a little at a time**

- Torvald did not write the builds. "She took dictation."
- She made three copies deliberately, "so that it would take three people who trusted each other. She did not think there would ever be three."
- She did not die in a laboratory fire. He does not know how she died.
- The machine room has been noisier this past week. Since about the day they left Pankow.

**Checks**

- **Average (8+) Persuade or Diplomat check (SOC):** Fenn allows a full comparison rather than a supervised look.
- **Difficult (10+) Electronics (computers) check (1D hours, INT):** in the Drang build the odd Ports are **II, V and VII**: different ones from the Pankow build. Every other Port matches Torvald's hand.
- **Average (8+) Science (cybernetics) or Science (xenology) check (EDU):** the odd Ports are not programs so much as a recorded conversation. One side of one.

**The theory arrives.** Two builds, six odd Ports, no overlap. If the third build holds III, VI and VIII, then no copy is "genuine". The real thing is spread across all three. Let the players reach this themselves.

---

## 3.5 — SCENE 5: THE PATRON AT THE FIELD

When they return to the landing field, the *Annotated* is down beside the *Marginalia*. Balkan is sitting on a folding chair in a breather mask, reading.

He is charming about it: he could not bear to wait. He asks what they found. **This is the hinge of the scenario.**

- If they tell him the three-per-build theory, he goes very still, then laughs and doubles their fee. He asks whether Fenn would sell. If told Fenn would sooner die: "Really?"
- If they hold it back, he knows (**Deception opposed by his Investigate 2, INT +2**). He is just as pleasant.

Either way he says he will "pay his respects" to Fenn and follow on.

**Aguilar or Varga, Average (8+) Recon check (INT):** a second man came down from the yacht and has not been introduced. This is **Cade** (Appendix C).

---

## 3.6 — END OF ACT ONE

The *Marginalia* jumps for Schaut and Otha. Behind them, that night, Tobiah Fenn is found face down in the station's coolant sump. Ports II, V and VII have been cut out of the Drang machine: the boards themselves, pulled from the rack. The travellers will not learn this until Otha.
