# TRAVELLER: NOVEM PORTUS SPATIALES
## Chapter 5 — Act Two: The Rival

*Pankow Highport and Downport, then the one jump. Someone is tidying up behind them.*

> **Travel in this act.** Highport to downport is a short shuttle or ship descent. Narrate it; no rolls.

---

## 5.1 — SCENE 1: THE NEWS FROM KELL

The morning after Kell, the highport's company traffic channel carries a brief notice: the caretaker of Outstation 3 has died in an accident, and the station is sealed pending inspection.

- **Average (8+) Admin or Streetwise check (INT):** the *Annotated* logged a departure from Kell two hours after the *Marginalia* did, and is docked at the highport again.
- **Irena Sabel** is still on the highport while the *Cutwater* turns round. She finds one of them alone and says, quietly, "Don't keep it switched on near the hauler. It can hear itself." She will not explain. She seems surprised to have said it.
- **Corr, Average (8+) Electronics (computers) check (INT):** if the core is powered while the *Cutwater* is docked, the noise from her hold falls into a rhythm that matches the header of Port I.

---

## 5.2 — SCENE 2: RENKE

Before the job, Marguerite left a **backup image of the Baltic build** with an old friend on the highport, the ship broker **Oda Renke**, as insurance.

**What they find**

- Renke's office on the brokers' ring is sealed by *Ostrander Lithic* security. She was killed last night, while the crew was on Kell.
- Her body was found seated at her desk with her hands flat on the table and her head turned to the left, exactly as the figure sits in the line drawing that Port IV displays when run.
- Her safe is open. The backup image is gone.

**Checks**

- **Average (8+) Investigate check (1D hours, INT):** Renke let her killer in. Two cups on the desk. She knew him, or he was expected.
- **Difficult (10+) Streetwise check (INT):** a man from a private yacht was on the brokers' ring last night. "The kind you'd lend money to." The *Annotated* was back from Kell by then; Cade could have made the round trip and been on the ring in time.
- **Average (8+) Medic check (EDU):** a single contact stunner burn at the neck, then asphyxiation. Careful work.

**If they confront Baltic about Fenn or Renke:** he says, "How dreadful," and returns to the subject of the Ports. He is not bothered at all. Let the players notice.

---

## 5.3 — SCENE 3: DAME KESH

**Tessel House** is Vaderwall's consulate at Pankow Downport: a stone-faced residence that Dame Kesh's family built with Vaderwall stone, shipped in at great expense. Vaderwall is a feudal technocracy, where rank follows technical mastery and old systems are heirlooms. Corr was born there and knows the etiquette.

**Dame Ottoline Kesh**, seventy-four, uses a powered chair, has one working hand and no patience. Her consulship is a polite exile. She keeps the **Tessel build** in a vault under the residence, and has spent thirty years writing a monograph about Torvald.

**She receives them, until she learns who sent them.**

- "Baltic. That *annotator*. He has never made anything in his life. He came here a month ago, and I threw him out." The interview ends. Her secretary shows them out.
- **Formidable (14+) Persuade check (SOC)** to change her mind while they still carry his charter. **Average (8+)** if they openly renounce him and tell her about Fenn and Renke.

**What she knows, if won over**

- Torvald's last posting was Schaut. Kesh was her doctoral supervisor.
- The tower in the boot image is real. It is the first-landing survey tower on Schaut.
- Torvald wrote to her once in 1094: *"I have kept the ninth to myself. If the other eight are ever read aloud without it, I am sorry for whoever is listening."*

**Getting to the build**

| Approach | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Invited | Win Kesh over. She supervises; all checks at DM+1. |
| The fasting hour | Kesh dines alone for one hour each evening and the secretary leaves. **Average (8+) Stealth check (DEX)** for the group, then **Difficult (10+) Electronics (computers) check (1D minutes, INT)** on the vault's TL10 lock. |
| Force | Two house retainers (Chapter 3). The consulate is Vaderwall soil, so company security will not come in without Kesh's leave, and nobody else will come at all. |

**Masks at the vault door.** The vault has a gas suppression system, and by Vaderwall custom nobody enters without a breather mask. The secretary checks every visitor's mask at the door; anyone who comes in during the fasting hour finds a rack of spare masks just inside. All six travellers carry their own (Chapter 2), and Kesh keeps hers on a hook on her chair.

**The comparison:** **Difficult (10+) Electronics (computers) check (1D × 10 minutes, INT).** The odd Ports here are **III, VI and VIII.** The theory is confirmed. Eight of nine, or so it seems.

## 5.4 — SCENE 4: THE VAULT FLOODS

As the comparison finishes:

- **The ambush.** Whoever is nearest the door is hit from behind with a stunner (**Cade: Gun Combat (energy) 2, DEX +1; 2D+3, Stun**). If the group is too alert for this, Cade stays out of sight in the hallway.
- **The gas.** Cade triggers the vault's gas suppression system from the hallway panel, using a clone of Kesh's identichip that Baltic skimmed from her handshake a month ago. The door seals, alarms sound, and the vault floods with a heavy suppressant that drives out the air and is poisonous to breathe.
- **The clone dies.** Kesh's identichip runs Security/3 (CR22:111). Any copy of it works once and then erases itself. Cade's clone does its one job, then scrubs itself; he drops the dead blank into the hallway recycler on his way out.
- **Kesh** is in her chair, and her breather mask is not on its hook. By the time anyone reaches her she is dead. Ports III, VI and VIII are gone from the rack.

**The gas, by the book.** Treat the flooded vault as Atmosphere 2, Very Thin and Tainted, the same air as Schaut. A breather mask combines a respirator and a filter and protects fully (CR22:119, CR22:250). A traveller without one takes 1D damage from the taint (CR22:250); the suppressant is a heavy dose, so apply it every minute. A stunned or unconscious traveller cannot fit their own mask, but anyone beside them can do it for them.

**Getting out.** The door is sealed from the hallway, and only Kesh's identity opens it from inside. The obvious move is to hold her chip to the reader. It fails: the suppressant has corrupted it, and the reader rejects it as unknown. The only other copy of Kesh's identity has already erased itself. The travellers must override the panel with an **Average (8+) Electronics (computers) or Mechanic check (1D minutes, INT)**, with the gas rule above running for anyone unmasked.

**Afterwards** it looks like an old Vaderwall system failing in an old house. The vault's access log is keyed to Kesh's identichip, and with the chip corrupted the log cannot say who was inside or when. The only record that the travellers were in the vault is their own word, and Cade's.

- **Average (8+) Investigate check (1D × 10 minutes, INT):** the hallway panel was triggered by hand, and the hook on Kesh's chair was unclipped, not broken.
- **Average (8+) Investigate check (1D × 10 minutes, INT), in the hallway:** scraps of a new chip blank in the recycler, heat-scarred and wiped.
- **Difficult (10+) Electronics (computers) check (1D hours, INT), on the scraps:** it was a copy of Kesh's identichip, made recently from a skim. It tried to erase itself, as her security software forces any copy to do, and nearly succeeded. Enough survives to show it was used once.
- **Difficult (10+) Electronics (computers) check (1D hours, INT), on the reader:** a fragment of the log survives. It shows Kesh's identity opening the vault for the travellers, then Kesh's identity again, a minute before the gas, at the hallway panel. Kesh was sitting in the vault at the time. Someone had a copy of her.
- **Putting it together:** a skim needs close contact with Kesh. She told them she threw Baltic out a month ago. A **Routine (6+) Streetwise or Investigate check (INT)** with her secretary confirms Baltic's visit, and that he shook her hand at the door.

**Kesh's watchdog.** Tessel House runs a watchdog that outlives its owner. Any attempt to research Kesh's identichip, its scraps, the vault reader, or her records, finances or identity over the downport's local comms net (roughly 50 km around Tessel House) triggers a proactive attack on the device doing the research.

- **The software:** Agent/1 running Intrusion/2, both within Pankow's TL12 (CR22:111). It attacks with an Electronics (computers) check at DM+3: its Agent skill of 1 plus the Intrusion bonus of 2.
- **The defence:** opposed by the researching traveller's Electronics (computers) check. A device running Security software imposes DM−1 per level of Security on the watchdog; Corr's computer runs Security/1.
- **If the watchdog wins, Effect 1–3:** it wipes that research and locks the device out of the comms net for 1D hours.
- **If the watchdog wins, Effect 4+:** as above, and it reports the intruder's identity to Vaderwall's consular net and the downport proctors. The travellers are now on record as people prying into a dead consul's affairs.
- **If the traveller wins:** the research goes ahead as normal, and the watchdog's own log is readable. It fought one intruder before, a month ago, the night after Baltic's visit, and lost. The intruder's traffic was routed through a private yacht at the highport: the *Annotated*.
- **Out of range:** the watchdog cannot reach the highport. Players who carry the scraps or a copy of the reader's data up to the *Marginalia* can work on them safely. Anything they do through the downport net, including a call to the consulate, is fair game.

**Saving something:** a traveller who searches the flooded vault for Kesh's papers before leaving (**Average (8+) Investigate check (1D minutes, INT)**, with the gas rule above for every minute spent unmasked) recovers one stained, blistered page: Torvald's letter, quoted above, and a hand-drawn plan of the Schaut tower with a room marked **"IX"**. Kesh's monograph is ruined. Hand out both sides of the page (Appendix F, §F.3 and §F.4).

**At the downport field:** a note in a good hand is waiting with the field warden:

> *My dear friends, the charter is concluded, with my thanks. The completion fee is lodged with the highport bank. You have been exactly as good as I hoped. Mr. Cade will call for my property. Do not follow. A.B.*

Cade does call, an hour later, and finds what the travellers have just found: the *Marginalia* has been entered and **the sealed core holding the Baltic build is already gone.** Someone else took it. Cade is as surprised as they are, and for one moment both sides know it.

- **Average (8+) Investigate check (INT):** the lock was opened with a bought port master key, not forced. A hired local, paid well.
- **Cade** will not start a fight on the field with hazard crews sealing off Tessel House and half the downport watching. He leaves to tell Baltic.

**Why the core matters:** Baltic already holds an image of his own build, taken from Renke's safe. An image is not enough. The native Ports only behave on the Schaut-grade hardware they have lived in for twenty-five years, which is why he took boards, not copies, from Kell and Tessel House. He needs the physical core.

The travellers have their notes, their filled-in grid and a stained page.

## 5.5 — SCENE 5: THE RECENSION

Back on the highport, the trail has one more turn.

**Liana Telver**, Andros Telver's widow, did not sell willingly, wants the build back, and believes Baltic had her husband killed (§3.10). She is the patroness of the **_Silver Recension_**, a society of rich Veneet dilettantes and bored *Ostrander Lithic* executives who believe the Torvald Builds are a recovered scripture. It was her hired hand who took the core from the *Marginalia*.

She has chartered the highport's observation lounge for the evening and is staging a recital: thirty robed members, a great deal of incense, and the Baltic build running on a lectern under the window, with Pankow turning below.

**Finding it:** **Average (8+) Streetwise check (INT)**: thirty robed executives booking a lounge is the best gossip on the highport.

**What happens, unless the travellers change it**

1. The Recension chants Port headers. Nothing occurs. They have one build and no idea what it is.
2. Baltic walks in with Cade, in ordinary clothes, and tells them so, wittily and at length.
3. Liana goes for him. Cade kills her.
4. Baltic lifts the core from the lectern and leaves. The Recension scatters.

**Player options**

- **Take the core first:** an opposed **Stealth** or **Deception** check against the Recension's ushers (skill 0), before Baltic arrives. If they succeed, Baltic reaches Schaut with six native Ports and only an image of the other three. He will run it anyway, and it will go worse (Appendix D, §D.5).
- **Confront Baltic here:** he will talk. He is still friendly. He explains that the builds are "a salutation" and that he intends to be the one who delivers it. Cade is armed with an autopistol and a stunner. Baltic will not fight; he will leave.
- **Follow him:** the *Annotated* undocks for the jump point within the hour. See §5.6.

**This scene is a red herring by design.** The Recension knows nothing about the builds. Liana knows one true thing: her husband threatened Baltic the week he died. If the travellers talk to her before Baltic arrives, she tells them, and offers the airlock log from her yacht. If time is short, cut it, and have Baltic take the core from Telver's hired hand offstage.

---

## 5.6 — END OF ACT TWO: THE JUMP

The travellers know:

- The nine Ports are a handshake, spread three to a build.
- The ninth was "kept back".
- The tower is on Schaut.
- Baltic is going to read the other eight aloud.

**The one jump.** Schaut is one parsec away. The *Marginalia* takes on refined fuel at the highport and jumps a few hours behind the *Annotated*; both ships spend the same week in jump, so Baltic arrives first only by those hours. Play the jump as a scene cut with no rolls. If the table wants a moment in jump space, use it for the players to compare notes and decide what they will do about Baltic.
