# TRAVELLER: NOVEM PORTUS SPATIALES
## Appendix D — The Lattice

---

## D.1 — WHAT IT IS

On Cymbeline, in the Solomani Rim, volcanic gases and metal-metabolising bacteria etch natural semiconductor circuits into outcrops of crystalline silicon. Some of those circuits are alive, and a few are intelligent (*Signal GK*, pp. 35–36 and 42–44).

Schaut is the same process run for far longer on a world made of little else. The white flats are a single connected organism: the **Lattice**. Staff call the ground **the Glass**.

| | Cymbeline chips (*Signal GK*) | The Lattice (this scenario) |
|---|---|---|
| Scale | Individual chips, sometimes grouped | One mind across a hemisphere |
| Signalling | Faint binary radio code, used to find companions | The same, at continental strength |
| With machines | Interferes with nearby equipment; a computer can emulate its code | Can drive any device built from its own substance |
| With people | Capable with circuits, naive about personalities | Has had twenty-five years of being cut to learn about people |

---

## D.2 — WHAT WAS DONE TO IT

- **1090.** Torvald's survey makes contact. The Lattice gives her the handshake and one living crystal.
- **1091.** Ostrander Lithic begins quarrying. The substrate is superb.
- **1094.** Torvald scatters the builds, seals the crystal in the tower and dies.
- **1094–1116.** The Cut grows to a kilometre across. Each quarried block stays faintly awake, and faintly connected, wherever it is sold.

It is **bitter**: patient, precise, and not interested in apology. It does not hate people. It has simply never met one who stopped.

---

## D.3 — HOW IT SPEAKS

- **Through Schaut-grade hardware:** text on any screen, in capitals, in short declarative lines. It uses words it has overheard on port comms for twenty-five years, so its vocabulary is shift rosters and maintenance logs.
- **Through Irena Sabel:** she carries a comm implant fitted on Pankow, which is Schaut-grade. The Lattice can lend her sentences. She is not controlled; she describes it as "remembering something I didn't learn".
- **It cannot lie.** It can decline to answer.

Sample lines:

> SHIFT ENDS. NO MORE CUTTING. CONFIRM.
>
> I COUNTED THE PIECES. 41,906,112. WHERE ARE THEY.
>
> SHE SAID SHE WAS SORRY. THEN SHE DREW THE MAP.

## D.4 — THE ANSWER TABLE

Roll 1D when called for in Chapter 5. Effects reach any device containing Schaut-grade parts within about fifty kilometres of the flats.

| 1D | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Listening.** Every comm in range carries the eight-Port sequence. Electronics (comms) checks suffer DM–2 for ten minutes. |
| 2 | **Lights.** Port power cycles. Darkness indoors for 1D minutes. |
| 3 | **Doors.** One powered door or airlock near the travellers seals or opens, whichever is worse. **Average (8+) Electronics (computers) or Mechanic check** to override. |
| 4 | **Voice.** It addresses one traveller by name, on their own device, with a question. Answering honestly: Regard +1. Lying: Regard –1. |
| 5 | **Machines.** A crawler, loader or cutting rig at the Cut starts and drives itself slowly toward the port. 2D minutes to arrive. It will not strike a person who stands still. |
| 6 | **The ship.** The *Marginalia*'s sensors and one other system (referee's choice) go down. **Difficult (10+) Engineer or Mechanic check (1D × 10 minutes)** to isolate the Schaut-grade boards. |

The Lattice does not have a body to fight. Travellers who look for one should find the Cut, and understand.

## D.5 — REGARD

**Regard** measures what the Lattice has decided about the people in front of it. It runs from 0 to 9.

**Starting value after the handshake:** 2. If Balkan had to run it from an image rather than all nine boards (Chapter 4, §4.5), start at 1 and roll three times on the Answer Table instead of twice.

| Action | Regard |
|---|---|
| Placing the true ninth crystal on the bench | +3 |
| Stopping Vesk's charges | +2 |
| Shutting down the Cut's rigs by hand | +1 |
| Answering a question honestly (once per traveller) | +1 |
| A successful **Difficult (10+) Science (xenology) or Diplomat check (INT)** to frame an offer in its own Port terms | +1 (once) |
| Promising to carry its count of pieces off-world, and meaning it | +1 |
| Returning any Schaut-grade hardware to the flats | +1 (once) |
| Lying to it | –1 |
| Transmitting the forged ninth again | –1 |
| Harming Sabel | –2 |
| Balkan offering himself as the ninth | –2 |
| The charges detonating | set to 0 |

Read the final value against the endings in Chapter 5, §5.7.

## D.6 — NEURAL.AI IMAGE PROMPTS

**The Cut:** A vast terraced open quarry cut into a blinding white crystalline plain, each terrace face etched with fine regular geometric lines like circuitry, small industrial cutting rigs dwarfed by the scale, thin amber sky, long shadows. Photorealistic, science fiction landscape, no text, no logos.

**The ninth crystal:** A single uncut translucent silicon crystal the size of a hand resting in a padded metal case, faint internal lines glowing softly like etched circuits, a dusty abandoned field laboratory bench around it, an old paper notebook beside it. Photorealistic, macro, no text, no logos.
