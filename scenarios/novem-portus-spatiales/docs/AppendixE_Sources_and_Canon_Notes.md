- **Arturo Pérez-Reverte, *The Club Dumas* (1993)**, and its film adaptation *The Ninth Gate*. The structure is borrowed: three copies, the true set split between them, custodians dying behind the investigator, an unrelated society mistaken for the main plot, and a forged ninth piece. No text is reproduced.
- **Classic Traveller Adventure 13, *Signal GK*.** The silicon lifeform. See §E.3.

## E.3 — THE LIFEFORM

From *Signal GK*: natural chips form where volcanic gases and bacteria etch crystalline silicon (pp. 35–36); a powered chip signals to find companions and interferes with nearby equipment; its code is a binary language a computer can emulate; intelligent chips are strong in mathematics and weak at reading people (pp. 42–44).

Invented here: a chip organism on Schaut, its planetary scale, its quarrying, its bitterness, and its ability to drive hardware made from itself.

## E.4 — THE WORLDS

- **UWPs and hex numbers** come from the Voyager subsector map for Magyar, 1116. The map also shows gas giants and the borders the route crosses.
- ***Solomani Front*** does not describe these worlds; it covers the Solomani Rim sector. It supplies context only: the Imperium holds a small territory in Magyar reinforced from the Rim (p. 21); Ultima subsector is the gateway into it (p. 125); Magyar was long a source of reavers and pirates (p. 121) and of Aslan migration (p. 148).
- **Not established here:** which power claims each world. The scenario does not depend on it. If Schaut lies in the Imperial-held pocket, Ostrander Lithic operates under Imperial charter and Ending 7+ becomes a matter for the sector duke.
- **Invented:** Ostrander Lithic, every named person, Glasshouse Station, Outstation 3, Tessel Hold, the Lithic Club, and all local detail.
- **"Hellworld"** is a survey description, not a Core Rulebook trade code.

## E.5 — SCENARIO RULINGS

- **Schaut's air.** Very thin and tainted: a breather mask (combined respirator and filter) is required. The unmasked check in Chapter 5 (Average END each minute or 1D damage) is a ruling for this scenario, not a rulebook value.
- **Pregens** are designed, not rolled. Term counts and ages are consistent (age 18 plus four years per term) and skill totals are modest for the terms served. A full term-by-term rebuild is the obvious next pass.
- **No real-time interstellar comms.** Balkan cannot telephone. He keeps in touch by travelling: his yacht makes Jump-2 and is waiting at ports, and messages follow by ship.
