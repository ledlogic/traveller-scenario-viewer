## C.1 — THE *MARGINALIA*

A standard TL12 **Type A Free Trader** (CR22:194), nineteen years old.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hull** | 200 tons, streamlined, Hull 80 |
| **Armour** | Crystaliron, Armour 2 |
| **Drives** | Thrust 1, Jump-1 |
| **Computer** | Computer/5 with Jump Control/1 |
| **Sensors** | Civilian grade |
| **Fuel** | Fuel scoops and fuel processors (20 tons a day) |
| **Accommodation** | 10 staterooms, 20 low berths |
| **Cargo** | 81 tons |
| **Weapons** | None fitted |
| **Crew posts** | Pilot, astrogator, engineer, medic, steward |

**Quirks (CR22:188):** Damaged Sensors (DM−1 to Electronics (sensors) checks) and Double maintenance costs, both rolled when Okafor-Lindh took the ship as a mustering-out benefit (Chapter 2). The quirks belong to the ship; the second still applies in full.

**Sensor status.** The damage is still there. Last year, while Okafor-Lindh was running the ship for her old captain, Halvard masked it by fitting two cheap reconditioned sensor boards. They are Schaut-grade. With the boards in, the sensors work normally and the DM−1 does not apply. Take the boards out, or isolate them, and the DM−1 comes straight back.

| Situation | Electronics (sensors) |
|---|---|
| Schaut-grade boards fitted (start of play) | Normal, no DM |
| Boards isolated or removed, for example after Answer Table result 6 on Schaut (Appendix D, §D.4) | DM−1, the original Damaged Sensors quirk |

On Schaut, those boards are why the ship's sensors hear the Lattice so clearly, and why the Answer Table can reach her.

## C.2 — THE *ANNOTATED*

Baltic's yacht: 100 tons, Jump-2, Thrust 2, unarmed, crew of two (Cade pilots). Treat as a refitted **Scout/Courier, Type S** (CR22:190), with the double turret removed and the staterooms fitted out expensively. Every processor aboard is Schaut-grade, bought as the best available. After the handshake she will not start.

## C.3 — THE *CUTWATER*

*Ostrander Lithic*'s ore hauler on the Schaut run: a slow, ugly bulk carrier that docks at Pankow Highport every three weeks with a hold of white crystal block for the orbital yards. She carries a small cargo shuttle for the surface leg at Schaut; Irena Sabel is one of its crew. Nobody aboard thinks about the hold. The hold thinks about them.

No statistics are needed: the *Cutwater* is scenery in §4.3 and §5.1.
