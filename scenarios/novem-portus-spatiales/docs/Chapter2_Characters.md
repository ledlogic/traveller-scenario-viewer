## 2.2 — CHARACTER 1 — MARGUERITE OKAFOR-LINDH, "MARGIN"
**Captain & Broker · Merchant (Free Trader) · 5 terms · Rank 4, Experienced Trader · Age 38**

![Marguerite Okafor-Lindh](img/okafor-lindh.webp)

*"I don't read the code. I read the people who want it read."*

**Born IY 1078, Pankow** (Kline subsector, Magyar 2518, UWP A561412-C: excellent starport, small world, standard atmosphere, 10% water, tens of thousands of people, company government, law level 2, TL12). Raised in the *Ostrander Lithic* company town, where everyone she knew worked for the same employer and expected her to as well.

**Twenty years on tramp traders, one career, no regrets she will admit to.** She signed onto a free trader's crew at eighteen as a deckhand who could add up, and was quoting cargoes herself before her first term was out. The middle years brought two brushes with port law, both settled with paperwork rather than prison, and one night in 1102 when her captain took a stranded Scout courier aboard without asking anyone's leave. Teodor Varga rode home with them, and Marguerite spent the next month in front of a port magistrate explaining why a merchant ship was carrying a sealed Scout pouch. It was the first time she had to take charge of anything, and she found she was good at it. The same years brought a friendship with a Pankow ship broker, Oda Renke, that outlasted every ship she served on.

**She made Experienced Trader at thirty**, and by thirty-four she was running the deals for the whole crew. Through her final term she ran her captain's ship for him, and when he retired this year she took the ship as her mustering-out benefit: a nineteen-year-old free trader, a quarter of it paid for. She renamed her the *Marginalia*. The ship came with damaged sensors and double the maintenance bill of anything her age should have. Halvard fixed the first; nobody has fixed the second.

The software-appraisal sideline was Lukas's idea. Making it pay was hers.

## 2.3 — CHARACTER 2 — TEODOR VARGA, "SLIPKNOT"
**Pilot & Astrogator · Scout (Courier) · 4 terms · Rank 2 · Age 34**

![Teodor Varga](img/varga.webp)

*"One parsec at a time. It's not a ship, it's a habit."*

**Born IY 1082, Veneet** (Kline subsector, Magyar 2519, UWP C742865-8: routine starport, medium world, thin tainted atmosphere, 20% water, hundreds of millions of people, captive government, law level 5, TL8). Raised under a government that answers to someone off-world, on a crowded world where a filter mask is part of getting dressed.

**Sixteen years carrying other people's secrets.** He joined the Scout Service's courier branch at eighteen for the one thing Veneet could not give him, which was distance. His first run nearly ended him: a failed jump on the fringes left his courier crippled, and he hitch-hiked home across the frontier on five different ships. The last was a free trader out of Pankow, where a sharp young cargo hand named Marguerite Okafor-Lindh talked her captain into taking him, and where the captain let him fly the last two legs. He came home in 1102 with a debt of favour to every crew that carried him, and two people who would rather he had not made it.

**He made Scout at twenty-six** and kept his head down after that: courier runs, jump plots, the occasional citation. A relationship ended badly along the way and left someone on Veneet who still will not say his name kindly. An accident during a cargo transfer in his last term took some strength out of his arms, and the years took a little more.

He left at thirty-four, tired of not knowing what he carried. He now carries whatever Marguerite tells him to, which he finds restful.

## 2.4 — CHARACTER 3 — YUSRA HALVARD, "TORQUE"
**Chief Engineer · Merchant (Merchant Marine) · 5 terms · Rank 3, 3rd Officer · Age 38**

![Yusra Halvard](img/halvard.webp)

*"She's a one-parsec ship. Ask her nicely and she is still a one-parsec ship."*

**Born IY 1078, Dexter** (Voyager subsector, Magyar 2416, UWP B587510-B: good starport, small world, dense atmosphere, 70% water, hundreds of thousands of people, company government, no law level to speak of, TL11). A wet, green company world where nearly everyone farms for the same employer, and where a girl who liked engines had one obvious way out.

**Twenty years in the engine rooms of other people's ships.** She signed with a megacorporate line at eighteen and spent her first term flying cargo shuttles between hauler and dock before anyone noticed she was better below decks. By twenty-six she was a 4th Officer with a reputation for fixing what the manuals said could not be fixed, and somewhere along the way she found a partner who still writes to her from Dexter.

**She made 3rd Officer at thirty** and stopped there. The line liked her where she was. Her one real lapse came in her last term, when a crewmate asked her to carry a crate of reconditioned processor boards off a hauler and past customs, and she said yes and talked her way through. She kept a box of the boards for herself. Two of them are in the *Marginalia*'s sensor rack now.

She left at thirty-eight with a Ship Share, which she put into Marguerite's ship, and a preference for keeping one small ship running well.
