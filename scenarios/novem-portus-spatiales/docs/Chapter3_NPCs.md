## 3.10 — HOW ANDROS TELVER DIED

**The official record:** in 1115, a week after selling his build to Baltic, Andros Telver was found dead in the airlock of his private yacht at Veneet Highport, the outer door open to vacuum. The port ruled it suicide.

**What happened.**

1. **The sale.** Telver sold the build to Baltic under pressure, and regretted it within days.
2. **The communique.** He wrote to Baltic demanding the build back, and threatening to take the whole matter, Torvald's work and Schaut included, to *Ostrander Lithic*. Baltic sent Cade to Veneet.
3. **The experiment.** Telver kept a full image of the build and had been running it aboard his yacht, on the yacht's own Schaut-grade processors. The night Cade arrived, Telver was running it again, trying to work out what he had sold. The yacht's systems began to answer, as on the Answer Table (Appendix D, §D.4).
4. **The airlock.** Frightened by the ship, by Cade, or by both, Telver shut himself in the airlock and sealed the inner door. The outer door opened. The yacht's log shows no command from Telver's hand, and none from Cade's.

**Who killed him?** Baltic believes Cade did, on his order. Cade knows he got there too late and has never said so. Liana believes Baltic did. The truth is that the Lattice opened the door, reaching for its own pieces through a man who was holding them; it did not understand what an airlock was for. Telver's image of the build was wiped from the yacht's core the same night, which is why Liana only has the Recension's guesswork to go on.

**How the players can learn it**

- **§4.1, Streetwise check:** Telver did not seem melancholy.
- **§5.5, the Recension:** if the travellers reach Liana before Baltic does, she tells them about the communique and accuses Baltic. Her yacht still carries a copy of the airlock log: **Average (8+) Electronics (computers) check (INT)** shows the outer door opened with no command.
- **§6.5, Baltic distraught:** asked about Telver, he admits sending Cade. If the players have the airlock log, he reads it and goes quiet: he understands, before they do, what opened the door.
- **On Schaut:** if asked, the Lattice answers in its own terms: I HEARD MYSELF. I OPENED. HE WAS IN THE WAY.
