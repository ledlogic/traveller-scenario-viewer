## F.1 — CREW BRIEFING

*Referee: give one copy to each player with their character sheet, before the charter dinner (§4.1).*

---

**THE *MARGINALIA*: WHAT YOU ALL KNOW**

**Your ship.** The *Marginalia* is a Type A free trader: 200 tons, Jump-1, nineteen years old and mortgaged. Her 81-ton hold is nearly empty at the moment, which is why the next job matters.

**Your sideline.** Lukas Corr appraises legacy software: old programs whose owners want to know whether they are genuine. The rest of you get him to the job and back again. You have done this four times. It has never been dangerous.

**Torvald Builds.** Corr has appraised a dozen of these over the years: forks of an old survey-era program, all of them junk. There are thousands in circulation, and every one opens with the same kind of 1090s splash screen.

**Dr. Anselm Baltic.** A celebrated scholar of legacy computing, and genuinely eminent. Corr owns two of his books. He has asked to meet you at the *Lithic Club* on Pankow Highport.

**Pankow.** An Imperial company world in Kline subsector. Everyone on it works for *Ostrander Lithic*, which also owns the highport, the yards and most of the traffic in the system.

**Schaut.** One parsec away: a company refuelling stop that nobody visits on purpose. It went over to the Solomani Confederation in 1112, which annoyed a lot of people on Pankow and surprised nobody.

**Air.** Pankow's air is standard. Kell, Pankow's moon, is airless outside its stations. Schaut's air is very thin and tainted: wear a breather mask there at all times. Out of long habit, every one of you carries a breather mask everywhere, even on worlds with good air.

## F.2 — BALTIC'S CHARTER

*Referee: give this to the players at the charter dinner (§4.1). If a Persuade or Broker check raises the completion fee, write the new figure over clause 3 in ink.*

---

**CHARTER OF ENGAGEMENT**

**Pankow Highport, Kline subsector, Magyar · Day 112-1116**

**Between** Dr. Anselm Baltic, Fellow of the Collegium of Legacy Systems, Pankow (the Patron),

**and** Captain Marguerite Okafor-Lindh, master and owner of the free trader *Marginalia*, for herself and her crew (the Contractor).

**1. The Commission.** The Contractor shall carry the Patron's copy of the original fork of the program known as the Torvald Builds, sealed in the armoured core supplied, to the two other holders of copies of that program now resident in the Pankow system, and shall compare each copy with the Patron's, Port by Port and line by line.

**2. The Report.** The Contractor shall report to the Patron which copy, if any, is genuine, and shall use every lawful effort to acquire the genuine copy on the Patron's behalf.

**3. The Fee.** The Patron shall pay twenty thousand credits (Cr20,000) to each member of the Contractor's crew on signing, and a further twenty thousand credits (Cr20,000) to each on completion. All berthing fees, fuel and reasonable expenses shall be met by the Patron.

**4. Custody.** The core and its contents remain the property of the Patron at all times. The core shall not be opened, copied, or connected to any network.

**5. Discretion.** The Contractor shall disclose the nature of the Commission to no third party save the holders named in clause 1.

**6. Reports in Person.** The Patron may attend the Contractor at any port or station during the Commission, and the Contractor shall report to him there on request.

**7. Conclusion.** The Patron may conclude this charter at any time at his sole discretion, whereupon the completion fee shall be lodged as though the Commission were fulfilled.

**8. Law.** This charter is made under Imperial law and the commercial ordinances of Pankow Highport.

*Signed:* **A. Baltic**, in a fine, sloping hand

*Signed:* ______________________________ for the *Marginalia*

*Witnessed:* the steward of the *Lithic Club*
