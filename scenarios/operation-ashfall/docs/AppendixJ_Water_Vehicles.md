## J.1 — WHERE THIS COMES FROM

This appendix reuses vehicle hardware originally statted for *Operation Deep Current* (Gashidda), Appendix F. Not everything there transfers — Deep Current's creature mechanics (Void Mantle), the Driftweb organism, the atmosphere processor, and its Hiroshima-supplement cross-references are all specific to Gashidda's ecology and setting, and none of that appears here. What *does* transfer cleanly is the vehicle hardware itself: submarines, patrol craft, and work pods sourced from a real third-party supplement, generic enough to reflavor for any Traveller water world — including Ys.

**Source citation:** *Traveller Water Vehicles Volume 1* by Colin Dunn, Northern Edge / Travellers' Aid Society (TAS). MGT2e compatible. Covers surface vessels (TL6–TL12) and submarines (TL6–TL14).

Five vehicles below are genuinely useful for Meridian Sound content (Appendix G); two more are included for completeness in case a table wants them.

## J.2 — SHAFIN PATROL BOAT
*The natural fit for Meridian Sound's naval harbor patrol presence*

**TL:** 10 | **Type:** Surface Patrol Vessel | **Skill:** Seafarer (ocean ship)

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Agility | −2 |
| Speed (Cruise) | Medium (Slow) |
| Range (Cruise) | 800 (1,200) km |
| Crew | 10 |
| Passengers | 8 |
| Cargo | 3.625 tons |
| Hull | 100 |
| Cost | Cr625,000 |

**Armour:** 12 all facings. **Weapons:** 4× Medium Rotary MG in pintle mounts (4D, Auto 6), Lt. Antimissile System (1D, Auto 6). **Defences:** Decoy Dispenser, Prismatic Aerosol Dispenser, Smoke Discharger. **Carries a Sea Ranger RIB (§J.3) in a deck berth.**

**Ashfall role:** This is the vessel type behind Meridian Sound's ordinary harbor traffic patrols — the presence that normally keeps the naval approaches to the Pi Building orderly, and (per Appendix G, §G.1) the deterrent system that's unstaffed tonight because the crew that would run it is stretched thin managing the uprising. If the team's Container Drop (§G.2) or Pinnace Gambit (§G.3) trips an alarm, a Shafin-equivalent is the most likely **response vessel** — armed for cover and compliance, not real combat; a crew facing serious resistance falls back and calls for actual naval backup rather than pressing an engagement. Treat one showing up as a strong signal the team's Countdown-adjacent complications are compounding, not an immediate combat encounter.

| Speed (Cruise) | Medium (Slow) |
| Crew | 1 |
| Passengers | 3 |
| Hull | 10 |
| Cost | Cr32,275 |

**Armour:** 3 all facings. **Weapons:** None (crew carry personal arms). **Equipment:** Basic sensors and underwater sensors, improved navigation and comms.

**Ashfall role:** What a Shafin actually deploys for close-quarters work — boarding a suspicious vessel, running down a lead in the harbor shallows. Open-topped, fast, and completely unarmored beyond the hull itself; a team caught by one of these in open water is dealing with numbers and small arms, not serious firepower.

## J.4 — HIGHLINE UTILITY SUBMARINE
*The plausible cover identity for a "routine harbor tender"*

**TL:** 13 | **Type:** Utility Submarine | **Skill:** Seafarer (submarine)

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Agility | −4 |
| Speed (Cruise) | High (Medium) |
| Range (Cruise) | 2,000 (3,000) km |
| Crew | 2 |
| Passengers | 6 |
| Cargo | 9 tons |
| Hull | 180 |
| Cost | MCr30 |

**Safe Depth:** 3,000m | **Crush Depth:** 9,000m. **Equipment:** Improved sensors and underwater sensors, sensor mast, fresher, life support, hostile environment protection, **ventral airlock** supporting dive operations at depth. **Weapons:** None (attachment points exist but the vessel isn't normally armed).

**Ashfall role:** This is exactly the kind of vessel the Pinnace Gambit's decoy transponder (Appendix G, §G.3) claims to be — a routine, unremarkable civilian/utility submersible, the sort of harbor traffic nobody flags twice, even without an uprising providing cover. If a referee wants the team to actually requisition or steal a working submersible rather than just spoof one, a Highline-class is the correct, plausible choice: enough cargo and passenger capacity for the whole team, an airlock for diver egress at depth, and nothing about it that reads as military.

## J.5 — SEAHARVESTER-PATTERN WORK POD
*A maintenance submersible, and a second legitimate cover for approaching the Pi Building's pylons*

**TL:** 10 | **Type:** Submersible (Work Pod) | **Skill:** Seafarer (submarine)

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Agility | −2 |
| Speed (Cruise) | Slow (Very Slow) |
| Range (Cruise) | 150 (225) km |
| Crew | 1 |
| Passengers | 1 |
| Cargo | 100 kg (external tool rack) |
| Hull | 12 |
| Cost | ~Cr400,000 |

**Safe Depth:** 900m | **Crush Depth:** 2,700m. **Equipment:** Hostile environment protection, short-term life support, **ventral airlock**, 2× improved manipulator arms, exterior flood lighting, external tool mount points. **Weapons:** None — civilian maintenance vessel.

**Ashfall role:** A small, unremarkable inspection pod — the kind of thing a naval facility runs quarterly hull and pylon inspections with, exactly like the ones supporting the Pi Building's own water-facing foundations (Appendix G, §G.1). A team that wants a *third* option beyond the Container Drop and the Pinnace Gambit — quieter than either, slower, more plausible if someone has the patience to fake a maintenance window rather than force an entry — could use one of these to approach the support pylons directly under color of routine work. Requires Seafarer (submarine); operating one without any Seafarer skill at all should carry a real penalty (DM−3, per the source material) rather than working by default.

## J.6 — SUBMERSIBLE MODIFICATION (GENERAL RULE)

Any ground vehicle, grav vehicle, or watercraft can have a Submersible option added at construction, usually paired with an Aquatic Drive and Life Support. Hostile Environment Protection is required.

- **Cost:** Doubles Cost per Space; consumes 10% of the vehicle's total Spaces (round up)
- **Speed:** One Speed Band lower than an equivalent dedicated submersible of the same TL
- **Range:** Reduced to 10% of base
- **Safe/Crush Depth:** Half of an equivalent dedicated submersible

**Ashfall role:** The rule to reach for if a table wants to know whether some other vehicle already in play — the Reach-rated hauler (Chapter 3, §3.4), say — could theoretically be adapted for a stint underwater. It's a real, costed option, not free improvisation.

## J.7 — FOR COMPLETENESS: TWO MORE, IF A TABLE WANTS THEM

Two additional Deep Current vehicles are strong hardware but a harder narrative fit for Ashfall specifically — included briefly rather than in full writeups, since neither ties into existing Appendix G content the way §J.2–§J.5 do.

**GP 'Hsu'-Class Research Submarine** (TL14, deep-diving to 14,000m, unarmed, science-focused) — plausible as a naval oceanographic research vessel somewhere in Ys's fleet inventory, but nothing in the current scenario calls for extreme-depth research capability; Meridian Sound's relevant depths (per Appendix G) don't need it.

**Instellarms 'Shield'-Class Military Submarine** (TL13, torpedo-armed planetary defense boat, advanced stealth) — a genuinely interesting worldbuilding answer to "why hasn't the Navy just used a submarine to solve this," but deploying one against a domestic uprising is wildly disproportionate, and its presence would raise the question of why Krein didn't already use harder assets against the uprising itself. Best left offstage unless a referee specifically wants an escalation option late in the campaign.
