## F.1 — IMRE BASARAN

![Imre Basaran](img/basaran.webp)

*Retired naval systems technician, encountered at the Dockyard Screening (Chapter 3, §3.3)*

*"I was seventeen kilometers out when it happened. Close enough to see the flash reflect off the harbor. I've spent thirty years telling myself that distance meant something."*

Basaran is 61, wiry, and has the specific stillness of a man who's spent decades trying not to be noticed by his own government. He spent nine years as a junior systems technician on the original equatorial test site — the installation that became the Glass Reach thirty-one years ago (Chapter 1, §1.4) — and has never fully explained, even to himself, why he stayed in naval service afterward instead of leaving Ys entirely. He's at the screening tonight because his granddaughter, Petra, dragged him there; he didn't want to go, and he hasn't stopped watching the exits since he arrived.

**Characteristics:** STR 6 (+0) · DEX 7 (+0) · END 7 (+0) · INT 9 (+1) · EDU 10 (+1) · SOC 5 (–1)

**General Skills:** Streetwise 1 · Recon 1 · Persuade 0 · Athletics 0

**Expert-Level Skills** *(what thirty-one years in and around Ashfall actually taught him)*

| Skill | Lvl | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Engineer (life support) | 2 | Original test-site systems training, never fully lapsed |
| Electronics (computers) | 2 | Enough to recognize the old analog fail-safe architecture on sight, not enough to defeat it alone |
| Mechanic | 1 | General technical competence from a working life around naval hardware |
| Investigate | 1 | Not trained — earned, the slow way, from decades of quietly paying attention |

**Equipment:** Nothing notable — civilian clothes, a battered analog wristwatch he won't explain, a locket photo of Petra. He is not a combatant and the referee should never put him in a position where his stat block is meant to matter in a fight.

**What he offers the team:** see Chapter 3, §3.3 in full. In short — the physical route into the control bunker, confirmation that its fail-safes are deliberately offline/analog, and (if the team hasn't found it independently) a lead to his nephew at the naval logistics depot warehouse (Chapter 3, §3.4) for Reach-rated transport.

**Playing him:** reluctant, precise, uncomfortable with being thanked. He is not a mentor figure and doesn't want to be treated like one. If Achebe (Chapter 2, §2.5) is the one who earns his trust, let that read as genuine — he responds to competence and quiet care, not authority, and Kade's command presence will get her further with loyalist checkpoints than it ever will with him.

Hands clasped, slightly forward-leaning, the posture of someone ready to leave a conversation at any moment. Muted color palette, warm practical lighting against a cool blue-grey ambient background. ## F.2 — OTHER NAMED CONTACTS (LIGHTWEIGHT)

These don't need full stat blocks — use Basaran's block as a rough template (civilian, non-combatant, Athletics 0) if a referee needs numbers for any of them unexpectedly.

- **Petra** — Basaran's granddaughter, late teens, the reason he's at the screening at all. Present as color, not a combat participant; a referee wanting a gut-punch epilogue beat (Chapter 6, §6.6) can use her reaction to the outcome without ever needing her stats.
- **Basaran's nephew** — night-shift dispatcher at the naval logistics depot warehouse (Chapter 3, §3.4). Never named on-page deliberately; give him a name at the table if he gets more than one line of dialogue.
