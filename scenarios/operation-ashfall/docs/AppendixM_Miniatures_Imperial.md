## M.1 — WHAT YOU NEED

Every Imperial-side figure type across the whole doc set, with suggested quantities and the visual cues that actually matter for picking or converting minis. Scale should match whatever baseline you're already using for the PC roster (Chapter 2).

| Type | Qty | Look | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Governor Krein** | 1 (unique) | Older civilian man, administrator's coat, **no armor, unarmed** — should read as harmless, not military at all | Appendix A |
| **Loyalist Close Protection Detail** | 6–10 (generic guards) | Combat Armour, Basic tier (bulky, Protection +13), Advanced Combat Rifle. Populates lobby checkpoints, Floor 4's heavy presence, and Floor 5's Staff Barricade Position | Appendix A §A.5; Appendix I §I.2 |
| **Colossus-Pattern Sentry Walker** | 1 (unique, oversized) | Size 8 chassis, Rhino/Orca-equivalent scale per real Robot Handbook design rules — needs genuinely oversized "mech/walker" scale, not standard infantry, stationed in the plaza between the towers | Appendix N §N.2 (supersedes the original Appendix G §G.4 write-up) |
| **Dog Sentries** | 3 (2 at Floor 0's plaza approaches, 1 in Krein's office) | Size 4, genuinely dog-scale — small, fast, quadrupedal-flavored Walker locomotion, distinct from the big Walker's silhouette | Appendix N §N.3 |
| **ISS Field Agents** | 3–4 | **Plainclothes, deliberately non-military** — concealable armor under civilian clothes, no visible rifles, reads as investigators, not soldiers | Appendix C §C.4 |
| **Station Chief Vantry** | 1 (optional unique) | Same profile as ISS agents above — a career interrogator, not a combatant. Can reuse an agent mini or get something distinct if she's run on-page | Appendix C §C.1 |
| **Reach Wardens** | 3–5 | **Scavenger militia — mismatched, field-patched gear**, Flak Jacket, Shotgun. Should look visually distinct from uniformed Loyalist guards — improvised, not issued | Chapter 5 §5.4 |

**Not Imperial, but the other physical prop this scenario calls for:** the **Halveth** (Appendix G §G.5) — the ~21m ambush predator in Meridian Sound — is native fauna, not an Imperial asset. Listed here anyway since it's the natural thing to prep alongside this list, not because it belongs to the same faction.

## M.2 — SCALE NOTE

Everything above except the Sentry Walker and the Halveth should sit at your standard human-scale baseline (matches the 32mm-scale sizing already worked out for Nerys/Dolphin conversion elsewhere in this project). The Walker and the Halveth are the two pieces that specifically need to read as oversized against that baseline for the encounter to land visually — undersizing either one loses the "this is genuinely a bigger problem" read that both are designed around mechanically (Appendix G §G.4's stability-check mechanic for the Walker, and the Halveth's Large Sauropod-tier stat block).
