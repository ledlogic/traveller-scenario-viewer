## Appendix H — Unit Patches

## H.1 — WHAT THIS IS

SolSec doesn't officially sanction unit patches. Team UNDERTOW has them anyway, the same way it has cigars nobody's supposed to smoke in a sealed Battle Dress helmet (Chapter 2) — unofficial morale culture that survives specifically because command has bigger problems than a patch on somebody's kit bag. Use this as a lightweight table reward: when a player's character does the thing a patch is named for, they get to add it to their gear, no roll required. It's pure flavor by default — see §H.3 for an optional light mechanical version if your table wants one.

## H.2 — THE PATCHES

| Patch | Earned When | Flavor |
|---|---|---|
| **"Fight to the Finnish"** | A character (Nerys especially, but anyone) finishes a fight decisively — last hit, last word, last one standing | A stylized dorsal fin over crossed weapons. Nerys's personal favorite for reasons that require no explanation. |
| **"Let Me Tell You a Tail"** | A character spins a story, true or not, that changes how an NPC acts — a lie, a tall tale, one of Rivit's "memories" (Chapter 2, §2.7) landing at exactly the right moment | A coiled, exaggerated tail shape, equal parts storyteller's flourish and warning label. |
| **"It Blewed Up Good"** | Voss (or anyone) successfully uses Explosives to solve a problem — a breach, a collapsed spire (Chapter 5, §5.2), the override itself (Chapter 6, §6.2) | Deliberately misspelled, deliberately proud of it. A small mushroom-cloud silhouette that's more cartoon than accurate. |
| **"Something's Fishy"** | A character catches a lie, spots surveillance, or successfully investigates something they weren't supposed to find — an ISS tail (Appendix C), a StudyDeck lead (Appendix D) | A magnifying glass over a fish skeleton. The pun does all the work; nobody's proud of it, everybody wears it anyway. |
| **"So Long and Thanks 4 Fish"** | Earned once, at extraction or at the very end of the scenario, by anyone who made it out | A Hitchhiker's Guide nod worn completely straight-faced. The closest thing this unit has to a service ribbon. |

## H.3 — OPTIONAL: LIGHT MECHANICAL VERSION

If a table wants patches to mean something beyond flavor, without turning them into a full advancement system: **a character wearing a patch gets a one-time +1 DM on their next check that matches the patch's theme**, spent whenever the player wants to invoke it, then the patch stays on the jacket as a memento with no further effect. This rewards the moment that earned it without stacking bonuses indefinitely — one patch, one bonus, done. "So Long and Thanks 4 Fish" is the obvious exception, since it's awarded at the very end of a session and never gets a chance to matter mechanically — that one's pure sentiment, and should stay that way.
