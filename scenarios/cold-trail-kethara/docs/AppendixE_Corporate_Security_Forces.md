## D.1 — OVERVIEW: WHO MERIDIAN IS AND WHY THEY MATTER

**Meridian Recovery Solutions** is a two-person corporate security and asset recovery firm operating under Imperial commercial law out of the Lakamsal Town downport district. They are contracted by **Shulgi & Enki Vehicle Hire** (owner: Dara Vasquez) to locate, recover, and if necessary legally detain parties who have abandoned or stolen rental vehicles.

**Shulgi & Enki corporate colours: tan and dark brown.** The vehicles, uniforms, and equipment all carry these colours — tan bodywork with dark brown trim and logo markings, practical and commercial-looking rather than authoritative. Meridian Recovery operates in the same palette as their client: their field jackets, vehicle markings, and robot stencilling all use the Shulgi & Enki tan/dark brown scheme, since Meridian works almost exclusively for Shulgi & Enki on Lakamsal and has adopted the client's visual identity by convenience rather than policy. *(Note on the field jacket specifically: it is ordinary branded clothing, Protection 0 — not armour. Kaur's Flak Jacket, worn over hers, is what actually protects her. See Appendix H, "Armour & Protection Quick Reference," for the full cast's protection values.)*

They are not a military threat. They are something worse: a *civilian complication* with legal standing on Imperial soil, arriving at a covert SolSec installation during the most exposed period of the mission.

**Why they cannot simply be neutralised:**

| Problem | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Killing or seriously injuring them | Two dead Imperial civilians in an illegal SolSec base. The investigation that follows does not end. |
| Detaining them long-term | They have check-in protocols with Shulgi & Enki. Missed check-in at Hour 24 → Vasquez files a missing persons report. By Hour 36, the downport knows something went wrong in the Reach. |
| Letting them leave | They have seen the base. They have seen armed personnel, camo netting, signals equipment. Their debrief to Vasquez → Vasquez to the local council → local council has a legal obligation to report to Imperial administrative authority within 72 hours. |
| Convincing them it's a legitimate geo survey | Geological survey contractors do not have auto-sentries, gauss rifles, signals intercept arrays, or reasons to stop civilians from leaving. Chen has been doing this for twenty years. He will know. |

**The clean resolution the scenario is designed to make difficult:** The team needs to hold Meridian, keep them comfortable and unharmed, prevent any communication with Shulgi & Enki, and then either depart before anyone misses them (extraction timeline), or find a way to delay Vasquez's concern long enough to be aboard SHEPHERD. This requires Deception, Persuade, and Leadership — the skills of the command cell, not the shooters.

## D.4 — TACTICAL APPROACH: HOW MERIDIAN ARRIVES

### The Approach Sequence

Meridian does not arrive in a rush. Chen is methodical. The approach from the northwest along the game trail takes approximately 3–4 hours from the point where they pick up the 23-day-old tire tracks. They stop twice to photograph the tracks and verify they are following the right trail.

**Hour 18–20:** Meridian reaches the outer edge of the jungle trail, approximately 1km from the base perimeter. Chen launches Eye for a preliminary survey of the area ahead. He can see: tree canopy (the base is invisible under it), the river glinting through the undergrowth to the east, and the top of the basalt formation rising above the canopy line. He cannot see the cave entrance, the camo netting, or any personnel.

**Hour 20–22:** Meridian advances slowly, following the game trail. Holt rolls 100m ahead as a pathfinder (Chen's standard operating procedure — if there is a megafauna encounter, better Holt takes the first impact). The motion sensor grid at 800m radius detects Holt at this range. The team has approximately **40 minutes of warning** before Meridian reaches the wire perimeter, assuming they notice the sensor alert.

**Hour 22–24:** Meridian reaches the wire perimeter. Eye is now within 200m of the cliff face and can see: the camo netting (from above, partial view), the shape of vehicles under the netting (the two rental trucks), the perimeter wire, and the sensor post masts. Kaur calls out the wire to Chen. Chen stops the convoy. He picks up his loudspeaker.

## D.5 — ENCOUNTER RESOLUTION TRACKS

The Meridian encounter has three primary resolution tracks. The scenario is designed to push toward Track 2 (negotiated hold), but all three are viable depending on player choices.

---

### TRACK 1 — MISDIRECTION (Cleanest)
*Goal: Send Meridian away without them understanding what they have seen*

**Requires:** Deception 10+ (Vasiliou or Breckenridge) with a coherent cover story delivered before Eye gets imaging of the equipment. Modifier: DM-2 if Eye has already transmitted footage of the antenna array or perimeter sentries; DM+1 if the team's cover story is internally consistent and pre-prepared.

**The cover story that almost works:**
> "Imperial Survey Service — restricted xenobiological study zone. You are in a protected research area. Your vehicles are being held as material evidence in an investigation into unauthorised access to a restricted ecological zone. You'll need to leave the vehicles with us and contact the IISS downport office to arrange release."

The problem: Chen will want to verify this. He has an Admin 2 and an Advocate 1, and he knows the IISS downport presence is a single relay drone, not an active station. He will ask for credentials. He will ask which regulation authorises vehicle seizure without a civil warrant. He will be politely, methodically, professionally impossible.

**Alternative misdirection (higher risk, lower skill target):** Vasiliou presents IISS survey credentials from the team's cover identity kit — they are registered as a geological survey company. Deception 8+ (the documents are legitimate-looking, just the wrong institution). On success, Chen accepts the story long enough to withdraw to the game trail and make a check-in call. On failure — he notices a discrepancy in the credentials (she is claiming IISS authority with a private survey company registration — this does not match). He will politely note the discrepancy and ask her to clarify.

**If Track 1 succeeds:** Meridian withdraws to the game trail perimeter. Chen files a check-in report noting "access dispute, claim of Imperial survey authority — verifying through official channels." He does not have official channels fast enough to matter before extraction. The team has bought 12–24 hours.

### TRACK 2 — NEGOTIATED HOLD (Most Likely)
*Goal: Detain Meridian comfortably, control communications, release them at or after extraction*

**The basic approach:** Vasiliou meets Chen at the perimeter, acknowledges this is a government operation she cannot discuss, and tells him directly that he and his team will need to remain on site for a period she cannot specify while the operation concludes. She will ensure their safety, their comfort, and their compensation. She will not tell him why.

**Chen's response:** He will negotiate. He is not going to simply agree to be held without knowing what he is being held for. He will want:
- Confirmation that this is a legitimate Imperial or Confederation government operation (Vasiliou cannot confirm either without compromising the mission)
- Assurance that his employer will be notified that he is safe (cannot allow communication)
- The ability to verify the vehicles are undamaged (this can be granted — it doesn't hurt anything)
- A written acknowledgement of the hold for his records (Vasiliou will refuse this)

**The negotiation skill check:**
- Persuade (Vasiliou or whoever leads) 8+ to convince Chen that cooperation is in everyone's best interest
- Each point of Effect gives 4 additional hours before Chen becomes actively uncooperative
- Effect 4+ means Chen actively cooperates, manages Kaur, and maintains a professional relationship throughout the hold
- Effect 1–3 means Chen cooperates under protest — he will comply but his mood will deteriorate after 12 hours
- Failure means Chen announces he is leaving and invokes his civil rights under Imperial commercial charter — the team must decide whether to physically stop him

**Kaur during Track 2:**

Kaur will comply with Chen's lead — she respects his authority in the field — but she will be watching for an opportunity to send her burst transmission to Fenwick. Every hour she is held is an hour she looks for that window. The team should assign someone to keep her engaged and away from her motorcycle's instrument panel.

**Kaur's burst transmission opportunity:** Whenever the team's attention is split — during the Tyrant encounter, a Shield Beetle incursion, an Imperial drone sweep alert, or any other crisis — Kaur looks for the window. Recon contest: Kaur Recon 2 vs whoever is watching her. If she wins, she sends the burst. It takes 4 seconds and she will deny it afterward.

**Managing the check-in clock:** The team needs to either spoof Chen's check-in (Electronics (comms) 10+, Breckenridge — Kaur's voice can be mimicked if Breckenridge has heard enough of it, Deception 8+) or convince Chen to send a false check-in himself (Persuade 10+ — he considers this professionally improper). Each failed check-in narrows the extraction window.

**Hold logistics:** Where do you put two civilians and a recovery robot?

| Location | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Motor pool bay | Spacious enough, away from sensitive equipment | They can see the ground scout drone, the parts locker, Haramoto's equipment — all clearly military spec |
| Crew quarters | Private, comfortable | They are inside the classified installation |
| Outside, at the perimeter | Maintains separation | They can hear and see the cliff face, the antenna array, any megafauna encounter |
| At their own vehicles, game trail | 400m from base | They are outside sensor range; Kaur can reach her motorcycle; requires a guard posted |

**Holt during Track 2:** Holt has attached to one of the trucks. If Track 2 is selected, the simplest approach is to allow Holt to verify the trucks' condition (Chen's immediate request), then have Chen command Holt to stand down. Holt will sit beside the trucks, inactive, until Chen activates it again. It is not a threat during a negotiated hold — it becomes a threat again the moment Chen decides the negotiation is over.

### TRACK 3 — COVER STORY HOLDS (Lucky)
*Goal: Convince Meridian they have arrived at a mundane geo survey gone wrong and should leave*

This track only works if Eye has not yet transmitted images of the military equipment, and if the team has pre-positioned their cover story before Chen arrives. It requires:

- The auto-sentries powered down or concealed (they are unambiguously military hardware)
- Team members in civilian field clothes, not combat armour
- Haramoto visible doing something that looks like geological survey work
- A plausible story for the trucks being still on site ("engine trouble, waiting for a parts delivery")
- Deception 10+ (the cover is thin — Chen knows geo survey schedules and a 23-day rental with zero communication is not normal geological field work)

**If Track 3 succeeds:** Chen accepts a delayed-return story and agrees to facilitate an extension on the rental (Vasiliou pays Cr500 immediately for goodwill). He leaves, files an extension report with Vasquez, and Meridian departs. This is the fastest and cleanest resolution — but it requires the team to have prepared it, which means Vasiliou needs to have tasked someone with cover management from the moment the motion sensors detected Holt at 800m.

## D.6 — MERIDIAN AND THE MEGAFAUNA

Neither Chen nor Kaur has operated this far south in the Reach. They know the megafauna are present. They do not know how large.

**Chen's reaction to the Tyrant:** If the apex predator appears while Meridian is at the base vicinity, Chen will immediately attempt to retreat to the recovery car and secure the doors. He is not a fool — he grew up on this world and he knows what a 15-tonne predator means. The encounter will interrupt any negotiation mid-sentence. Vasiliou's response to the Tyrant in this moment will tell Chen more about who these people are than any cover story.

**Kaur's reaction:** She will draw her autopistol. She knows it is inadequate. She will draw it anyway, because militia training. If Mouton or Kowalski handles the Tyrant effectively — especially if Kowalski deploys the chemical deterrent — Kaur will be visibly impressed. This is actually the moment where a skill demonstration (competence under extreme pressure) does more for the team's negotiating position than any Deception roll. Kaur respects competence above everything.

**After the Tyrant withdraws:** Chen will say, quietly, to whoever is nearest: *"What kind of geological survey carries chemical deterrent rated for a Titan predator?"* He will not push for an answer. He already knows it is not a geological survey.

### IVRB Note — What It Is and Why It Matters

The **Imperial Vehicle Recovery Bureau** is an Imperial commercial regulatory body — part of the Domain of Sol's administrative infrastructure for frontier worlds. It tracks rental and commercial vehicle assets across the subsector, maintains registration databases, and coordinates with colonial authorities on abandoned or stolen commercial property. It has a reputation for meticulous record-keeping and dogged administrative follow-through that has made it the bane of anyone who has ever tried to quietly lose a rental vehicle in the frontier.

What it is **not**: an enforcement arm. The IVRB does not deploy agents, does not carry weapons, and does not have authority to compel action by military or colonial militia forces — unless the matter involves property belonging to registered noble houses, licensed megacorporations, or commercial entities of SOC 10+ standing. Shulgi & Enki Vehicle Hire is SOC 6 equivalent. Two rental trucks are not going to bring an IVRB field team to the Kethara basin.

What the IVRB filing **does**: creates a permanent, timestamped administrative record that cannot be un-filed, cannot be suppressed below Domain level, and will surface in any subsequent Imperial investigation of events at Lakamsal grid KR-4418. If Ibáñez-Ochoa's after-action report mentions an abandoned civilian vehicle installation in the Kethara basin, the IVRB cross-reference will be pulled automatically within 48 hours. The trucks' registration traces back to the SolSec cover company. The cover company traces back to Altiplano — an obscure, depopulated Imperial world chosen precisely because a registration there draws no automatic suspicion the way a Confederation-territory filing would. But the beneficial-ownership paperwork beneath that registration, if anyone ever digs deep enough, has patterns consistent with Confederation shell corporations regardless of which Imperial rock the paper company nominally sits on. This is a very long chain — but the IVRB filing is the first link in it, and it is now permanent.

**For the team:** Getting off Lakamsal before Hour 48 is not just about the extraction window. It is about not allowing the IVRB clock to start.
