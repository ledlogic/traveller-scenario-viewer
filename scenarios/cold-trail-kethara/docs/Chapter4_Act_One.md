## 4.0 — SCENARIO STRUCTURE

Cold Trail in Kethara Reach runs across four Acts, each approximately 45–60 minutes of table time in a standard 3–5 hour convention session. The Acts are not rigid — they represent the natural escalation of the pressure system. The referee should let player decisions compress or extend each Act rather than watching the clock.

**The three countdowns running at all times:**

| Countdown | Starts | Triggers | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| TSU-9 Failure | Hour 0 (140% load) | Hour 6 (Failing); Hour 18 (Failed) | Thermal detection; base exposed |
| Jury-rig expiry (if used) | When Haramoto jury-rigs | 6 hours later | Load returns to 140% and climbing |
| Imperial sweep window | Hour 4 | 8 hours of passes (Hours 4–12) | 2 failures = Marines dispatched |
| Extraction window | Hour 0 | Hour 72 | SHEPHERD arrives; mission ends |
| Marchetti-Solis arrival | Hour 0 (in-system) | Hour 72 (courier pass) | She personally investigates |
| Ibáñez-Ochoa response | When Marchetti signals | +90 minutes (55 if G-carrier direct) | Marines on the ground |

**The key insight for the referee:** by Hour 8, three threat layers are potentially running simultaneously — each requiring a different response. Osei-Bonsu needs a social/deception solution. Marchetti-Solis needs a technical/signals solution. Ibáñez-Ochoa needs an extraction. The team cannot fight all three. They must deceive, delay, and run in the right sequence.

## 4.1 — ACT ONE: THE MISSION IS ALMOST COMPLETE
*Day 23, Hour 0*

### Opening Read-Aloud

> Twenty-three days. The jungle doesn't care how long you have been watching it — it moves, it breathes, it makes sounds that belong to something larger than anything on the survey charts. But the mission is close. Pelczar's console glows green. The surveillance run is 80% complete. The courier confirms extraction in 72 hours. You are, by every professional measure, winning.
>
> Then the morning shift begins.

---

### The Surveillance Run

The fixed-wing drone is on Hour 4 of its final 6-hour pass — 280km northeast, monitoring the Imperial relay installation's transmission architecture. Okonkwo manages the link. Roll **Electronics (remote ops) 8+** every 30 in-game minutes of surveillance time. Failure = 2-hour repositioning delay; the drone must reacquire its pattern. A critical failure (Effect -3 or worse) = drone loses signal and enters autonomous mode — 1D6 minutes to recover, during which the surveillance pass is paused.

The run completes at Hour 6 if no delays, Hour 8 if one delay occurs. Pelczar needs the complete run before she can transmit the data package.

**Referee note:** Establish calm professionalism in this first scene. The team has been here 23 days. This is routine. Routine is the enemy — it makes the cascade harder to process when it starts. Let the players settle in before you pull the floor.

---

### The Engineer's Discovery (Hour 1)

Haramoto notices. She always notices. She does not immediately announce it to the group — she runs a second diagnostic first.

The TSU-9 console reads 140% load. Her second diagnostic confirms it. She has approximately 18 hours before unit failure without crash site parts.

> *"The spoofing unit. Hundred and forty percent. I can jury-rig it — buys us six hours. Or we go to the crash site. Those are the options."*

She says this to Vasiliou quietly, not to the room. She does not editorialize. She waits.

**The first decision point:**
- **Jury-rig now** (Engineer (electronics) 8+, 1 hour): Drops load to ~115%, buys 6 hours. Does not solve the problem. Haramoto will have to go to the crash site anyway — the jury-rig just decides when.
- **Send someone to the crash site immediately**: 4km northeast through open delta, stalker territory, dawn patrol time. Round trip: 2–3 hours minimum. Gets the coolant canister and the laser rifle (the correct tool for Shield Beetles). Costs team members and splits defensive posture.
- **Do both**: Jury-rig now, plan crash site run for Hours 3–5. Most common outcome. The jury-rig expiry at Hour 7 then creates a second pressure point.

### Vasiliou's Stim (Hour 0–1)

Sometime during Act One's opening — after the briefing, during the first sensor checks, or when Haramoto presents the TSU-9 news — Vasiliou approaches whoever is nearest (most likely Mouton) and asks quietly:

> *"Do you have a stim in your kit?"*

She pockets it without comment. She uses it within 20 minutes. By Hour 2 she is noticeably, uncharacteristically animated. The comms minimisation reminders will come fast this Act.

### Pelczar's Concern (Hour 2)

Pelczar pulls Vasiliou aside. She looks like someone who has been awake for six days and knows it.

> *"Two things. The first: the Imperial station timing shift from this morning — I've been thinking about it. It's not a sweep pattern. It's a response pattern. Someone near us signalled them. Compromised equipment or compromised personnel. I don't know which."*

She pauses. Vasiliou waits.

> *"The second thing. The relay's product has changed. Six weeks ago it was fleet positioning data — standard military surveillance. Now it's something else. Volume is three times what it was. The encryption has escalated twice. I'm seeing cipher identifiers I've never encountered on a frontier relay. Whatever is passing through that installation right now — it is not what this mission was briefed on. And I am three decryption passes from understanding what it is. I need clean signal conditions to run them. The storm is going to delay that by at least four hours."*

She is wrong about the timing shift — it is a storm-cycle calibration burst from the relay's overloaded hardware, not a response from inside BLIND COBRA. She is entirely right about everything else.

**Breckenridge's position:** He knows the timing shift is a calibration burst. He has not told Pelczar this. He is not sure how to raise it without explaining how he knows what Imperial relay calibration patterns look like. Watch his player's face when Pelczar makes the mole accusation. This is the moment.

**The players do not know which parts to believe.** That is the point.

### Problem Two — TSU-9 Failure

**If jury-rig used at Hour 1:** The jury-rig expires at Hour 7. The load returns to 140% and climbing. Haramoto must act now — the full repair or the unit enters Failing status.

**If no jury-rig used:** The unit crosses into Failing (141%+) around Hour 6. Military sensors detect the base at 20km range. Osei-Bonsu's orbital relay drone auto-logs an anomalous geothermal signature and sends him an automated alert. He will investigate within 2–4 hours.

**The full repair:** Mechanic 10+ (Difficult), 2 hours, requires crash site parts. Haramoto rolls at net 7+ (skill 3, toolkit DM+2). Returns unit to ~85% load. Buys approximately 10 additional days.

**Can Haramoto do the full repair before the unit fails?** If crash site parts retrieved at Hour 3–5, she can begin repair at Hour 5–7. She finishes Hour 7–9. This is tight — the surveillance run may still be ongoing during her repair window. Pelczar needs Breckenridge on ECM. Okonkwo is managing drones. Mouton is on perimeter. Vasiliou is coordinating. Nobody can do two things at full effectiveness.

### The Shield Beetles Inside the Base (Optional)

If the base entrance has not been secured against wildlife intrusion, Shield Beetles may enter the motor pool — attracted by the same warmth and organic material that draws them to the cliff face. The entrance throat fits one beetle with 10cm clearance.

Two beetles in the motor pool is an inconvenience. Three beetles in the motor pool when Pelczar is trying to concentrate is something else. Vasiliou's line:

> *"Are the ports intact?"*
> *[Ports confirmed.]*
> *"Then the beetles are an inconvenience. Deal with them. Carefully."*
