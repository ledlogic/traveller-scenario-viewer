## G.3 — STREPHON ASSASSINATION NEWS PROPAGATION

*Campaign reference — what happens after the scenario ends.*

**The Event:** Emperor Strephon is assassinated by Archduke Dulinor on Holiday 1117 — the first day of Imperial Year 1118. The scenario takes place approximately **4 months before this date**.

### How News Travels to Lakamsal

| Date | Event | Who Learns | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| Holiday 1117 | Strephon assassinated at Capital (Sylea, Core sector) | Nobody at Lakamsal | — |
| 1118, Week 2–3 | First confused reports leave Capital via x-boat | Nobody at Lakamsal | News still 40+ parsecs away |
| 1118, Week 6–8 | News reaches Domain of Sol headquarters | Domain command level | X-boat relay — reports still contradictory |
| 1118, Week 8–10 | News reaches 291st Fleet headquarters at Banasdan | Admiral and senior staff | X-boat + naval courier priority traffic |
| 1118, Week 8–12 | **SolSec headquarters learns** | SolSec Director level | SolSec's own cross-border intelligence networks — possibly before the Imperial frontier forces |
| 1118, Week 9–11 | **Marchetti-Solis learns** via NI secure channel | Marchetti-Solis personally | Encrypted priority courier — among the first at the frontier |
| 1118, Week 10–12 | **Ibáñez-Ochoa learns** via 291st Fleet | Ibáñez-Ochoa and ship's officers | Fleet broadcast — possibly conflicting with earlier sealed orders |
| 1118, Week 10–14 | **Osei-Bonsu learns** via IISS administrative update | Osei-Bonsu | Scout Service relay — low priority, delayed |
| 1118, Week 12–16 | **Lakamsal civilian population hears rumours** | Colonists, downport traders | Merchant traffic, free traders — confused and contradictory |
| 1118, Week 14–20 | **Confirmed news reaches Lakamsal** | Everyone | Multiple sources converge — still debated |

### What It Means for the Campaign

**If BLIND COBRA extracts successfully (around 1117, month 8–9):** They are debriefed approximately 3 months before the assassination. Pelczar's Partition 3 data arrives at SolSec headquarters. Analysts note the elevated threat language and forward planning papers treating the Solomani Front as a near-term active flashpoint. They interpret it as evidence of an Imperial pre-emptive move against the Confederation.

**They are wrong.** The nervous traffic was not preparation for an offensive. It was the early tremors of an internal crisis that nobody in the court fully understood yet.

Three months later Strephon is dead and the Imperium begins its collapse. SolSec's analysts pull Pelczar's data back out of the file and re-read it. They realise they were looking at the earliest signals of what became the Rebellion — and they misread them as an external threat rather than an internal one.

### The Characters — Where They Are When They Hear

| Character | When | How | Their Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vasiliou | 1118, week 8–10 via SolSec | Secure briefing | Professionally unsurprised. Requests reassignment to the Front. |
| Okonkwo | 1118, week 8–10 via SolSec | Hears from Vasiliou | Asks what it means for the drones. He means this literally — his drones are still on Lakamsal if the strike team didn't recover them. |
| Breckenridge | 1118, week 8–10 via SolSec | He already suspected. | The cipher identifiers told him something at Domain level was unstable. He did not predict assassination. He sits very still for a very long time. |
| Haramoto | 1118, week 8–10 via SolSec | Engineering briefing | Immediately calculates what happens to Imperial frontier infrastructure during a civil war. She is right about most of it. |
| Mouton | 1118, week 8–10 via SolSec | Told by Vasiliou | Says nothing. Checks his rifle. Waits for orders. |
| Pelczar | 1118, week 8–10 via SolSec | She already knows more than anyone. | Opens Partition 3. Re-reads it. Understands now. Says: *"We were looking at the first tremor. And we called it an earthquake."* |
| Kowalski | 1118, week 8–10 via SolSec | Medical briefing | Asks what happened to the Kethara Tyrant. Nobody else thinks to ask this. |
| Osei-Bonsu | 1118, week 10–14 via IISS | Administrative notification, weeks late | Still on Lakamsal. Looks up at the sky. Wonders if anyone is still watching. |
| Marchetti-Solis | 1118, week 9–11 via NI | Encrypted priority courier | She had the data. She was routing it. She filed maintenance reports about a relay carrying the first warning signs of the Rebellion through its overloaded civilian processors. Nobody read the reports. |
| Ibáñez-Ochoa | 1118, week 10–12 via 291st Fleet | Fleet broadcast, contradictory | Opens his sealed orders. Reads them again. The authority that signed them may no longer exist. Folds them. Puts them away. Does his duty. |
