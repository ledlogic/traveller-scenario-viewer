## "Hour Six — The Apex Finds the Heat"

## C.1 — PURPOSE OF THIS APPENDIX

This appendix presents a sample play sequence for **Hour 6** of the scenario — the moment when the jury-rig expires, the TSU-9 enters Failing status, and the Kethara Tyrant is actively at the cliff base. It is designed to show the referee how to run three simultaneous tracks without losing pacing or letting any single problem resolve too cleanly.

This is not the only way Hour 6 can play. It is the most common convergence point based on playtesting, using a five-player group with Haramoto dispatched to the crash site at Hour 2 and the jury-rig applied at Hour 1.

**Assumptions for this sample:**
- Jury-rig applied at Hour 1 — expires at Hour 7 (slightly optimistic) or Hour 6 (standard)
- Crash site retrieval team: Haramoto + Mouton, departed Hour 2, on return trip
- Kowalski present (6th character)
- ECM sweep: 2 passes completed — both contested rolls won by Breckenridge
- Apex predator first detected at Hour 5, currently at cliff base, investigated and not yet engaged

---

## C.2 — THE SCENE: HOUR 6, FOUR MINUTES TO JURY-RIG EXPIRY

### Setup

The TSU-9 console reads 138%. The jury-rig has held — barely — for five hours. In four minutes, Haramoto's crude flow restrictor reaches its thermal limit and the load will begin climbing again. She is four kilometres away in the open delta with Mouton.

Breckenridge is at the signals console running his third ECM contest pass. Okonkwo is splitting his attention between the armed recon drone (Priya, orbiting the cliff base at 60m altitude) and the surveillance drone completing its final pass. Pelczar has not looked up from her console in six hours.

The Kethara Tyrant is at the river bank below the cliff face. It is not moving. It is watching the thermal shimmer from the three overtaxed TSU-9 nodes — three points of warmth in the cold post-storm air, at heights between 8m and 18m on the basalt face. The IISS biotelemetry tag in its shoulder transmits to Osei-Bonsu's orbital relay drone. Heart rate: elevated. Activity state: investigating.

Kowalski is at the perimeter sensor station. She has been reading the tag for twenty minutes.

> *"It's been stationary for eighteen minutes. That's not predation behaviour. It's not territorial behaviour either. It's the behaviour of an animal that has found something it doesn't understand and is deciding whether it needs to understand it."*

Vasiliou: *"What does it do next?"*

Kowalski — Science (biology) 8+, Effect 3: *"One of two things. It investigates further — tries to climb or press against the cliff face to get closer to the heat source. Or it gets bored and leaves. We have maybe ten minutes before it decides."*

---

### The Jury-Rig Expiry — Minute 0

The TSU-9 console chimes. Amber warning light pulses. The load reading ticks from 138% to 141%.

**Stage 2 — Failing.** Eight nodes are now running hot. The thermal pattern is no longer credible as natural geology.

Vasiliou, looking at the console: *"Haramoto."*

The encrypted comms unit is already live. Haramoto's voice comes back, slightly breathless — she has been moving.

> *"I know. I can see the console remote feed. We have the canister. Six minutes."*

---

### The Three Simultaneous Problems — Running Order

**The referee should cut between these tracks, not resolve them sequentially.**

---

**TRACK ONE — TSU-9 (Haramoto + Mouton, external)**

Haramoto and Mouton are on the game trail 800 metres northeast of the station. Haramoto carries the coolant canister in a field pack. Mouton is on rear security — Stalker territory.

The perimeter motion sensors log two ground contacts at the 600m ring: Stalker pair, Stealth 3. Okonkwo sees them on the ground scout drone's sensor feed before Mouton does.

> *"Mouton — two contacts, your six, 400 metres. Coming from the northeast."*

**Roll: Recon 10+ for Mouton to spot before engagement. Electronics (remote ops) 8+ for Okonkwo to redirect the ground scout to provide overwatch.**

If Okonkwo provides overwatch (success): the Stalker pair sees the drone, assesses it as too large to take, and veers south. Mouton and Haramoto reach the station in six minutes. No engagement.

If Okonkwo fails (on another task, or fails the roll): Stalker pair closes to 100m before Mouton sees them. He has to decide: engage and make noise, or evade into the denser undergrowth (Athletics (endurance) 8+ to outdistance them carrying a full pack).

**Time pressure:** Every minute Haramoto is not at the grav belt is a minute the TSU-9 continues climbing toward FAILED status. At 161%+ the thermal signature is detectable by any aerial asset. Marchetti-Solis's next drone pass is 42 minutes away.

---

**TRACK TWO — The Apex Predator (Okonkwo + Kowalski, internal)**

The Tyrant has made its decision. It approaches the cliff base — not charging, not territorial, but moving with the deliberate pace of an animal that has identified something worth investigating. 14 metres. 10 metres. It stops at the cliff face.

At this range, Priya's downward thermal sensor reads a massive heat signature — 15 tonnes of warm-blooded apex predator directly below the Zone A port cluster. Three of the ports are already running above thermal threshold. If the Tyrant physically investigates the ports — pressing against them, as a 15-tonne animal might press against something that smells warm and organic — the mechanical stress could damage a port seal.

> **Okonkwo:** *"Vasiliou, I can bring Priya down for a harassment pass. Drive it off the face. Flyer (rotor) 10+."*

> **Vasiliou:** *"Risk to the drone?"*

> **Okonkwo:** *"If I miss the roll — the predator swipes at it. Eight hits, Armour 2. A hit at close range from a Claw 4D attack destroys it."*

**Option table for the group:**

| Option | Roll | Consequence of Success | Consequence of Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drone harassment pass | Flyer (rotor) 10+ | Predator retreats — gone 4 hours | Priya takes 1D damage or is destroyed; predator agitated |
| Kowalski — chemical deterrent | Science (biology) 8+ | Predator avoids treated area 2 hours | Effect -2 or worse: predator aggressive response |
| Flood the cliff approach (drainage sluice) | Engineer 8+ — Vasiliou operates the console | Clean — predator retreats from water flow | Sluice malfunctions; uses emergency water reserve |
| Wait it out | No roll — costs 2D hours | Predator leaves when bored | During wait: DM-1 all ops; nobody can be on the cliff face |

**Kowalski's read:** *"Chemical deterrent is cleanest. But I need Okonkwo to lower Priya to my position so I can attach the dispenser canister to its payload bay. That's a minute while the drone is stationary."*

**The conflict:** Okonkwo cannot lower Priya to Kowalski AND simultaneously maintain ECM coverage on Breckenridge's next pass window. He is one person. He needs to choose.

---

**TRACK THREE — Imperial ECM (Breckenridge, internal)**

The fourth ECM pass is due in eight minutes. Breckenridge has won the previous three. He is running the SIGINT suite at maximum output — the TL11 intercept rig is warm to the touch, its fans audible over the cave's background noise.

On the current pass, the TSU-9 entering Failing status adds an automatic DM-1 to his ECM masking — the overheating nodes are generating irregular EM interference that he cannot fully compensate for. His effective roll: Electronics (comms) 3 + INT DM +2 + SIGINT suite DM+2 + Failing status DM-1 = net +6, target contested vs ECM 3.

He will likely win. But if Okonkwo has diverted drone assets (losing the drone shadow option), his margin decreases. And one failure brings the cumulative count to 1. One more failure after that calls the Marines.

**Breckenridge, quietly:** *"Whatever we are doing about the predator — do it before the next pass. If I'm running ECM and Okonkwo is dealing with the Tyrant and Haramoto isn't back yet —"*

He doesn't finish the sentence. Everyone in the room understands the rest.

## C.3 — RESOLUTION SEQUENCE

The referee cuts between tracks in this order, keeping each segment to 60–90 seconds of real table time:

**Cut 1 — Haramoto/Mouton (1 minute)**
Okonkwo redirects the ground scout to overwatch. Mouton makes his Recon roll. Either the Stalkers are deterred or Mouton has a brief engagement decision. Haramoto arrives at the station entrance or is delayed 2 more minutes.

**Cut 2 — The Predator (1 minute)**
The group decides: drone harassment, chemical deterrent, drainage sluice, or wait. The decision is fast because Okonkwo needs to either lower Priya or maintain his station. This forces the choice rather than discussing it.

**Cut 3 — ECM (30 seconds)**
Breckenridge makes his roll. Success or failure. If failure: one suspicion logged. Vasiliou acknowledges it with a look at the console.

**Cut 4 — Haramoto at the cliff face (1 minute)**
If Haramoto has arrived: she is in the grav belt, coolant canister secured, ascending to Zone B. She cannot begin port servicing while the predator is at the cliff base. If the deterrent worked — she goes to work immediately. If the Tyrant is still present — she hovers at Zone A height, 8 metres above the river bank, waiting.

The Tyrant looks up at her.

She continues working.

---

## C.4 — PACING NOTES FOR THE REFEREE

**Do not let players solve problems completely before the next one arrives.** In this sequence:

- If Haramoto arrives and immediately begins port servicing, the ECM pass interrupts with Breckenridge's roll before the first port is done
- If the chemical deterrent is applied, it takes effect over 2 minutes — during which the ECM pass occurs
- If Mouton engages the Stalkers, the gunfire is a noise event that the Tyrant registers — it looks south, then back at the cliff face

**The three problems should feel tangled, not sequential.** The correct tone is professional competence under compound pressure — these are trained operatives managing multiple simultaneous threats, not heroes dramatically overcoming one crisis before the next begins.

**Vasiliou's role during this sequence:** She does not take direct action on any of the three tracks. She coordinates — allocating Okonkwo's attention, confirming Haramoto's position, watching Breckenridge's console. This is her job. She is also, quietly, watching her stim window closing.

*[Hour 1 stim: active. Effect window: approximately 80 minutes from Hour 5. She is approaching the back edge of it. She will not show this. She is aware of it.]*

## C.5 — POSSIBLE OUTCOMES

| Outcome | Result | What Changes |
|---|---|---|
| All three tracks managed | Stalkers deterred; Tyrant retreats; ECM pass won; Haramoto begins repair | Best case — all problems delayed, repair begins |
| Predator delayed but not driven off | Haramoto cannot access Zone B for 2D hours; TSU-9 continues climbing | Repair delayed; load reaches 155%+ before she can start |
| ECM failure (first) | 1 failure logged; Marchetti-Solis increases attention; Breckenridge at +0 effective next pass | One failure from Marines being dispatched |
| Stalker engagement | Gunfire heard; Tyrant turns toward sound; Priya must track Tyrant AND cover retrieval team | Okonkwo split attention DM-1 on both |
| All three fail simultaneously | TSU-9 enters FAILED; Tyrant at cliff face prevents repair; ECM compromised | Emergency extraction — the scenario pivots to Chapter 5 immediately |

**The last outcome is not a failure state.** It is a different version of the scenario, resolved in Chapter 5. BLIND COBRA is a team of professionals — they extract from worse situations than a 15-tonne predator, a failing thermal unit, and an Imperial sweep drone. They will not all make it to the ramp smiling. But they will get to the ramp.
