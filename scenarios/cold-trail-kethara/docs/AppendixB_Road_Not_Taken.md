## B.1 — PURPOSE OF THIS APPENDIX

Cold Trail in Kethara Reach is designed to support multiple valid approaches. The scenario has a default flow (Acts One through Four, following the cascading threat structure) but experienced players will deviate from it, and individual table decisions open paths the core scenario does not cover in detail.

This appendix documents the most significant alternative paths and their consequences — what happens when a player fires a weapon at the wrong moment, what happens if the auto-sentries engage the Tyrant, and what happens when the team makes decisions that are locally sensible but strategically costly.

## B.2 — THE AUTO-SENTRY PROBLEM

### What the Sentries Are Set To

At scenario start, the three auto-sentry units are in **ALERT mode** — they acquire targets that breach the perimeter wire arc but do not fire without confirmation from the ops centre. This requires an Average Electronics (sensors) 8+ check from the operator (standard is Okonkwo, or whoever is at the sensor station) to confirm threat classification before the sentry fires.

This is not the default setting on TL10 auto-sentries. Vasiliou changed it on Day 14 after two false-acquisition events from Grazer herds. She did not want gauss fire inside the perimeter attracting every predator in the river basin. The team has been operating under this constraint ever since.

**What the sentries would do on automatic (ACTIVE mode):** Fire at any large contact breaching the perimeter wire — no confirmation required, Skill 2, 3D gauss. This would be effective against Stalkers (Armour 2, easily penetrated), marginal against the Tyrant (Armour 5, AP gauss penetrates but 3D is slow killing), and completely useless against Titanic Beetles (Armour 18+).

### If Someone Switches Sentries to ACTIVE

Any character with Electronics (sensors) 0+ can switch the sentries to ACTIVE mode — it is a menu option, not a contested action. It takes a minor action at the ops centre console.

**Consequences of ACTIVE mode:**

| Scenario | Immediate Result | Secondary Result |
|---|---|---|
| Grazer herd at perimeter | All three sentries engage simultaneously — blood in the delta in minutes | Tyrant arrives within 1D hours, drawn by blood and the acoustic spike of three gauss weapons firing |
| Kethara Tyrant at perimeter | Sentries engage — 3D gauss ×3 per round, eventually effective | Thermal spike (+15% TSU-9 for 2 hours per engagement minute); acoustic signature detectable at 5km by Osei-Bonsu's survey scanner |
| Stalker pair at perimeter | Clean engagement — Stalkers taken in 2–3 rounds | Blood draws the Tyrant; acoustic signature; the other Stalkers in the pack investigate |
| Shield Beetles | Rounds bounce off Armour 10+ — no penetration | Beetles, now alarmed, scatter toward the cave entrance and the port array |
| Marines at perimeter | Engagement begins immediately — no warning shot, no challenge | Imperial forces have rules of engagement that permit return fire on automated weapons; Voss's Marines will treat ACTIVE sentries as a hostile act and proceed accordingly |
| Meridian Recovery robot (Holt) at perimeter | Holt is targeted — Armour 6, 24 hits, takes damage | Chen immediately calls the situation into Shulgi & Enki; Kaur draws her weapon; negotiation becomes dramatically harder |

**Vasiliou's standing order on the sentries:** *"ALERT mode. If you switch them to ACTIVE without my authorisation, you had better have a very good reason when I ask you about it."*

She will ask about it. Immediately. While the situation is still unfolding.

### If the Sentries Fire on the Tyrant

The most likely auto-sentry misfire in the scenario is a Tyrant engagement when the ops centre operator is managing a simultaneous crisis and someone else switches the sentry to ACTIVE as a quick fix.

**Sequence of events:**

**Round 1:** All three sentries acquire the Tyrant and open fire simultaneously. 3D gauss ×3 = 9D total, AP (bypasses Armour 5), average 22.5 damage vs 60 hits. The Tyrant takes a hit. It is not killed. It is very angry.

**Round 2:** The Tyrant turns from the cliff face and charges the sentry nearest to the cave entrance — the one it can see and hear. Bite 5D against the sentry unit: Armour 2, the sentry is destroyed on a hit. Acoustic spike as the sentry's ammunition cooks off. Thermal spike begins — all the gauss fire has heated the area detectably.

**Round 3 onward:** Now the team has an actively aggressive 60-hit Tyrant with 38 hits remaining, inside the perimeter wire, charging toward the cave entrance throat.

**TSU-9 impact:** The engagement generates a thermal and acoustic spike equivalent to a firefight at the cliff base. TSU-9 load increases +25% for 2 hours. If the unit was already at 140%, it enters FAILING status. Osei-Bonsu's orbital relay drone logs an anomalous event. He receives an alert within 10 minutes.

**The resolution:** The team must now deal with a wounded, angry 38-hit Tyrant in close quarters — at the cave entrance throat (3m × 2.5m, one Tyrant cannot fit through but it can block it and bite anyone near the entrance), with the TSU-9 at FAILING, and Osei-Bonsu's investigation clock started.

**Haramoto's comment**, once the situation is resolved: *"We could have flooded the cliff approach. It would have cost us four litres of emergency water reserve. I am going to go look at the sentry unit now."*

She means both things she says.

## B.3 — FIRING ON THE APEX PREDATOR (DIRECT COMBAT)

### The Decision to Fight

The Tyrant can be killed. The team has the weapons to do it — Mouton's Gun Combat (energy) 3 with a laser pistol does 3D vs effective Armour 4 (energy reduces Armour by 1), and Haramoto's gauss rifle at AP5 bypasses Armour entirely for 3D. A coordinated firefight from inside the cave entrance would likely bring the animal down in 4–6 rounds.

**What the scenario is designed to communicate:** direct combat is the highest-cost solution, not because the team cannot win, but because winning creates more problems than it solves.

**The full consequence chain of killing the Tyrant:**

**Immediate:**
- Thermal spike: +25% TSU-9 load for 2 hours per combatant firing energy weapons
- Automatic ECM failure logged (the thermal event is visible to Marchetti-Solis's passive sensors)
- Acoustic signature: three to six weapons firing at close range, detectable at 5km by Osei-Bonsu's survey scanner
- Blood: significant volume of apex predator blood in the river basin, in the delta, at the cliff base

**Short term (Hours 1–6 after the kill):**
- Osei-Bonsu's biotelemetry tag on KR-ALPHA-7 goes inactive (heart rate drops to zero). He receives an automated alert within 6 hours. He will investigate.
- Stalker packs in the basin follow the blood scent to the cliff base. The carcass attracts scavengers. Scavengers attract Stalkers. The cliff base becomes a feeding site.
- The juvenile Tyrant, previously lurking at the treeline, is now the dominant animal in the territory. It is younger, less predictable, faster. Its territorial behaviour is not established — it will investigate the cave entrance in ways the adult, knowing better, would not.

**Medium term (Hours 6–24):**
- Osei-Bonsu finds the carcass at the cliff base with gauss rifle entry wounds. He is a survey scientist. He knows what a gauss rifle does to an animal. Geological survey teams do not carry gauss rifles.
- His contact report now includes *"KR-ALPHA-7 killed by what appears to be directed gauss fire. Weapon consistent with military-grade equipment. Location: grid KR-4418. Previously-noted anomalous activity."*
- This contact report reaches Marchetti-Solis within 4 hours of filing. She now has: confirmed anomalous thermal event (from the firefight), confirmed military-grade weapon use, confirmed location.
- She contacts Ibáñez-Ochoa. Marines dispatched. 90 minutes.

**The count:** killing the Tyrant directly saves the team approximately 30 minutes of managing the animal. It costs them 6–18 hours of escalation buffer with the Imperial threat layers. This is the trade-off the scenario is designed to make visible.

## B.4 — THE EARLY EXTRACTION ATTEMPT

### What Happens If the Team Transmits EXTRACT NOW Before Hour 8

A player might decide that the situation is too dangerous to continue and push to extract immediately — before the surveillance run completes, before Pelczar finishes her decryption, potentially before the data package transmits at all.

This is a valid choice. The scenario does not punish it as a wrong answer. But it has consequences.

**If EXTRACT NOW is transmitted before the surveillance run completes:**

The data package is incomplete. Pelczar can transmit whatever she has — partial surveillance data, her six days of preliminary decryption work, the fragments that do not yet add up to anything coherent. The strike team receives a partial package. Their mission brief for the relay destruction is based on incomplete technical data.

Whether they proceed with the destruction on a partial package is a decision the scenario does not make — that is for SolSec planners. The referee can use the following outcomes based on table preference:

| Partial Package Quality | Strike Team Decision | Campaign Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| >70% complete | Proceed — the missing data is non-critical | Relay destroyed on schedule; some technical details uncertain |
| 50–70% complete | Proceed with caution — elevated risk of complications | Relay partially destroyed; one system may survive and require a follow-up |
| <50% complete | Delay — request BLIND COBRA re-insert or find another option | Relay survives; full mission failure; campaign pivot |

**If EXTRACT NOW is transmitted before Pelczar completes any decryption passes:**

Partition 3 goes with her as encrypted raw data. It will eventually be processed at SolSec headquarters — but without the three decryption passes, it is unreadable cipher. The intelligence it contains, including the first signals of the Rebellion, remains locked until someone with equivalent processing capability and the correct cipher approach works through it. This could take months or years. The intelligence may arrive too late to be relevant.

Pelczar's private assessment of this outcome: she does not share it in the moment. On SHEPHERD, in jump space, she will write three paragraphs in her personal log that she will later encrypt to the point of theoretical unbreakability.

## B.5 — THE HARD COMPROMISE: SURRENDERING TO MARCHETTI-SOLIS

### If the Team Surrenders Before Marines Breach

If the team chooses to surrender after Voss's loudspeaker challenge but before Marines enter the base, the following sequence occurs:

**The geological survey cover holds for 48–72 hours under standard Imperial processing.** The cover company is legitimate — SolSec built it carefully. The documents are clean. The characters' backgrounds are internally consistent. A standard Imperial customs and intelligence screening will not immediately break it.

**What breaks it:**

| Timeframe | Breaking Point |
|---|---|
| 48 hours | Inconsistencies in the survey company's activity records — no filed survey reports despite 23 days on site |
| 72 hours | Marchetti-Solis cross-references BLIND COBRA's signals fingerprint against her 12-year database of anomalies |
| 96 hours | Breckenridge's signals signature matches the burned Imperial asset file from 12 years ago |
| 7–10 days | The cover company's Alpha Crucis registration traces to a Confederation shell corporation pattern |

**The cover's single exploitable gap for the team:** The 48-hour window. If SHEPHERD can execute a diplomatic or covert rescue within 48 hours of capture — an unlikely but not impossible scenario requiring SolSec's backchannel contacts in the Domain of Sol — the team can be extracted before the cover breaks.

Mrazová will not execute an unauthorised rescue approach. Her protocol is firm. However, the 48-hour window is long enough for SolSec headquarters to be notified (via SHEPHERD's abort transmission when the team goes silent) and to initiate whatever backchannel options exist. The referee should decide whether those options are available based on campaign needs.

**DC-3 in the surrender scenario:**

Vasiliou's standing order is clear: destroy all classified material on capture. DC-1 and DC-2 are destroyed during the surrender sequence — they are in vest pockets, the charges are built into the cases, the team has practiced this. Six seconds per core.

Pelczar will not destroy DC-3. This is not defiance of Vasiliou's order — technically, DC-3 is not on the station manifest and does not exist as official classified material. This is Pelczar's legal interpretation of a standing order she knows is wrong to exploit.

Vasiliou, if she has time, will look at Pelczar before the Marines enter. She will not say anything. She will understand.

DC-3 goes into Imperial custody with Pelczar. Her personal encryption is non-standard — not in SolSec's key inventory. Imperial Intelligence will be trying to crack it for years. If they succeed, they will find the burned Imperial asset file on Breckenridge first. Pelczar knows this. She has made her calculation.

## B.6 — WHAT HAPPENS TO THE BASE

### If BLIND COBRA Extracts Successfully

The base is abandoned. SolSec's protocol for base abandonment: the explosives in the power plant are armed on a 12-hour timer. The camouflage netting is left in place. All classified equipment either leaves with the team or is destroyed.

Twelve hours after extraction, the base's power plant fails in a controlled demolition — a small, hot explosion inside the cliff face that collapses the cave entrance and ruptures the TSU-9's remaining components. From outside, it looks like a geological event — a steam vent failure in the basalt, a not-uncommon occurrence in volcanically active terrain.

**What Osei-Bonsu finds, one week later:**

He flies to KR-4418 on his next survey pass. The cave entrance is collapsed. The camouflage netting is partially buried under debris. There is evidence of a controlled implosion — the damage pattern is wrong for a natural steam event; the angle of collapse suggests interior rather than exterior force. He documents this carefully. He files his report with the IISS survey database, flagged as an anomaly requiring follow-up investigation.

Nobody reads the follow-up request for six months. By then the Assassination has occurred and nobody at the Domain of Sol IISS branch is focused on survey anomalies on forgotten frontier worlds.

The juvenile Tyrant takes up residence in the collapsed cave entrance. It is warm in there. Something smells interesting.

### If the Base Is Overrun

The Marines secure the site. Marchetti-Solis arrives within 8 hours to supervise the intelligence extraction. She finds:

- The TSU-9 Cold Ghost thermal spoofing unit — classified Confederation technology at TL11 software capability. This is the most significant intelligence windfall. She will be very interested in what the Confederation has developed.
- Okonkwo's drone control gauntlet — SolSec technical operations kit, traceable to Confederation Materiel Directorate.
- Breckenridge's SIGINT suite — TL11 signals intercept technology. She will recognise elements of the design philosophy.
- Pelczar's console and the installed SIGINT master suite — if she did not have time to destroy it, this is the second most significant intelligence windfall. TL12 Confederation signals processing capability on a frontier world.
- Whatever the team did not destroy.

What she does not find: DC-3, if Pelczar has it on her. The data package, if it transmitted before capture. Anything Breckenridge encrypted before being taken.

Her after-action report will note, with characteristic precision, that the operation was sophisticated, the equipment was high-end, and the operational tradecraft was consistent with a professional state intelligence service. She will not speculate publicly about which state. She will, privately, know.
