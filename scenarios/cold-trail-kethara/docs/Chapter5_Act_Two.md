## 5.1 — ACT TWO: THE JUNGLE BITES BACK
*Day 23, Hours 2–4*

### The Herd

Motion sensors trigger at approximately Hour 2. Fourteen three-tonne herbivores have wandered into the outer sensor ring — the Kethara Grazer herd that has been grazing the delta margin. They are not aggressive. They are enormous. Three of them have Osei-Bonsu's biotelemetry tags (KR-GRAZE-14, -22, -31 — Kowalski detects these if she is scanning, Science (biology) 6+).

**Handling options:**

| Approach | Mechanic | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Non-lethal drone deterrence | Flyer (rotor) or Recon 8+ | May attract predators; Athletics (dex) 6+ or drone takes 1D damage |
| Let them through | Automatic — wait it out | 4-hour surveillance delay if run not yet complete; netting damage |
| Lethal force | Gun Combat 8+ per target | Blood draws predators in 1D hours; thermal spike +20% TSU-9 for 2 hours; kills tagged animals (Osei-Bonsu investigates within 24 hours) |
| Environmental redirect | Recon + Tactics 10+ | Clean — no side effects on success |

**What Vasiliou says about lethal force:**
> *"Non-lethal. If there's blood in the delta we will have larger problems than the herd."*

She does not explain further. She does not need to.

### The Sweep Begins (Hour 4)

Before the herd is fully resolved — or immediately after, depending on pace — Pelczar's console flashes.

> *"Someone told them something is here. Or they got lucky. Either way — eight hours."*

Marchetti-Solis's atmospheric survey drones have shifted to active search pattern. Eight passes over eight hours — one per hour, Hours 4–12. Each pass is a contested **Electronics (comms)** roll: Breckenridge (skill 3, SIGINT suite DM+2) vs Imperial ECM rating 3.

**Two failures:** Marchetti-Solis signals Ibáñez-Ochoa. Marines dispatched. 90 minutes to boots at KR-7.

**Storm modifier:** During peak hours (4–10), Breckenridge gets DM+1 (background interference works in his favour on raw ECM, but Marchetti-Solis's passive sensors are also more sensitive — net roughly neutral). During clearing (Hours 10–14), DM-1 as storm dissipates.

**Breckenridge's optional countermeasures:**
- **Array reconfiguration:** Electronics (comms) 10+, 2 hours, Haramoto assists — DM+2 on all subsequent passes for 6 hours
- **Frequency spoof:** Electronics (computers) 12+, one attempt — negates one pass entirely on success
- **Drone shadow:** Okonkwo's surveillance drone tails a sweep drone, feeding ECM noise — Okonkwo cannot do anything else while shadowing

**This is the point of no return.** The sweep has started. Everything from here runs simultaneously.

## 5.2 — THE CRASH SITE RUN (HOURS 2–5): ACT TWO BRANCH A

**Trigger:** Haramoto's TSU-9 diagnosis at Hour 1 — jury-rig buys 6 hours but only the crash site coolant canister enables full repair.

The abandoned survey vehicle sits 4km northeast across open delta — Stalker pack territory, no cover, exposed ground. Someone has to go.

**Who goes:**
- **Haramoto** — she needs to identify the right components (Mechanic Average 8+, 30 min on site)
- **Mouton** — escort, perimeter security, Stalker deterrence
- **Kowalski** — if taken: scanner reads Stalker approach, field medic if things go wrong

**Vasiliou's orders for the run:** See Chapter 2, Vasiliou per-character callouts (Haramoto crash site; Mouton escort; Kowalski second option).

**The run — skill sequence:**

| Phase | Skill | Target | Effect on Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delta approach (1km open ground) | Recon | Average (8+) | Stalker pack ambush: 1D Stalkers, Pounce attack |
| Locate parts at site | Mechanic | Average (8+) | 30 min delay, try again |
| Return with coolant canister | Survival | Routine (6+) | Stalker follows scent trail back to base |

**If the Stalker pack is encountered:** Three animals, pounce attack on the rear-most character. Mouton's combat capability is the main deterrent — a burst from the gauss rifle drives them off for 2 hours. Lethal force causes a thermal spike visible to Marchetti-Solis's passive sensors (DM-1 on the next ECM pass).

**The canister:** Allows Haramoto's full repair (Mechanic Difficult 10+, 2 hours). She finishes Hour 7–9. The repair returns TSU-9 to ~85% load — functional and masked.

**If nobody goes:** Jury-rig expires at Hour 7. TSU-9 enters FAILING (141%+). Orbital sensors can detect the thermal signature. Osei-Bonsu receives an automated alert. See master clock Hour 6–7 in Chapter 6.

## 5.3 — MERIDIAN RECOVERY ARRIVES (HOURS 18–24): ACT TWO BRANCH B

*The civilian complication. Not a threat — something worse: a legitimate legal claim with no easy answer.*

Shulgi & Enki Vehicle Hire contracted Meridian Recovery Solutions to locate and recover their abandoned rental vehicle. Investigators Chen and Kaur followed the transponder signal to the Kethara basin. They are not military. They are not armed beyond civilian carry. They have, however, every legal right to be here.

**Chen** arrives first, driving the tan-and-dark-brown recovery car up the track from the northwest. **Kaur** approaches from the far side on the motorcycle. **Holt** (the recovery robot) follows the car on tracks. They are expecting an embarrassed tourist or a broken-down worker, not a SolSec forward operations cell in the middle of an active surveillance mission.

**What they know:** Vehicle transponder in this grid reference. Rental is 23 days overdue. Standard recovery operation.

**What they don't know:** Everything else.

**Vasiliou's orders to Mouton:** *"Two civilians coming up the track from the northwest — rental recovery, non-military. Non-lethal posture. I need them stopped, contained, and not communicating with anyone until I tell you otherwise. Chen is the senior — he's nervous. Kaur is ex-militia — she's going to push. Watch Kaur."*

**The three options:**

| Approach | Skill Required | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Cover story** — geological survey team, vehicle borrowed for equipment transport | Persuade or Deception 10+ (Chen suspicious, Kaur skeptical) | Failure: Chen requests comms check with company; Kaur starts photographing equipment |
| **Detain** — non-lethal, confiscate comms, hold in motor pool | Mouton STR/Melee vs Kaur DEX 13, Brawl 2 if she resists | Escalation: if force used, Chen will press charges planetside; creates record |
| **Negotiate** — offer payment (Vasiliou authorises up to Cr5,000) to delay the report | Broker or Admin 8+, or just the Cr5,000 | Chen accepts; Kaur privately unconvinced but follows his lead; buys 48 hours |

**Chen's behaviour:** Methodical, patient, not easily flustered. Will accept a reasonable story with minimal pressure. Not brave — he will comply with a firmly stated instruction if it doesn't require him to sign anything.

**Kaur's behaviour:** She notices things. Gauss rifle on the rack in the motor pool. Six people for a "geological survey." The TSU-9 ports on the cliff face — she spent three years as a militia engineer and she knows what a cooling array looks like. She says nothing immediately. She is deciding.

**Holt (recovery robot):** Will attempt to attach its winch to the nearest unattended vehicle if not actively redirected. Can be stopped with the remote control (Chen has it) or physically (Structure 8, Hull 6 — not combat capable but solid).

**If Meridian is not resolved within 2 hours:** Kaur quietly activates her personal emergency beacon. It transmits for 20 minutes before anyone notices. Lakamsal Colonial Authority receives the signal in Hour 24–26.
