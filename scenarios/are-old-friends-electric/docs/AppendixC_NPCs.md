## C.1 — BALDWIN WEI (2026-09-09 UPDATE: AGE 66, IY1142)

**Special Consultant, SolSec Directorate · Formerly Solomani Rim Desk**

*"I told them, in 1106, that we don't allow anything to be lost. Nobody believed a thirty-year-old field agent then either."*

Wei's full career file — his 1106 field record, his authorization of Operation Ashfall at 41, and his complicated, half-official information channel to the High Council — is documented in *Operation Ashfall*, Appendix K, §K.2. Everything there still stands. What follows is 25 years further on: the same man, considerably greyer, still not entirely willing to let a case stay closed.

**Career-Prime Characteristics (age 41, IY1117 — unchanged from *Operation Ashfall*, Appendix K):** STR 8 (+0) · DEX 11 (+1) · END 9 (+1) · INT 10 (+1) · EDU 9 (+1) · SOC 8 (+0)

**Ageing Record, 1117–1142:** Wei's total terms served for Ageing-table purposes remains **4** — his entire field career, ending at 30 (*Operation Ashfall*, Appendix K, §K.2). Everything since has been a desk, which is exactly why he shows the mildest ageing on this document's entire cast. Six checks at DM–4 (Core Rulebook, pp. 48–49) over the 25-year gap, mostly landing on "no effect" at that low a DM. Net: **STR –1, END –1**.

**Current Characteristics (age 66, IY1142):** STR 7 (+0) · DEX 11 (+1) · END 8 (+0) · INT 10 (+1) · EDU 9 (+1) · SOC 8 (+0)

**Referee note — why this matters:** hold this stat block up next to Chapter 2's roster. Wei aged gently because he spent thirty-six years mostly not in the field. The six people he's asking for a favour aged the way they did *because* they didn't. He knows this. It is a large part of why this was such a hard ask for him to make, and it should colour every scene he's in.

**Skills (1142):** Leadership 4 · Admin 3 · Advocate 2 · Investigate 2 · Diplomat 1 *(new — three decades of persuasion instead of command has left its mark)* · Electronics 0 · Electronics (computers) 2 · Electronics (comms) 1 · Pilot 0 · Pilot (small craft) 2 · Gun Combat 0 · Gun Combat (slug) 2 · Tactics (military) 1 · Drive 0 · Drive (grav vehicle) 2 · Jack of all Trades 2

**Equipment:** A plain, unremarkable cane he genuinely needs now, not just for cover. A Snub Pistol (TL8, Range 5m, 3D–3, 6-round magazine, Zero-G trait; Central Supply Catalogue, p. 144) he is not currently licensed to carry and carries anyway. A secure comm unit, one generation newer than the one in *Operation Ashfall*. Reading glasses. A single hardcopy photograph, decades old, that he does not show anyone and does not need to be asked about twice.

**Neural.AI image prompt:** A lean, silver-haired man in his mid-60s, deeply lined face, still sharp-eyed despite his age, wearing a plain, unmarked civilian coat. Leaning on a cane that is clearly load-bearing rather than decorative, standing very upright anyway out of old habit. Seated or standing at the edge of a cluttered, dimly lit room — data terminals, paper files, the same "reads too many reports, trusts very few of them" alertness from his younger self, now tempered with visible fatigue. Photorealistic, science fiction character portrait, no text, no logos.

## C.2 — TAVISH RENNER, "REAPER"
**Jardin Syndicate, low-level muscle · Sionnach fringe operator**

*"This is Syndicate space. You want to keep flying it, you pay the toll."*

Born on Sionnach (Jardin Subsector) — one of the neglected Jardin-Cluster worlds the sourcebook itself calls "of little importance" (*Solomani Front*, p. 208). Grandson of **Absalom Renner**, a Confederation courier and informant Perpetua Achterberg ran on this stretch of border more than forty years ago — protected for years, then quietly not, when the border stabilized and the Directorate's attention moved on. Tavish never met Perpetua. He grew up on half-remembered family stories about a grandfather who "used to matter to somebody" and a Confederation that stopped calling. Running small protection rackets for the Jardin Syndicate is not the life he'd have picked if this corner of the Rim had any other economy on offer.

**Characteristics:** STR 7 (+0) · DEX 7 (+0) · END 6 (+0) · INT 7 (+0) · EDU 5 (–1) · SOC 5 (–1)

**Skills:** Pilot 0 · Pilot (small craft) 1 · Gun Combat 0 · Gun Combat (energy) 1 · Melee 0 · Melee (blade) 1 · Streetwise 1 · Persuade 0

**Equipment:** Laser Pistol (TL9, Range 20m, 3D, 100-charge magazine, Zero-G trait; Central Supply Catalogue, p. 149), a Cutlass (TL2, Melee, 3D; Central Supply Catalogue, p. 134) worn more for the look of the thing than any real intent to use it, a scavenged transponder unit rigged to read back to a decommissioned survey vessel, a cheap flight jacket two sizes too big that used to belong to somebody else.

**Personality:** Loud, rehearsed, badly overconfident — right up until he isn't. Everything about his opening approach is performance, learned from older Syndicate hands he's trying to impress. Underneath it is a young man from a world nobody remembers who has genuinely never had anyone explain to him what he's worth, in either direction. Play the bravado as real and the crack underneath it as real too — he is not a joke, and the scene works best when the table realises that at roughly the same moment he does.

**Neural.AI image prompt:** A wiry young man in his early 20s with a shaved head and a cheap, oversized flight jacket, a laser pistol holstered a little too visibly at his hip, a scavenged old cutlass slung across his back more for show than function. Standing in the open airlock of a battered fighting launch, chin up, trying hard to project menace he hasn't fully earned yet. Backdrop of gas giant cloud bands and drifting ring debris. Photorealistic, science fiction character portrait, no text, no logos.

## C.3 — TAVISH'S CREW (2)
**Unnamed · Local muscle, along for the intimidation factor**

Two locals from the same Sionnach settlement Tavish grew up in, recruited more for loyalty than competence. Neither has fired a weapon at another person before this. Both are riding in what the Fighting Launch's own deck plan labels Cargo (Appendix B, §B.3) — there was nowhere else to put them.

**Characteristics (both):** STR 7 (+0) · DEX 6 (+0) · END 6 (+0) · INT 5 (–1) · EDU 4 (–1) · SOC 4 (–1)

**Skills (both):** Gun Combat 0 · Melee 0 · Streetwise 0

**Equipment (shared between the two):** One Autopistol (TL5, Range 10m, 3D–3, 15-round magazine; Central Supply Catalogue, pp. 142–143), one Blade (TL1, Melee, 2D; Central Supply Catalogue, p. 134). They split these before boarding and have already argued about who gets the gun.

**Referee note:** these two exist to make the boarding-action option (Chapter 3, §3.4) feel like it has stakes without actually threatening the crew — Gun Combat 0 with no specialty means they're rolling with real difficulty against any trained defender. Use them for colour and chaos, not lethality.
