# TRAVELLER: ARE OLD FRIENDS ELECTRIC
## Chapter 2: Characters

*Mongoose Traveller 2nd Edition · Ready-to-Play*

---

> Stats are presented as **STR / DEX / END / INT / EDU / SOC**. Each character shows **Career-Prime** characteristics (who they were at the end of active service, before further ageing) alongside **Current** characteristics (who they are now, at the table). Skills, once learned, don't fade; only the body does. Every character here has actually been run through the Effects of Ageing table (CRB:48-49) term by term; nobody's numbers are hand-waved. Full method and citations: Appendix A.
>
> **House rule, parent skills.** Wherever a specialty skill (e.g. "Gun Combat (slug)") is listed above level 0, the parent skill ("Gun Combat 0") is listed too, once per block, covering every specialty of that skill the character has.
>
> Between them, these six can fly a starship, astrogate a covert border crossing, put down suppressing fire, and get through a locked door that doesn't want to open. That's the whole point.

## 2.2: CHARACTER 1: ANTONELLA INTERLENGHI, "DEACON"

![Antonella Interlenghi, "Deacon"](img/deacon-02.webp)
**Team Leader · Solomani Security (SolSec), Retired · Final Rank: Captain**

**Age:** 74 · **Homeworld:** Scaramouche (Harlequin Subsector) · **Cash:** Cr0 · **Debt:** Cr100,000

*"I have talked my way out of worse rooms than this one. Let me work."*

Born on **Scaramouche** (Harlequin Subsector), UWP A7C6503-A, Trade Codes *Fluid Oceans, Non-Industrial, Asteroid Belt* (SF:140), a world of pure Solomani descent that has sat under Imperial allegiance since the Rim War (SF:143-144). Her family emigrated while she was still a small child, part of the Rim Foundation's long-running resettlement program for ethnic Solomani on Imperial worlds, and resettled on a Confederation frontier world in Alpha Crucis. Scaramouche is a birth record to her, nothing more; she grew up entirely within the Confederation, tested and certified Racial Solomani along with the rest of her family as a matter of routine. Got into University at 18 on the strength of her connections, clashed badly enough with one of her tutors there to make a lasting enemy of him, and failed her actual graduation roll outright. She walked out with a degree anyway, the kind of quiet administrative favour that tells you more about her family's standing than her transcript does. Qualified for **SolSec** at 22, a dedicated Confederation career distinct from the generic Core Rulebook Agent path, Field Agent assignment (ACS2:134). Four terms in the field ended when a covert mission into the Imperium was betrayed: arrest, a show trial as a spy, and years in Imperial custody before a prisoner exchange finally brought her home. Not a clean retirement, a trade. She rebuilt her entire SolSec career from Rookie, this time as an Administrator, and made Captain a second time before a bad Advancement roll, not a choice, ended her service for good at 58. She spent everything she had, and borrowed six figures more, on the equipment sitting in her kit right now, because she wasn't going to walk into this one less ready than she used to be.
