*Mongoose Traveller 2nd Edition · Ready-to-Play*

---

> These six characters are designed to cover the scenario's full skill spread between them. A group of 4–5 should select from this roster to ensure no critical skill area is left uncovered.
>
> All characters are **Solomani citizens** with documented SolSec service records (classified). Cover identities show them as employees of Kanzaki Extraplanetary Holdings, a marine infrastructure survey firm. Cover documentation is TL12 quality (DM+2 vs forgery detection checks).
>
> **Character generation note:** These characters have been legitimised using Option A — careers, skills, characteristics, background skills, mustering out benefits, life events, and aging checks have all been applied. Connection +1 skills are **NOT pre-assigned** — see the Connection Rules section at the end of this document for the table handout process.
>
> Stats are presented as STR / DEX / END / INT / EDU / SOC.

## THE CONNECTIONS RULE — TABLE HANDOUT

*Read this aloud or distribute as a card at the table before play begins.*

---

> In Traveller, characters know each other before the campaign begins. Each of you has a pre-defined connection with one other character. When you sit down, find your connection partner and spend 2–3 minutes agreeing on the specific event that links you — use the suggested event as a starting point, or invent something that fits your characters better.
>
> Once you have agreed on the event, **both players gain +1 to any one skill of their choice.** Rules:
> - The skill cannot be raised above level 4 by this bonus
> - You cannot choose Jack-of-all-Trades
> - The +1 must go to a skill your character already has at level 0 or higher
>
> You may make a second connection with a **different** character if time allows. Each connection must be with a different person. Maximum two connections per character.
>
