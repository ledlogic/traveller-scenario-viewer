APPENDIX F: NEW MONDULKIRI — GEOGRAPHY, GEOLOGY AND POLITICAL STATUS

### What the Canon Establishes `[SF]`

The *Solomani Front* describes New Mondulkiri in a single sentence: *"The large starport at the island-capital of New Mondulkiri hosts one of the subsector's largest Imperial Navy bases and an associated Ling Standard Products shipyard."* All further detail below is extrapolated from world data and the broader Gashidda entry.

---

### Geography

New Mondulkiri is a **reef-cap island** in Gashidda's equatorial zone — one of the world's very few permanent surface features above sea level. It sits atop an ancient basaltic seamount, its original volcanic formation now almost entirely buried under millennia of Shelf Architect carbonate-silicate accretion. The island is flat-topped, geologically stable, and chemically inert at surface level — all necessary conditions for the Class A starport and naval installation built on it.

Size is constrained by its geological origin. New Mondulkiri is not large — perhaps 15–20 km at its widest, with low coastal cliffs of pale grey-white arcite mineral on the windward side and a shallow-gradient reef approach on the leeward. The ocean in all directions is the same cool grey-green. Surface weather is persistent and unpleasant: equatorial storm cells circulate around Gashidda's ocean with nothing to interrupt them, and New Mondulkiri sits directly in their path. Wind is constant. Rain is frequent. The sky is rarely fully clear.

The island has no natural freshwater — all water is processed from seawater or recovered from atmospheric condensation. No agriculture. No natural resources of value. It exists because a flat stable surface above sea level is extremely rare on Gashidda, and the Imperium needed one.

---

### The Starport and Naval Base

**Class A Starport** — New Mondulkiri Interplanetary. Imperial territory under standard starport extrality — legally a pocket of the Imperium regardless of the surrounding world's political status. Full refined fuel, complete repair facilities, naval yards, and the Ling Standard Products shipyard occupying the eastern half of the island's industrial zone.

The starport handles the world's entire interstellar traffic, which means every agent, supply shipment, corporate representative, and piece of intelligence flowing to and from Gashidda passes through here. It is the occupation's logistical throat.

**The Naval Base** — one of the largest in Dingir subsector. `[SF]` The Dingir subsector fleet maintains a permanent presence: the subsector fleet flagship (a cruiser or carrier), supporting destroyers, and system defence boats operated directly by the Imperial Navy since Gashidda has no permitted planetary navy under military rule. Additional transport and assault ships are assigned to support Imperial Army occupation duties. `[SF]`

**The cruisers in REEF LIGHT's maintenance berths** are subsector fleet assets, likely mid-range tonnage by Imperial standards (50,000–100,000 tons), in for routine refit between patrols. Their sensor array configurations are of interest to Confederate Naval Intelligence because they reveal the Imperium's current electronic warfare doctrine on the Solomani Front.

**The LSP Shipyard** occupies the eastern industrial zone. Civilian in classification but effectively an extension of the naval base — it handles warship maintenance contracts and the refit cycles of the garrison fleet. LSP's relationship with the Imperial military government is comfortable and mutually profitable. Harada's union activity touched this organisation indirectly: the reactor workers whose unionisation drive was blocked in 1100 include those who service the shipyard's power grid.

### Political Status

New Mondulkiri is the administrative seat of Gashidda's Imperial military government. The military governor's offices are here. So is Director Voss's Naval Intelligence section. So is Harbour Officer Pramanik and the administrative intake the agents process through on arrival.

It is simultaneously the most Imperial and most isolated place on Gashidda. The Gashiddan population lives almost entirely in the undersea arcologies. New Mondulkiri is where the occupiers live — naval personnel, LSP corporate staff, Imperial administrators, and the support population that serves them. It has the atmosphere of a garrison town that knows it is not welcome in the surrounding country and has stopped pretending otherwise.

The Vegan trade attaché Dzhuur Ve-Ashtuk's office is here, in the starport's diplomatic quarter — a small cluster of non-Imperial consular offices that the occupation maintains as evidence of normal commercial relations. Dzhuur's 22 years on New Mondulkiri represent an unusual institutional memory, and the only non-human perspective on the occupation's long arc.
