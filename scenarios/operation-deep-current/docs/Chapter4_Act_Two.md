# TRAVELLER: OPERATION DEEP CURRENT
## Chapter 4 — Act Two: The Current Sets

---

**Estimated table time: 75–90 minutes**

*Act 2 is the operational core. Three objectives run concurrently over several in-game days. Intercut between threads. Apply time pressure. Every choice consumes time. Fourteen days sounds like plenty. It isn't.*

### 4.1 — Operation TIDE MARK — The Frame

**Objective:** Install fabricated financial records in Achterberg's private terminal implicating him in receiving Confederation payments via KEH subsidiaries — suggesting he took kickbacks for steering Imperial Navy infrastructure contracts away from local firms.

**Access options:** Pose as LSP maintenance contractors (requires forged work orders); exploit an environmental complication to access the building during an emergency; or bribe a building maintenance worker through Kofi's criminal network.

**Key checks:**
- **Stealth 10+**: Moving through executive suites off-hours. DM+2 with convincing contractor credentials; DM–2 if Voss Suspicion 3+.
- **Electronics (computers) 10+**: Installing evidence without triggering integrity logging. Failure: Voss +1 Suspicion; 48-hour review clock starts.
- **Deception 8+**: If challenged by Achterberg's staff.
- **Broker or Admin 8+**: Ensuring records look authentic to an independent financial auditor. Success: frame survives 2D6 weeks of scrutiny. Failure: unravels within days (may still be politically effective if Imperial intelligence acts quickly).

### 4.2 — Operation SOUNDING LINE — The Recruitment

**Objective:** Recruit Chief Technician Harada Mitsuko as a long-term SolSec asset.

**Access chain:** Kofi → dockworker **Rashid** → reactor technician **Yael** → Harada. Rashid (recommended) is being monitored by Voss's people.

**The test:** Before meeting the agents, Harada demands help with a genuine problem — a corroded pressure seal on a secondary water-intake pipe in reactor sub-level 7, ignored by administration for three budget cycles. Solve it or she won't listen.

**Key checks:**
- **Persuade 10+** *or* **Engineer (any) 8+**: Building credibility with union intermediaries. DM+2 for revealing actual SolSec affiliation directly to Harada under comm-silence protocols.
- **Recon 8+**: Spotting surveillance on Rashid before approaching. Failure: Voss +1 Suspicion.
- **Engineer (life support) or Mechanic 10+**: Repairing the pressure seal in a hazardous sub-level. Partial fix on failure (2 days before it fails again). *Or* **Admin/Advocate 10+**: Force an emergency repair order through bureaucracy (slower but safer).
- **Persuade 12+** *or* **Persuade 10+ with 2 prior trust successes**: Closing the recruitment pitch. She agrees to be "a sympathiser who will protect certain people and pass certain information." For SolSec's purposes, this is a success.

### 4.3 — Operation REEF LIGHT — The Photograph

**Objective:** Photograph sensor array configurations of two Imperial Navy cruisers in maintenance berths at New Mondulkiri shipyard.

**Access:** Surface access requires Tarquin's fishing-trawler documentation. "**Three-Fin" Valdez**, a Vanthara Truth-sympathising submersible operator, manoeuvres within 400 metres of the maintenance berths during a routine maintenance run.

**Key checks:**
- **Persuade 8+** with Tarquin (requires Scene 1-C success): Obtaining trawler documentation. DM+2 if agents performed a service for the Vanthara Truth community.
- **Electronics (sensors) 10+**: Covert imaging at range, in low light, from a moving platform. DM+1 with Kofi's high-quality imaging kit; DM–2 if Three-Fin's submersible is faster than agreed due to a patrol.
- **Recon 8+**: Identifying optimal approach angle. Success: re-roll the Electronics check if it fails first attempt.
- **Pilot (watercraft) 8+**: Only required if agents operate the submersible themselves.

**OPTIONAL DISCOVERY — THE CARBON COMPOSITE RUDDERS** `[OC]`

During the REEF LIGHT approach, an agent with **Science (engineering) 1+**, **Electronics (sensors) 10+** (Effect 3+), or **Mechanic 2+** who actively studies the cruiser hulls rather than purely photographing the sensor arrays may notice something unexpected: the rudder assemblies on both vessels are not standard naval steel alloy. They are carbon-composite — lightweight, low-magnetic-signature construction of the same class as experimental high-speed craft such as the M-80 Stiletto technology demonstrator class from early Terran naval history.

**Why this matters:** Carbon-composite rudders are unusual on naval vessels of this class. Standard military navies have historically avoided them not for reasons of strength — the material is strong enough — but because carbon composites in sustained deep-water exposure exhibit specific patterns of biofouling and microbial degradation that steel and titanium do not. The Imperium's adoption of carbon composites for major structural components on front-line warships represents either a significant shift in naval engineering doctrine, or an experiment being run on the Gashidda garrison fleet specifically. Either way, Confederate Naval Intelligence will be highly interested.

**The intelligence value:** If the agents photograph the rudder assemblies and deliver this data alongside the sensor array imagery, the REEF LIGHT mission yields a bonus intelligence package. This upgrades the mission outcome by one step (see Outcome Summary).

**What Ikaika knows (if present):** His former Imperial Navy service means he immediately recognises the significance. He can identify the composite grade by visual analysis if the imaging is clean. He says nothing at the time — says it quietly to Sera on the submersible ride back. The look on his face is that of a man recalibrating several assumptions simultaneously.

**What Seluun Mian-Ashta knows (if the agents have made contact):** The Gashiddan reef ecosystem contains organisms whose digestive chemistry is specifically adapted to processing carbon-silicate mineral compounds — the Shelf Architects themselves, and the reef-gas biochemistry that derives from them. An organism that evolved to process Shelf Architect arcite mineral would find carbon-composite ship components nutritionally interesting. Seluun has, in fact, been observing unusual patterns of Shelf Architect growth concentration near the New Mondulkiri naval base for approximately eighteen months. They have not yet connected this to the vessels' construction materials. The agents can connect it for them — or draw their own conclusions.

**Referee note — the longer game:** If the agents report the composite rudder finding to SolSec, Confederate Naval Intelligence will task a follow-up operation to investigate whether the Gashiddan deep reef ecosystem is actively degrading Imperial naval infrastructure — and whether this process can be accelerated or directed. This is a campaign seed, not a scenario objective. Plant the seed; let it grow.

**UNDERWATER HAZARD — VOID MANTLE** `[OC]`

The approach route to the New Mondulkiri maintenance berths passes through the outer reef-column zone at 30–80 metres depth — the lower end of the Void Mantle's documented range. Void Mantles do not follow vessels; they are ambush predators, motionless and invisible until contact. The primary risk is during any hull-walk, external equipment deployment, or unplanned diver entry — not the submersible transit itself, which moves too quickly for a Void Mantle to track.

If any agent leaves the submersible during REEF LIGHT — to attach equipment, clear a fouled sensor housing, or in an emergency — roll 1D:

| Roll | Void Mantle Status |
|------|--------------------|
| 1–4 | None in immediate area |
| 5 | One present at 20–40m distance; Recon 10+ to detect before entering tentacle range |
| 6 | One present within tentacle range of the berth approach; automatic encounter unless Recon 10+ (DM+2 if diver carries electrostatic deterrent rod) |

Full Void Mantle combat statistics and encounter rules are in **Appendix J**.
