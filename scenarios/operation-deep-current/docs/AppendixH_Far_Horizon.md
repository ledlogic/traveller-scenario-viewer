### RUNNING THE SHIP IN PLAY

**Day-to-day:** The *Far Horizon* should feel like a working ship — rotating cover duties, port authority check-ins, occasional legitimate cargo queries. It is the team's one safe space and their exit. Protecting it is as important as any objective.

**If port clearance is denied (Voss Suspicion 5):** Three options — Broker 10+ bribe (Cr 20,000+); KEH emergency transponder contact (6-hour delay); or civil disturbance from Tarquin's or Harada's network (costs a favour).

**Jump-2 emergency:** Requires 80 tons of fuel. The ship holds 40 tons standard. Emergency refuelling at New Mondulkiri would attract immediate attention. This option exists on paper; the team escapes at Jump-1 or not at all.

**Deck Plan (written):**
*Upper Deck:* Bridge forward (two pilot stations, navigator console, engineering monitor, panoramic viewport); captain's stateroom aft-port; navigator's stateroom aft-starboard.
*Mid Deck:* Common room/galley forward (bolted table, six seats, galley alcove, water recycler); EM-shielded briefing room port-side (marked "Survey Planning"); three further staterooms port and starboard; low berth compartment aft-starboard.
*Lower Deck:* Cargo bay forward (survey submersibles, equipment, concealed locker port-side aft); engineering compartment aft (power plant, jump drive coils, manoeuvre drive access); fuel tankage along hull flanks; ventral airlock at forward centreline.

## RUNNING THE SHIP IN PLAY

**Day-to-day cover maintenance:** The *Far Horizon* should feel like a working ship. Agents rotating cover roles, port authority check-ins, the occasional legitimate cargo query from the KEH front. These are not obstacles — they are texture. The ship is the team's one safe space and their exit. Protecting it is as important as any objective.

**If port clearance is denied (Voss Suspicion 5):** The emergency departure protocol can execute a Jump-1 departure in 10 minutes from cold start — but this requires all five crew positions to be occupied and functioning. If agents are scattered across the arcology when the order comes, extraction to the ship is the Act 3 crisis before the departure crisis. The survey submersible pods can reach the surface from Makara in approximately 40 minutes under normal conditions; 25 minutes at flank speed (risk of detection by port authority sonar).

**Jump-2 emergency use:** Requires topping up to 80 tons of fuel, which the *Far Horizon* does not carry in normal operations. The ship holds 40 tons standard. Emergency refuelling at New Mondulkiri would attract attention. This option exists on paper; in practice, the team escapes at Jump-1 or not at all.

**The concealed turret:** Players may ask about arming it. There is nothing to arm it with at Gashidda — all available weapons at New Mondulkiri are Imperial Navy controlled. It is a post-mission refit option, not a combat asset.
