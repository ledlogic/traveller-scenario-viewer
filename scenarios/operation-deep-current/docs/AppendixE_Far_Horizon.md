### RUNNING THE SHIP IN PLAY

**Day-to-day:** The *Far Horizon* should feel like a working ship — rotating cover duties, port authority check-ins, occasional legitimate cargo queries. It is the team's one safe space and their exit. Protecting it is as important as any objective.

**If port clearance is denied (Voss Suspicion 5):** Three options — Broker 10+ bribe (Cr 20,000+); KEH emergency transponder contact (6-hour delay); or civil disturbance from Tarquin's or Harada's network (costs a favour).

**Jump-2 emergency:** Requires 80 tons of fuel. The ship holds 40 tons standard. Emergency refuelling at New Mondulkiri would attract immediate attention. This option exists on paper; the team escapes at Jump-1 or not at all.

**Deck Plan (written):**
*Upper Deck:* Bridge forward (two pilot stations, navigator console, engineering monitor, panoramic viewport); captain's stateroom aft-port; navigator's stateroom aft-starboard.
*Mid Deck:* Common room/galley forward (bolted table, six seats, galley alcove, water recycler); EM-shielded briefing room port-side (marked "Survey Planning"); three further staterooms port and starboard; low berth compartment aft-starboard.
*Lower Deck:* Cargo bay forward (survey submersibles, equipment, concealed locker port-side aft); engineering compartment aft (power plant, jump drive coils, manoeuvre drive access); fuel tankage along hull flanks; ventral airlock at forward centreline.
