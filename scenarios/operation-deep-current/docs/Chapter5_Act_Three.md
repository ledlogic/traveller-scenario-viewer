## ACT THREE: DEEP WATER

**Estimated table time: 45–60 minutes**

*Act 3 begins with crisis. Director Voss has moved to arrest Kofi Mensah-Tran.*

**Trigger:** Voss Suspicion 3+: moves on Day 10. Suspicion 2 or below: moves on Day 13.

Warning arrives via: Harada's union contacts, Tarquin's network, or Kofi's emergency signal — *"current shifts."*

---

### Scene 3-A: Kofi's Compromise

Kofi is moving through the submersible dock district with a two-agent plainclothes tail. Agents must extract him to Tarquin's network and a sealed reef-survey station safe house.

**Environment:** Lower industrial tiers — cargo conduit tunnels, pressurised passages, submersible berth airlocks, emergency bulkheads. Filter masks recommended. Amber work-light, patchy. Surfaces: wet.

**Key checks:**
- **Recon 10+** *or* **Streetwise 10+**: Identifying surveillance assets before they lock on. Failure: the tail identifies at least one agent's face.
- **Athletics (dexterity) 8+**: Moving at speed through the industrial tier.
- **Stealth 10+**: Evading without engagement. At 3 accumulated failures: Voss calls Naval Infantry reinforcement.
- **Persuade or Leadership 8+**: Steadying Kofi's nerve. Success: DM+1 on his checks in this scene.

Note: if combat occurs, the operation is severely compromised regardless of outcome. Shooting Imperial Naval Intelligence assets on an occupied world is not recoverable.

---

### Scene 3-B: The Final Calculation

**Accelerated completion:** All primary skill checks increase by one difficulty band during the Act 3 crisis window. Penalty waived for objectives where all preparatory steps were completed in Act 2.

**Moral check — TIDE MARK:** If the frame is still incomplete, completing it now means destroying a man while running for your lives. Let this moment breathe.

---

### Scene 3-C: Departure

Port clearance: **Deception 8+**. DM–2 if Voss Suspicion 4+; DM–4 if Suspicion 5.

**If Suspicion 5:** Choose one — Broker 10+ bribe (Cr 20,000+); or KEH corporate contact via transponder (6-hour delay); or civil disturbance from Tarquin's/Harada's network (costs a favour).

> *As the Far Horizon lifts from the island port pad and clears Gashidda's atmosphere, the ocean falls away below. The equatorial reef shelf, faintly luminescent even in daylight. Makara, somewhere under that water. The people they met. The things they set in motion.*
>
> *The deep current moves slowly. But it moves.*
