# TRAVELLER: OPERATION DEEP CURRENT
## Chapter 5 — Act Three: Deep Water

**Estimated table time: 45–60 minutes**

*Act 3 begins with crisis. Director Voss has moved to arrest Kofi Mensah-Tran.*

**Trigger:** Voss Suspicion 3+: moves on Day 10. Suspicion 2 or below: moves on Day 13.

Warning arrives via: Harada's union contacts, Tarquin's network, or Kofi's emergency signal — *"current shifts."*

---

### 5.1 — Scene 3-A: Kofi's Compromise

Kofi is moving through the submersible dock district with a two-agent plainclothes tail. Agents must extract him to Tarquin's network and a sealed reef-survey station safe house.

**Environment:** Lower industrial tiers — cargo conduit tunnels, pressurised passages, submersible berth airlocks, emergency bulkheads. Filter masks recommended. Amber work-light, patchy. Surfaces: wet.

**Key checks:**
- **Recon 10+** *or* **Streetwise 10+**: Identifying surveillance assets before they lock on. Failure: the tail identifies at least one agent's face.
- **Athletics (dexterity) 8+**: Moving at speed through the industrial tier.
- **Stealth 10+**: Evading without engagement. At 3 accumulated failures: Voss calls Naval Infantry reinforcement.
- **Persuade or Leadership 8+**: Steadying Kofi's nerve. Success: DM+1 on his checks in this scene.

Note: if combat occurs, the operation is severely compromised regardless of outcome. Shooting Imperial Naval Intelligence assets on an occupied world is not recoverable.

---

### 5.2 — Scene 3-B: The Final Calculation

**Accelerated completion:** All primary skill checks increase by one difficulty band during the Act 3 crisis window. Penalty waived for objectives where all preparatory steps were completed in Act 2.

**Moral check — TIDE MARK:** If the frame is still incomplete, completing it now means destroying a man while running for your lives. Let this moment breathe.

---

### 5.3 — Scene 3-C: Departure

Port clearance: **Deception 8+**. DM–2 if Voss Suspicion 4+; DM–4 if Suspicion 5.

**If Suspicion 5:** Choose one — Broker 10+ bribe (Cr 20,000+); or KEH corporate contact via transponder (6-hour delay); or civil disturbance from Tarquin's/Harada's network (costs a favour).

> *As the Far Horizon lifts from the island port pad and clears Gashidda's atmosphere, the ocean falls away below. The equatorial reef shelf, faintly luminescent even in daylight. Makara, somewhere under that water. The people they met. The things they set in motion.*
>
> *The deep current moves slowly. But it moves.*

### 5.5 — Hot Extraction (Optional — Cover Burned)

*This scene replaces Scene 3-C if the group's cover is fully burned and Voss has committed Imperial forces to stopping them.*

**Trigger:** Voss Suspicion 5, or agents identified by name, or active Imperial pursuit underway.

**The situation:** The *Far Horizon* is impounded or under watch. Standard departure is impossible. Three-Fin's submersible is the only exit — a 200km run to a pre-arranged deep-water pickup by a Confederate courier vessel, running under the Driftweb layer where Imperial Navy sensors lose resolution.

**Weapons acquisition** — before the run, agents may acquire:

| Source | Availability | Cost |
|--------|-------------|------|
| Three-Fin's emergency cache | Always available | Favour owed |
| RSP-7-G armoury (if boarded) | If not expended | Already acquired |
| Tarquin's sealed caches in Makara lower tier | Requires 30-minute detour | Favour owed; +2 to Voss track |
| Black market, startown | Broker 10+ or Streetwise 10+ | Cr 5,000–15,000; illegal |

**The pursuit:**

Imperial Navy assigns a Shafin-class patrol boat and two Sea Ranger RIBs to intercept. The submersible has a 15-minute head start if extraction from Makara goes cleanly.

| Phase | Environment | Key Checks | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-----------|-------|
| **Makara exit** | Lower industrial tiers, filter mask zone | Stealth 8+ or Athletics (dex) 8+ | Each failure: +1 pursuer closes |
| **Surface crossing** | New Mondulkiri harbour, open water | Seafarer (submarine) 10+ | Three-Fin drives; DM+2 from her skill |
| **Driftweb layer transit** | 4–8km altitude equivalent underwater acoustic | Electronics (sensors) 8+ | Reduces Shafin's detection DM by 2 |
| **Deep water run** | Open ocean, 200km | Seafarer (submarine) 8+ | 3-hour run; fuel critical |

**Combat opportunity — Sea Ranger intercept:**

If pursuit catches the submersible at the surface crossing, two Sea Rangers attempt to board. This is the scenario's sanctioned firefight — the agents are no longer on Imperial soil, they are in international waters, and SolSec will not penalise them for a clean contact engagement.

*Sea Ranger crew:* 4 naval personnel per boat. Gun Combat (slug) 1, Athletics 1. Armed with service pistols (no heavy weapons — boarding rules of engagement). Hull 10, Armour 3. The RIBs are fast but unarmoured.

**Combat-capable character advantage:** A character who can suppress the Sea Rangers from the submersible's hatch or drive them off with accurate fire buys Three-Fin the minutes she needs to dive below their waterline operating depth. Gun Combat (slug or energy) 8+, Effect 2+: one RIB crew suppressed for 2 rounds. Two suppressed results: both RIBs break off.

**Avoiding combat entirely:** Electronics (sensors) 10+ before the surface crossing can identify the RIBs' approach vector and allow a dive before contact. Seafarer (submarine) 10+ from Three-Fin plots a reef-column course that breaks pursuit sensor lock.

**The Shafin:** Does not pursue below 50 metres. If the submersible dives clean, the Shafin cannot follow. The laser turret can engage surface targets at 10km — if the submersible is still surfaced at that range, one turret shot (4D damage to hull) before dive is possible. Hull 180 absorbs this. Structural integrity check if Effect 4+: Mechanic 8+ to prevent a systems fault during the deep run.

**Outcome:** Agents reach the Confederate pickup point regardless of combat result — the scenario does not end with capture unless the group chooses that. What varies is condition on arrival: wounded, low on fuel, with damaged equipment, but alive. SolSec values agents who survive a burned operation and know what burned it.
