## APPENDIX D: CAMPAIGN SEEDS

**Harada's Network (1117+):** Harada's union becomes a SolSec comm relay node. Imperial Intelligence notices unusual encrypted traffic. The agents are sent back — but the political situation on Gashidda is changing rapidly as the Rebellion begins to fracture the occupation command structure.

**Voss Returns:** Promoted for her near-capture of the Gashidda network, Voss now covers the entire Dingir subsector. She has an incomplete dossier on the agents. She will surface again.

**Achterberg's Aftermath:** The frame worked too well. Achterberg wants to defect in exchange for Imperial Navy infrastructure intelligence. SolSec must decide whether to accept him.

**Kofi's Breaking Point:** Extracted: burned out and carrying invaluable knowledge. Captured: Voss has months to exploit his psychological vulnerabilities.

**Tarquin's Movement:** The Vanthara Truth's fanatical core is growing, and they are beginning to treat SolSec's cooperation as leverage. A reckoning is coming within the movement.

**The Rebellion Begins (1116–1117):** When Strephon is assassinated and the Imperium fractures, Gashidda will be a flashpoint. The agents seeded something — but in the chaos of civil war, no one controls what it grows into.
