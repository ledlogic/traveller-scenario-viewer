## 1.2 — HISTORICAL TIMELINE — GASHIDDA AND THE SOLOMANI FRONT

*This timeline provides essential context for the scenario. The year 1116 is chosen deliberately: the Third Imperium stands at the apparent height of its power, yet the Solomani Confederation has never stopped fighting — and in the corridors of SolSec, intelligence assessments from 1115 and early 1116 suggest something is shifting at the highest levels of Imperial politics. The agents do not know what. SolSec's analysts do not know what. But the briefings carry a new urgency that wasn't there two years ago.*

### Deep History

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| **–3500** | Gashidda settled by Kimashurgur dissidents from the collapsing First Imperium. The equatorial reef shelf is colonised; the first undersea cities are founded on Shelf Architect mineral formations. `[SF p.185]` |
| **–2400 to –2200** | Gashidda develops as a significant regional capital under the late First Imperium. Deep-sea aquaculture and marine biotechnology make it wealthy by the standards of the era. `[SF p.185]` |
| **–1776 to –1526** | The Interstellar Wars. Terran forces conquer the First Imperium. Gashidda is taken by the Terran Confederation. Heavy immigration from South Asia reshapes the population's cultural and genetic character within four generations. `[SF p.185]` |
| **–1526 to –1400** | Rule of Man (Second Imperium). Gashidda prospers briefly as a naval supply node. `[SF p.185]` |
| **–1400 to –600** | The Long Night. Interstellar civilisation collapses. Gashidda's undersea cities survive in isolation. By the end of this period the world is entirely Solomani in culture and identity — one of the most purely Solomani worlds in what will become the Solomani Rim. `[SF p.185]` |
| **–600 to 0** | Gashidda joins the expanding Third Imperium as a willing partner. Its population are proud of their Solomani heritage but comfortable within the new order. `[SF p.185–186]` |

### The Solomani Era and the Rim War

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| **604** | The Solomani Autonomous Region established. Gashidda is well within its boundaries. Pro-Solomani sentiment, always strong, becomes politically dominant. `[SF p.24, p.119]` |
| **871** | The Solomani Confederation formally declared. Gashidda secedes from the Third Imperium without hesitation and becomes one of the Confederation's most committed worlds. `[SF p.119, p.234]` |
| **940** | The philosopher **Vanthara Noy** emerges as the dominant intellectual voice of the Gashidda Solomani Party. He articulates a vision rooted not just in racial politics but in near-mystical reverence for Terra as the birthplace of original Humaniti — the "First Sky" theology that will become the foundation of the Vanthara Truth movement. Gashidda becomes an exporter of Solomani revolutionary thinking throughout the subsector. `[SF p.186]` |
| **990** | The Solomani Rim War begins. Gashidda is one of the most fervently mobilised Confederation worlds — industrial production at full capacity, population almost entirely supportive of the Cause. `[SF p.186]` |
| **1002** | Imperial forces turn the tide of the war. Gashidda refuses to surrender even as neighbouring systems fall. `[SF p.103, p.116]` |
| **1004–1008** | Imperial assault on Gashidda. A four-year campaign combining orbital bombardment, amphibious assault on New Mondulkiri, and systematic reduction of the undersea cities. The population fights for every level of every arcology. The Imperium wins. The cost is significant and the hatred runs deep. `[SF p.186]` |
| **1008** | The Solomani Rim War formally ends. The Confederation survives, diminished. The peace settlement leaves Gashidda on the Imperial side of the new border. `[SF p.116, p.234]` |

### The Occupation Period (1008–1116)

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1008–1030** | Imperial Army counter-insurgency operations. Active guerrilla networks are systematically found and dismantled. SolSec cells are neutralised or go deeper underground. The last active guerrilla unit is broken by 1030. The Imperium declares the insurgency defeated. It is correct about the guns. It is entirely wrong about what that means. `[SF p.18, p.186]` |
| **1030** | 160,000 Imperial Army Nautical Force Command troops remain on occupation duty. No withdrawal date is announced. Ling Standard Products is given control of the New Mondulkiri starport and shipyard, beginning a long process of corporate consolidation of upper-tier arcology infrastructure. `[SF p.83, p.186]` |
| **1045–1060** | Cultural resistance solidifies. With political and military resistance suppressed, the population turns to spiritual and cultural expression. The **Vanthara Truth** movement — drawing on Vanthara Noy's pre-war philosophy — grows rapidly through meeting halls officially registered as cultural clubs. The **Wheel of Earth** movement emerges as a related mystical splinter. Neither can be effectively outlawed without creating martyrs. Both are watched. `[SF p.186]` |
| **1078** | SolSec's long-range strategic assessment identifies the Vanthara Truth network, the fusion reactor workers' union, and Gashidda's organised crime underworld as the three primary subversion vectors for a future operation. The assessment is filed and periodically updated. **Kofi Mensah-Tran** is placed as a sleeper agent in the cargo-submersible district of Makara. `[OC]` |
| **1090** | **The Makara Crisis.** Imperial authorities attempt to arrest Vanthara Master **Tenzin** in the Makara arcology. The action triggers massive riots across the city. Solomani militants seize the Makara university, the fusion reactor complex, and the submarine docks — holding them for eleven days. The Imperial Army's **Operation Sea Dragon** retakes the arcology with minimal civilian casualties, a model of underwater special operations. Tenzin dies during the operation. He becomes a martyr of the first order. Bloody riots follow and continue for weeks. The occupation tightens. The hatred deepens. `[SF p.186]` |
| **1090–1110** | Organised crime networks — many with surviving connections to the old pre-1030 SolSec cells — consolidate control of the lower hab-rings. Drug distribution, cargo submersible hijacking, and protection rackets fund a resistance culture the Imperium cannot eradicate without destroying the economy it depends on. `[SF p.186]` |
| **1100** | The Imperial military government blocks a Makara fusion reactor workers' unionisation drive, citing credible intelligence that it is controlled by Solomani agitators. The crackdown radicalises the workforce, elevates **Harada Mitsuko** as an unofficial union leader, and intensifies anti-Imperial sentiment throughout the industrial working class. `[SF p.186; Harada Mitsuko is OC]` |
| **1110–1115** | Relative stability — the quiet of a pressure vessel. No major incidents. Imperial patrols continue. The Vanthara Truth continues to grow. Kofi Mensah-Tran continues his deep-cover assignment, showing increasing signs of psychological strain in annual check-in reports. SolSec's updated assessments begin to use the phrase *"readiness window approaching."* `[OC]` |

### The Year 1116 — Why Now

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| **Early 1116** | SolSec's strategic planning division authorises **Operation Deep Current** — the long-planned Gashidda subversion mission. Timing is driven by three converging factors: Kofi's assessment that the Vanthara Truth network is at peak organisational strength; intelligence that Councillor Achterberg has accumulated enough personal debt to make the financial frame viable; and classified signals intelligence suggesting unusual activity in Imperial high politics that SolSec's analysts cannot yet characterise but believe may create a narrow window of reduced Imperial counter-intelligence capacity. `[OC]` |
| **Mid-1116** | The *Far Horizon* is prepared. Cover identities are established under the Kanzaki Extraplanetary Holdings shell. The agent team is briefed and dispatched toward Gashidda. `[OC]` |
| **Scenario present day: latter half of 1116** | The agents have fourteen days on Gashidda. `[OC]` |

> What the agents do not know — what no one outside a small circle of Imperial insiders knows — is that **Emperor Strephon will be assassinated in 1116**, triggering the Rebellion that will tear the Third Imperium apart. The occupation of Gashidda, the Solomani Front, the entire strategic context of the scenario — all of it is standing on the edge of a cliff that only you can see. You do not need to use the Rebellion in this scenario; it runs cleanly as a standalone. But if you want to deepen the experience, consider: the slight distraction in Imperial counter-intelligence posture, the urgency in SolSec's briefings, the agents' sense that history is accelerating without knowing why. The deep current is moving. Even SolSec doesn't know how fast.

## 1.3 — Overview

Operation Deep Current is a social-heavy covert operations scenario in which the players portray SolSec field agents tasked with destabilising Imperial control over **Gashidda** through subversion — social, economic, and political — rather than violence. Gashidda is an Amber Zone under Imperial military occupation, its population deeply sympathetic to the Solomani Cause despite the defeat of all active resistance.

The scenario takes place primarily in **Makara**, one of Gashidda's largest undersea arcologies. `[SF]` The world-building draws heavily on the hostile colonial atmosphere of **Hiroshima Colony**: `[HC]` sealed industrial habitats, tainted atmospheres, corporate control of basic infrastructure, and workers whose lives depend on equipment that the authorities are not interested in maintaining. Players should feel the claustrophobic weight of a world that is dangerous even in its quiet moments.

**The three mission objectives:**
1. **TIDE MARK** — Economically: frame Imperial loyalist Councillor Deven Achterberg with fabricated evidence of accepting Confederation payments, destroying his financial credibility and political usefulness.
2. **SOUNDING LINE** — Socially/Politically: recruit fusion reactor chief technician Harada Mitsuko as a long-term SolSec asset, embedding Confederate sympathies deep in Makara's industrial workforce.
3. **REEF LIGHT** — Intelligence: photograph Imperial Navy warship sensor configurations in the New Mondulkiri shipyard for Confederate Naval Intelligence.

**SolSec values agent survival and network integrity above mission completeness.** Make this clear to players. A partial success with an intact cover is worth more than a sweep with a burned network.
