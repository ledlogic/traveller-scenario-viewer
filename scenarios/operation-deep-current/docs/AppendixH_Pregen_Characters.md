## THE CONNECTIONS RULE — TABLE HANDOUT

*Read aloud or distribute as a card before play begins.*

---

> In Traveller, characters know each other before the campaign begins. Each of you has a pre-defined connection with one other character. Find your connection partner and spend 2–3 minutes agreeing on the specific event that links you — use the suggested event as a starting point or invent something better.
>
> Once agreed, **both players gain +1 to any one skill of their choice.**
> - Cannot raise a skill above level 4
> - Cannot choose Jack-of-all-Trades
> - The +1 must go to a skill already held at level 0 or higher
>
> A second connection with a **different** character is possible if time allows. Maximum two connections per character.
>
> **Pre-defined connection pairs:**
> - Sera ↔ Ananya
> - Ikaika ↔ Casimir
> - Dmitri ↔ Tern
