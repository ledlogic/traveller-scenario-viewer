# TRAVELLER: OPERATION DEEP CURRENT
## Chapter 3 — Act One: Descent

**Estimated table time: 45–60 minutes**

*The arcology elevator descends from the island starport into the equatorial reef shelf, and Makara comes into view through the hull viewports — a vast pressurised city built into the living rock of the reef, its lights casting warm colour into the surrounding blackness of the deep ocean. Bioluminescent reef columns glow blue-green in the distance. The elevator hisses as pressure seals engage. Welcome to Makara.*

---

### 3.1 — Scene 1-A: Arrival and Cover Establishment

The agents process through administrative intake as a legitimate survey crew. The intake official — Harbour Officer **Desh Pramanik** — is thorough but bored. He is also on Director Voss's informant list, though the agents don't know this yet. Simultaneously, Kofi makes contact carrying a sealed packet of gikhi-eel eggs — the recognition signal. The operational package handoff happens in a 90-second encounter that looks like a commercial transaction.

**Key checks:**
- **Deception 8+**: Maintaining cover through administrative check. Failure by Effect 2+: Voss +1 Suspicion within 24 hours.
- **Recon 8+**: Noticing whether Pramanik is paying unusual attention. DM+1 with prior SolSec briefing.
- **Streetwise 8+**: Reading Makara's social atmosphere — passive resentment runs deep; people speak carefully in public.

---

### 3.2 — Scene 1-B: Safe House Briefing

A maintenance contractor's apartment in the mid-tier hab-ring. Recycled air tasting faintly of ocean, kelp-protein cooking from a neighbour. Kofi provides full operational context and warns of unfamiliar watchers near the reactor complex.

**Environmental complication:** A ventilation fault triggers a building announcement — *"residual biological off-gassing may reach trace hazard levels; filter masks recommended."* This is routine. Use it to establish that this world has its own hazards running concurrently with the mission.

**Equipment acquisition:** The operational package includes Kofi's pre-arranged procurement contacts for survey and diving equipment through **Seaharvester LIC** distribution channels — the company has active marine biology and aquaculture contracts throughout Gashidda's equatorial zone, making their gear unremarkable in any survey context. Standard KEH cover equipment is already aboard the *Far Horizon*; Kofi's contacts provide access to Makara-local procurement for anything additional needed without routing purchases through LSP-flagged channels.

| Item | TL | Cost | Availability in Makara | Notes |
|------|----|------|----------------------|-------|
| Swimming Equipment (fins/mask/snorkel) | 3 | Cr100 | Freely available, dockworker supply shops | Without it: DM–2 and speed halved on all underwater tasks |
| Dive Suit (basic wet/dry) | 6 | Cr60 | Freely available | Cold water protection; compatible with tanks/rebreather/gills |
| Rebreather (6 hours) | 6 | Cr250 | Available via Seaharvester contact | Silent — no exhaust bubbles; important for REEF LIGHT approach |

HEV suits with submarine modification are military-adjacent and must be sourced grey-market through Kofi's lower hab-ring contacts — this takes 48 hours and costs 20% above listed price. `[CSC]`

**Key checks:**
- **Investigate or Recon 8+**: Assessing Kofi's intelligence quality and psychological stability. Failure: miss that Kofi's hands are slightly unsteady — he is more frightened than he's showing.
- **Electronics (computers) 8+**: Decrypting the operational package from a slightly corrupted one-time key. Failure: one intelligence item missing until Act 2.
- **Medic 6+**: Identifying the atmospheric issue as minor off-gassing, not an attack. Success: DM+1 to Persuade with mid-tier residential NPCs for the rest of the scenario.
- **Broker 8+** (optional): Negotiating equipment procurement costs. Success by Effect 2+: 10% discount and faster delivery on Seaharvester items.

### 3.3 — Scene 1-C: First Contact with the Vanthara Truth

Kofi has arranged a meeting with Priya Selvaratnam at a communal eating area. She tests the agents with coded Vanthara Truth theological probes: the "first sky," the "second breath," the "returning current." SolSec training covers this material.

**Key checks:**
- **Persuade 10+**: Convincing Priya the agents are trustworthy. DM+1 for demonstrated theology knowledge; DM+2 for pure Solomani bloodline; DM–2 for apparent contempt of the religious framing.
- **Carouse 8+**: Scanning for Imperial surveillance. Success: a plainclothes soldier two tables away, watching generally, not yet focused on the agents.

**Success:** Priya arranges a meeting with Tarquin — required for REEF LIGHT.
**Failure:** No betrayal — Priya simply doesn't trust them. Another path to Tarquin is possible but slower.

### 3.4 — Act 1 Complications

Roll 1D at any dramatically appropriate moment during Act 1 — on a failed skill check, during a tense transition between scenes, or when the referee wants to add pressure. Each complication is designed to cost time or attention without derailing the act.

| Roll | Complication | Effect |
|------|-------------|--------|
| **1** | **Wrong eyes at the transit hub.** Harbour Officer Pramanik takes longer than expected with the KEH documentation, asking specific questions about the survey contract scope. He is not suspicious — he is thorough, and his thoroughness is on record. **Deception 8+** to satisfy him cleanly. Failure: he files a supplementary arrival note with Naval Intelligence within 6 hours. Voss +1 Suspicion. |
| **2** | **Reef-gas advisory escalates.** The ambient off-gassing in the mid-tier hab-ring ticks from trace to minor hazard during the safe house briefing. The building environmental system announces a 4-hour ventilation restriction. Anyone moving through the lower mid-tier corridors without a filter mask takes DM–1 to END-based checks. **Medic 6+** to identify this as routine; failure means the agents waste 30 minutes waiting it out, compressing their briefing window. |
| **3** | **Kofi is late.** He does not appear at the recognition point at the agreed time. He arrives 40 minutes later, visibly rattled — he spotted a familiar face near the transit hub he couldn't place. **Recon 8+** or **Streetwise 8+** to assess whether the face was genuine surveillance or paranoia. Failure: the agents cannot determine which, and must proceed with uncertainty. Voss Suspicion +1 if the referee decides it was real; no effect if paranoia. |
| **4** | **Priya's test is harder than expected.** When the agents meet Priya Selvaratnam, she has already heard a rumour — Kofi's name, not theirs, mentioned in a dockworker conversation she overheard. She doesn't say this. She tests harder. **Persuade 10+** (up from standard 8+ if this complication is rolled before Scene 1-C) and **DM–2** unless an agent demonstrates specific knowledge of the 1090 Makara Crisis and Tenzin's death, not just general theology. |
| **5** | **Imperial Army spot-check.** A two-soldier patrol stops the agents in the mid-tier market corridor and requests ID verification. Routine — this happens to everyone in Makara. Cover IDs pass on **Deception 8+** (DM+2 from TL12 documentation). Failure by Effect 2+: the soldiers radio the IDs for deeper background check, results in 2 hours — the agents need to be somewhere else before then. |
| **6** | **Makara's infrastructure reminds everyone who is in charge.** A pressure seal failure in the hab-ring above the safe house floods a service corridor with seawater, triggering an emergency response. Imperial Army engineers arrive within 20 minutes to manage the crisis — and are now in the building. The safe house briefing must be abbreviated or the agents must wait 3 hours for the all-clear. **Medic 6+** or **Engineer 6+**: helping with the emergency buys goodwill with two residents (useful contacts later) but delays the briefing and puts soldiers' faces in memory of the safe house location. |
