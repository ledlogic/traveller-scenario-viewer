# TRAVELLER: OPERATION DEEP CURRENT
## Chapter 2 — Cast of Characters & Skills

## 2.1 — PLAYER BRIEFING

*Read or paraphrase to players before play begins.*

---

You are SolSec field agents. Your covers are those of a freelance technical survey crew aboard the subsidised merchant *Far Horizon*, arrived at Gashidda's New Mondulkiri starport on a routine contract to audit deep-sea infrastructure integrity on behalf of **Kanzaki Extraplanetary Holdings (KEH)** `[HC p.24–25]` — a Kyoto-based speculative investment house specialising in frontier resource financing, operating through modest financial liaison offices in working colonies. Its low profile and avoidance of direct extraction operations makes it attract less scrutiny than major conglomerate movements. On Gashidda it serves as a SolSec financial front. `[OC]`

The year is **1116**. Gashidda has been under Imperial military occupation for over a century. The Imperium is unpopular and its counter-intelligence apparatus is alert and capable. Your local contact is **Kofi Mensah-Tran**, a six-year deep-cover SolSec sleeper showing signs of psychological strain. Treat him carefully.

**Your mission has three objectives over a two-week window:**

1. **TIDE MARK:** Plant fabricated evidence implicating Councillor Deven Achterberg in receiving Confederation payments.
2. **SOUNDING LINE:** Recruit Chief Technician Harada Mitsuko of the Makara fusion reactor complex as a long-term SolSec asset.
3. **REEF LIGHT:** Photograph Imperial Navy warship sensor array configurations at New Mondulkiri shipyard.

Operational security is paramount. The *Far Horizon* departs in fourteen days.

**Your SolSec handler is CASE OFFICER YUSUF ADEYEMI-CROSS**, External Directorate, currently operating from the Solomani Confederation courier vessel *Tamsin Reach* at the Dingir system jump point — six days out from Gashidda at standard transit speeds. He authorised the operation and conducted the mission briefing before departure. He knows the objectives, the assets, and the cover. He does not know what you will find when you get there.

**Communication protocol — COMM SILENCE:**

SolSec doctrine for occupied-world operations is strict: **no outbound communication to handler during the operational window.** Every transmission from Gashidda to a Confederation vessel is a potential intercept. Voss's Naval Intelligence section monitors all non-commercial encrypted traffic departing New Mondulkiri and the arcology comm relays.

| Situation | Protocol |
|-----------|----------|
| Routine progress | No contact. The *Far Horizon*'s departure is the acknowledgement. |
| Mission abort required | Single burst on KEH emergency transponder — triggers 24-hour extraction response, no verbal content |
| Handler contact before departure | Not permitted under standard doctrine — requires Effect 4+ on a Deception check at the New Mondulkiri comm relay to avoid flagging |
| Post-mission debrief | Adeyemi-Cross will rendezvous with the *Far Horizon* at the Dingir jump point on departure |

The emergency transponder is a single-use device. Using it ends the operation. Adeyemi-Cross will extract the team — but the objectives become someone else's problem, on someone else's timeline, with a burned cover and a alerted Voss.

**Background and personality:** Dzhuur has been the Vegan trade attaché at New Mondulkiri for twenty-two years, which means they were in post before most of the current garrison officers were born. They remember the Rim War — not as history, but as personal memory. They remember what the Confederation's forces did to Vegan worlds before the Imperial counteroffensive. This does not make them a reliable ally for SolSec. It makes them someone with a long, complicated relationship with the idea of the Solomani Cause, and a very specific kind of wariness about both sides of this conflict.

**Tuhuir:** `[SF]` Dzhuur belongs to the **Muisstagz Muan Ewmiak** — the Guardian tuhuir, the Vegan civil service. This is the tuhuir whose purpose is to oversee and mediate among all others, grounded in the Sacred Covenant, the ancient Vegan ethical document that treats crime and violence as anathema and establishes the legal mechanisms for peacefully resolving conflict. For Dzhuur, this is not merely employment — it is a philosophical identity chosen at age 50 after years of *irrishtyoshun*, the Vegan period of search and pilgrimage. The Guardian tuhuir's worldview shapes everything about how Dzhuur approaches the situation: they do not act precipitously; they observe, evaluate, and seek the interpretation of events that causes the least harm and best serves the long-term stability of the Covenant's principles.

This is precisely why Dzhuur has not reported the agents. Guardian tuhuir practice is to be certain before acting, and to consider what action actually serves peace. Reporting a suspected SolSec cell to Imperial Naval Intelligence will cause harm — arrest, potentially violence, certainly disruption. Not reporting may cause different harm. Dzhuur is, in their patient, unhurried way, still working out which harm is lesser. They have perhaps ten days left before the *Far Horizon* departs and the question becomes moot. They are aware of this.

What Dzhuur has noticed: the *Far Horizon*'s crew moves like off-worlders, their survey certification is slightly newer than the ship's wear pattern suggests it should be, and one crew member's Gashiddan dialect is native-quality in a way that doesn't match their stated biography. Dzhuur has not reported this. Yet.

**In play:** Dzhuur can be a source of extraordinary intelligence — they know the political landscape of New Mondulkiri better than anyone in the scenario except possibly Voss — but engaging them is inherently risky. They are not hostile to the agents, but they are not sympathetic to the Confederation's racial ideology, and they will not be manipulated. The one angle that might work: genuine, non-ideological conversation about what a stable post-occupation Gashidda would actually look like. Dzhuur has thought about this for twenty-two years. They have opinions.

**The Vanthara Truth complication:** If Tarquin learns the agents have spoken with a Vegan official, he will want to know why. The Vanthara Truth's Solomani mysticism has no comfortable place for the Tyui. Dzhuur, who has lived on Gashidda for two decades, is aware of this tension and navigates it with the careful patience of someone who has made peace with being tolerated rather than welcomed.

**Characteristic notes:** `[SF]` As a Vegan, Dzhuur has EDU+2 applied (reflected in EDU 14), SOC-2 applied (reflected in SOC 11 — still high due to position and age), IR vision via eye membrane, and doubles all penalties for high gravity — Gashidda at 0.45G is comfortable; standard-gravity environments would trouble them significantly.

**Tuhuir:** `[SF]` Seluun belongs to the **Irriszhia Tuhuir** — the Vegan Scout Service, the tuhuir responsible for space exploration, whose name translates literally as *space exploration*. Its philosophical foundation is curiosity as a form of reverence — the idea that understanding the universe is among the highest expressions of Vegan existence. Seluun is not a serving Scout; they chose the academic path within the tuhuir's broader intellectual tradition, which encompasses scientific research, natural philosophy, and xenobiology. Their intellectual property — including fifteen years of Gashiddan reef research — belongs to the tuhuir and will revert entirely to it at Seluun's death. The tuhuir's financial structure and shared holdings mean Seluun has no personal wealth anxiety; their research is sustained and their position at the university is comfortable regardless of institutional politics.

This tuhuir identity is why Seluun has no difficulty living on a politically volatile occupied world. Irriszhia Tuhuir practice is to go where the knowledge is and remain there as long as the knowledge requires. The reef is worth knowing. They are staying.

The Gashiddan academic community has a wary, largely respectful relationship with Seluun. They are genuinely brilliant, genuinely useful to the university's research reputation, and — unlike the Imperial garrison — they are here because they want to be, not because they were ordered to be. The Vanthara Truth regards them as a philosophical problem they have agreed not to examine too closely. Seluun is aware of this arrangement and finds it faintly amusing.

**In play:** Seluun is useful primarily if the agents need environmental or biological intelligence about the reef approaches to New Mondulkiri, alternative submersible routes, or cover for legitimate deep-sea survey activity. They will share research information with fellow scientists without ideological complication. They are also the character most likely to make the players feel the moral texture of the setting from an unexpected angle — a being from a species that the Confederation's racial ideology would exclude, who has chosen to live on an occupied world because the work matters more than the politics, and who has 200 years ahead of them and a very long view of what occupation and liberation actually look like across time.

**Characteristic notes:** `[SF]` As a Vegan, Seluun has EDU+2 applied (reflected in EDU 15 — exceptional even by Vegan standards), SOC-2 applied, IR vision, heat tolerance, and high gravity intolerance. Gashidda at 0.45G is ideal for them physically. Their three manipulator tentacles (in place of fingers) make delicate specimen work natural; the cracked sample staining on their hands is a permanent feature of their working life.
