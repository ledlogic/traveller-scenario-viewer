APPENDIX K: THE VANTHARA TRUTH — SOURCE DESCRIPTION AND SOLSEC SUBVERSION DOCTRINE
### Canon Reference and Operational Analysis `[SF]`

---

### Where the Vanthara Truth Appears in Canon

The Vanthara Truth is described in the *Solomani Front* supplement specifically within the **Gashidda world entry**. The full canon passage reads:

> *"Many citizens belong to mystical societies such as Vanthara Truth and Wheel of Earth that promote a spiritual understanding of the Solomani Movement and reverence for Terra as the birthplace of original Humaniti. This movement has become too widespread for the occupation to effectively outlaw and has a fanatical core linked to militant violence and terrorism. Imperial attempts at suppression have only succeeded in creating martyrs."*

That is the entirety of the source description. From this the scenario derives: the name, the category (mystical society), the theological core (spiritual Solomani Movement, reverence for Terra as birthplace of original Humaniti), the political resilience (too widespread to outlaw effectively), the dual nature (mainstream spiritual + fanatical militant core), and the Imperial suppression problem (every crackdown creates martyrs).

Everything beyond this in the scenario — Vanthara Noy as the founding philosopher, the First Sky and Second Breath theology, the Wheel of Earth as a splinter movement, Tarquin's oceanic tattoos, Priya's coded theological tests — is original content built on that canon foundation. `[OC]`

---

### SolSec's Operational View of the Vanthara Truth

**The External Directorate doctrine** `[SF]`

SolSec's External Directorate is explicitly described in the source as being *"deeply involved in attempts to prop up and manipulate the various Solomani Party and Solomani extremist groups in the Rim."* Its methods include *"funding, supporting, infiltrating and creating legitimate non-violent Solomani Party political organisations — such as pro-Solomani student organisations — and extremist guerrilla and terrorist groups."* It is also engaged in *"military, industrial and political espionage."*

The strategic rationale is stated directly: it is considered *"important to keep hope alive in the occupied systems"* and resistance must be maintained *"in order to ensure the Imperium cannot assimilate the occupied worlds and use them as bases for future conquest."*

The Directorate is also noted for its long-term thinking: *"a SolSec agent may try to befriend or seduce someone from an influential or noble family while they are a young student."* This is the doctrine that produced Kofi Mensah-Tran's 38-year deployment timeline — Gashidda's subversion assets were identified in 1078 and have been patiently cultivated ever since.

---

### Why the Vanthara Truth Specifically

The Vanthara Truth fits SolSec's asset profile for four reasons, all grounded in the source:

**1. It cannot be suppressed.** The source states explicitly that the movement *"has become too widespread for the occupation to effectively outlaw."* A movement the Imperium cannot destroy without creating martyrs is self-sustaining and self-protecting — it imposes a perpetual cost on the occupation without requiring Confederation military commitment. Every attempted crackdown strengthens it. The 1090 arrest of Vanthara Master Tenzin and the riots that followed are the proof case.

**2. It provides pre-built infrastructure.** The Vanthara Truth has been constructing meeting halls, communication networks, trusted relationships, and community loyalty for decades — for entirely non-violent spiritual reasons. SolSec gains access to this infrastructure without having built it. Tarquin's fishing-trawler documentation, his secure comm network across Makara, his ability to shelter Kofi in a reef-survey station safe house — all of this exists because the Vanthara Truth has been operating as a civic institution for a generation. SolSec's External Directorate doctrine of supporting *"legitimate non-violent political organisations"* is precisely what the Vanthara Truth represents in its mainstream form.

**3. It manages the militant spectrum.** The source notes the movement has *"a fanatical core linked to militant violence and terrorism."* SolSec's doctrine covers the full spectrum from non-violent parties to extremist groups. The Vanthara Truth's peaceful mainstream provides community cover and plausible deniability; its fanatical core provides, when needed, actors who are already committed to violence and whose actions cannot be traced to Confederation direction. Tarquin is not merely a spiritual leader — he is the person who maintains the boundary between the mainstream and the core, and who decides when and whether that boundary shifts. His value to SolSec is partly that he keeps the fanatical core disciplined; his leverage over SolSec is that he could choose not to.

**4. It is ideologically aligned but operationally independent.** The Vanthara Truth's theology — Terra as birthplace of original Humaniti, the First Sky as sacred — is entirely compatible with the Solomani Cause but is not the Solomani Party. The Imperium cannot outlaw reverence for Terra without appearing to attack religion itself. The movement's spiritual framing gives its members genuine cover: a Vanthara Truth meeting hall is, legally, a cultural club. This operational ambiguity is exactly what SolSec needs on an occupied world with Law Level 9.

---

### The Long Timeline — Why 1116

The strategic assessment identifying the Vanthara Truth, the reactor workers' union, and the organised crime networks as Gashidda's three primary subversion vectors was filed in 1078. Kofi Mensah-Tran was placed as a sleeper agent the same year. Operation Deep Current is authorised in early 1116 — 38 years later.

This is not unusual by SolSec's standards. The source describes an agency that thinks in generations, not campaign seasons. The 38-year preparation period reflects the External Directorate's doctrine of patience: Kofi's six years of deep cover are the *activation phase* of a network that has been passively developing for nearly four decades. The agents arriving on the *Far Horizon* are not building something new. They are harvesting something that SolSec planted before most of them entered service.

This is also why the scenario's moral texture is what it is. The Vanthara Truth did not choose to become an SolSec asset. Its members — Tarquin, Priya, the tens of thousands of ordinary Gashiddans who attend meetings and perform observances — are pursuing a genuine spiritual and cultural practice. SolSec's relationship with the movement is parasitic in the precise sense: it takes what it needs without disclosing what it is doing. Tarquin knows something is happening. He does not know the full shape of it. The agents do not tell him.

---

### Referee Notes — Using This in Play

The Vanthara Truth should feel like a real institution, not an asset. Its members have lives, beliefs, and community obligations that exist entirely independently of SolSec's operational needs. The twice-daily Deep Bell migration observance is a genuine religious practice. The grief over Tenzin's death is genuine. The ordinary Gashiddans who attend Vanthara Truth meetings are not Solomani militants — they are people trying to maintain cultural identity under occupation.

The agents' use of this institution as an operational resource should feel like what it is: the instrumentalisation of a community's most private beliefs for political ends. SolSec considers this entirely justified. The scenario does not tell the players whether it is right. It shows them what it looks like.

**Tarquin's dual role** is the sharpest expression of this tension. He is simultaneously: a genuine spiritual leader with deep pastoral responsibility for his community; a pragmatic political actor who has been managing the relationship between the mainstream movement and its fanatical core for decades; and a man who knows the agents represent something he cannot fully control and is choosing to engage with anyway. He is not naive. He has calculated that whatever SolSec is doing on Gashidda is worth the risk to his community. He may be right. He may not be. The scenario does not resolve this for him.

### The Vanthara Truth — Cultural Practice Reference `[OC]`

*The following section provides concrete cultural detail for running Vanthara Truth encounters at the table. All material is original extrapolation from the canon foundation of South Asian Terran heritage and Solomani spiritual philosophy.*

---

#### Cultural Roots

Gashidda's population was shaped by heavy South Asian immigration during the Interstellar Wars era — Sindhi, Punjabi, Tamil, Bengali, Sinhala, Marathi, and Hindi cultural threads, carried across the stars and refracted through fifteen hundred years of ocean-world isolation. The Vanthara Truth is not a transplant of any single Terran tradition but a genuine synthesis: **Dharmic philosophical frameworks** — Hindu and Buddhist conceptual structures, devotional practice, cyclical cosmology — reinterpreted through Solomani political ideology and the specific spiritual weight of an ocean world that cannot see Terra's sky.

By 1116, the movement has been developing internal theology for nearly two centuries. It feels old. It has saints, schisms, regional variations, theological disputes. It is not a political front that happens to use religious language. It is a living tradition that has incorporated political meaning because the political situation of its people is inseparable from their spiritual one.

---

#### Core Theological Concepts

**The First Sky** — the sky of Terra as seen from the surface of Terra itself, before humanity left. The sacred original state; the reference against which all other skies are measured. Gashidda's grey-green overcast is a poor echo — Gashiddans understand themselves as living far from the source, which is theologically meaningful rather than simply sad. Devotional language attaches to it. Practitioners speak of *"turning the face toward the First Sky"* as an idiom for correct spiritual orientation.

**The Second Breath** — the first breath drawn by humanity outside Terra's atmosphere; the founding tragedy of the diaspora and its necessary sacrament simultaneously. Every human breathing on every world is participating in the Second Breath continuously. This makes atmosphere itself sacred — and on Gashidda, the atmosphere is visibly, literally alive. The Driftweb layer that gives the sky its grey-green colour is a biological organism breathing the same air as every Gashiddan. Vanthara Truth practitioners understand this as confirmation of their theology: to breathe Gashidda's air is to participate in a planetary communion, inhaling oxygen produced by an organism that has been alive in this atmosphere longer than humanity has been in space. The filter masks worn in Makara's lower industrial tiers acquire a specifically painful resonance in this context — to require a mask is to be separated from the breathing world by the occupation's industrial legacy. Protecting your breath is a spiritual act.

**The Returning Current** — the eschatological promise. Not a literal return to Terra but the return of Solomani primacy across the arc of history. Time flows like the deep ocean current — slowly, but it moves everything, and eventually it returns to its source. This is the theological foundation of the movement's patience under occupation. The Imperium is a temporary obstruction in a current that has been running for ten thousand years.

**The Wheel of Earth** — the splinter movement's central image, more literally cyclical and more urgently eschatological than the mainstream. Where Vanthara Truth is patient and this-worldly, the Wheel is intense and eschatological, drawing on samsaric imagery. Its practitioners believe the Returning Current is closer than the mainstream acknowledges. They are the wing most likely to produce what the canon calls the "fanatical core."

---

#### Naming and Language

South Asian naming conventions — filtered through fifteen hundred years of cultural isolation and Solomani political identity — give Gashiddan practitioners a characteristic onomastic texture. The population is not monolithic: Tamil, Sindhi, Punjabi, Bengali, Sinhala threads run through Makara's naming patterns alongside heritage from other Terran cultures absorbed during the occupation era.

**Common Vanthara Truth phrases used in normal speech:**

| Phrase | Context | Meaning |
|--------|---------|---------|
| *"May you find the First Sky in all skies."* | Greeting between practitioners | Standard devotional greeting; used casually among community members |
| *"The current does not ask permission."* | Response to injustice or obstruction | The situation will resolve itself in the Cause's favour regardless; patience is the correct response |
| *"We breathe what Terra breathed."* | Expression of solidarity | Used in adversity, grief, or shared hardship |
| *"Slow water carries the deepest stones."* | Counsel to patience | Direct reference to the Returning Current theology; do not act rashly |
| *"The reef remembers."* | Historical grievance | Reference to Tenzin, 1090, and the long memory of the occupied; used carefully in public |
| *"You are turning your face."* | Mild rebuke | Accusation of ideological drift, particularly toward collaboration with the occupation |
| *"The Second Breath is not yet finished."* | Encouragement | The work of the diaspora is not complete; reason for continued action, patience, or hope |
| *"First sky, last sky."* | Farewell between practitioners | A compact blessing; spoken quietly at partings |

**Theological tests used to identify practitioners** — what Priya is doing in Scene 1-C:

She probes for fluency in three concepts. A memorised phrase passes at surface level. Genuine engagement with the theology — showing understanding of *why* the First Sky matters, not just the label — passes at depth. She is listening for whether the agents have been briefed (adequate) or whether they understand (exceptional). The distinction matters to her.

*"Where does the sky remember itself?"* — expected response: a reference to Terra, the First Sky, or the birthplace of original Humaniti. Wrong answer: any specific world other than Terra. Evasion: suspicious.

*"When did we begin to breathe someone else's air?"* — expected: a reference to the Second Breath, the departure from Terra, the diaspora. Wrong answer: any historical event other than humanity's first departure from Terra.

*"What does the current carry back?"* — expected: the Returning Current, Solomani primacy, the eventual restoration of what was taken. Wrong answer: anything material or tactical. A person who answers with a political programme has misunderstood the theology. The correct answer is eschatological, not operational.

#### Physical and Ritual Practice

**Entering a meeting hall:** Footwear removed at the threshold. Two fingers briefly touching the door frame — a threshold prayer, 1–2 seconds, barely perceptible. Meeting halls are identified by a small blue-green wave symbol, often worked into the building's architecture rather than displayed as a sign.

**Seating:** Practitioners sit cross-legged on low floor cushions, not chairs. Meditation postures derived from yoga asanas, adapted for 0.45G — practitioners can hold seated positions significantly longer than standard-gravity worshippers, which is considered spiritually significant. A practitioner who can sit still for an hour is not merely disciplined; they are expressing their relationship with the current.

**Communal meals:** Vegetarian by strong preference. Kelp-protein and reef-vegetable substitutes for Terra-descended foods that cannot grow on Gashidda. The meal is prepared communally and eaten in silence or near-silence. Sharing food with a stranger is a significant act of trust. Being invited to a communal meal after an initial meeting is a sign that the community has provisionally accepted you.

**Offering bowls:** Small brass or brass-analog bowls holding water drawn from the deep reef — not purified water, *reef* water, which is chemically complex and slightly brackish. Placed at meeting hall entrances. Never disturbed. The bowls are replaced at each migration observance, the old water returned to the ocean through a designated outflow. Disturbing or removing an offering bowl is one of the most culturally offensive acts possible.

**Wrist cords:** Thin woven cords, traditionally blue-green, blessed by a Warden-Elder. Worn on the left wrist by Householder practitioners and above. Priya's Wheel of Earth bracelet is a variant — it incorporates the Wheel symbol but serves the same function. These cords are not displayed; they sit under clothing. Spotting one is a sign of paying close attention.

**Tarquin's tattoos:** His head and neck tattoos are ritual markings applied during his elevation to Warden-Elder, not decoration. The ocean-current patterns represent the Returning Current theology; their gradual spiral into galaxy-arm patterns represents the extension of the Solomani Cause beyond Gashidda into the stars. Every Warden-Elder carries some form of elevation marking, though the extent of tattooing varies by regional tradition. Tarquin's are exceptionally extensive — he is a man who committed fully and publicly to his role.

---

#### Observances and Calendar

**The Migration Observance** — the twice-daily Deep Bell school migration creates Gashidda's equivalent of a call to prayer. Practitioners gather at viewports during the 20–30 minute migration window and sit in silence. This happens in public spaces across Makara — communal eating areas, transit corridors with viewports, the mid-tier market. It is not disruptive; practitioners simply stop and sit. The effect is that twice each day, Makara becomes quieter and more still. The Imperial Army has never found grounds to prohibit it.

The timing of the migration is ecologically anchored to Gashidda's confirmed temperature profile. At the equator — where Makara sits — base temperature is a stable 20°C year-round with minimal seasonal swing. This thermal stability means the Deep Bell schools follow a reliable circadian rhythm driven not by seasonal temperature change (negligible at the equator) but by the light cycle and the thermal gradient between the sun-warmed surface layer and the colder deep shelf. Migration happens at dawn and dusk local time, when the gradient shifts. For practitioners in the arcology, whose relationship with natural light is mediated entirely through viewport glass and artificial lighting, the migration observance is one of their few remaining connections to Gashidda's actual biological rhythms. This matters theologically — the migration is proof that the world still lives on its own terms, regardless of who occupies the surface.

The scenario is set in spring/autumn by default (the default season in the temperature table), when equatorial daytime sits around 20°C and the storm belt at mid-latitudes (rows 3–5) is in its most active phase — the 1°C to -8°C base temperatures in that band drive the equator-to-pole temperature differential that powers Gashidda's persistent cyclonic weather. Agents who spend time near external viewports will see storm fronts building on the grey horizon. The equatorial calm within which Makara sits makes the visible violence of the distant storm belt feel like weather happening to someone else — which is, in its way, the Gashiddan condition.

**New Sky Day** — the anniversary of humanity's first departure from Terra. An elegiac observance, not a celebration. Practitioners fast through the morning and break fast communally in the evening. The meal is simple. The mood is the specific Gashiddan combination of grief and defiance.

**Returning Day** — the eschatological hope day. Celebrated with music, communal feasting, and the recitation of the Vanthara Truth's founding texts. This is the movement's most openly joyful day. The Imperial Army is always on alert for it.

**Tenzin's Day** — not an official observance; occupationally illegal as a "commemoration of terrorism." Falls on the anniversary of the 1090 arrest. Practitioners wear a thin blue-green thread on the left wrist — openly, publicly, knowing the Army knows about the thread and cannot ban a thread. The occupation has tried arresting people for it. It made things worse. The thread remains.

#### Organisational Structure

| Rank | Role | Number in Makara |
|------|------|-----------------|
| Householder | Lay practitioner; attends meetings, observes migrations | ~200,000 (est.) |
| Deacon | Junior religious official; leads small group meetings; evaluates newcomers (Priya's role) | ~400 |
| Sub-Elder | Manages a single meeting hall; community pastoral care | ~80 |
| Warden | Administrative authority over a district; coordinates multiple Sub-Elders | ~40 |
| Warden-Elder | Regional authority; Tarquin's rank; meets in council with other Warden-Elders | 12 |
| The Council | Twelve Warden-Elders meeting quarterly; no single authority; decisions by consensus | 1 per quarter |

**Wheel of Earth ranks** follow a parallel structure but with different titles and a stronger emphasis on contemplative practice. The *Silent Ones* — practitioners under vows of near-silence for months or years — are a specifically Wheel of Earth institution. They serve as walking symbols of contemplative resistance. They cannot be compelled to testify because they do not speak.

#### Contacts the Agents Might Use

**Rashid Selvam** — dockworker, Wheel of Earth practitioner, Sub-Deacon. Kofi's initial contact for the SOUNDING LINE approach chain. His Wheel of Earth affiliation makes him slightly more intense than mainstream practitioners — he is already convinced the Returning Current is close. He carries a blue-green cord under his sleeve. He is also, unfortunately, the person Voss's surveillance team has been watching for the last ten days. His ideological conviction makes him reliable; his watchedness makes him a liability.

**Yael Fernandez-Patel** — reactor technician, Householder practitioner. The link between Rashid and Harada in the SOUNDING LINE chain. Her Vanthara Truth membership is the reason Harada trusts her — they attend the same migration observances. She does not know she is part of an intelligence operation. She thinks she is facilitating an introduction between a union activist and some survey engineers who might understand the pressure seal problem. If she learns otherwise, she will feel used. She will not forgive it quickly.

**Brother Arjun** — a Silent One, Wheel of Earth. Present throughout the scenario in background scenes — at migration observances, in communal eating areas, near the meeting hall. He does not speak. He watches everything. He is not under observation by Voss because he has never been heard to say anything, and Voss values words. He notices things. If the agents find a way to communicate with him — written notes, gesture, the wrist cord recognition signal — he knows more about the movements of the occupation in the mid-tier than almost anyone.

**The Offering Bowl Network** — not a person but a practice. Vanthara Truth Deacons leave small chalk marks near offering bowls at specific meeting halls to indicate safe-house status, surveillance presence, or emergency signals. This is a dead-drop communication system operating entirely within the movement's normal ritual practice. Kofi knows the code. If the agents learn it from Kofi, they gain a passive intelligence channel that Voss cannot monitor because she does not know it exists.

#### How to Make It Feel Real at the Table

**Before the Vanthara Truth appears in the scene:** Establish the world's relationship with the movement in small environmental details — a threshold prayer from a market vendor that the agents walk past, a migration observance that briefly stills a crowded corridor, a blue-green wrist cord visible on someone who turns out to be relevant later.

**During Priya's test:** Do not summarise the test. Play it. Let her ask the three theological questions in character. Let the players answer. A player who has read the briefing document can answer competently. A player who improvises something that actually understands the theology — that the First Sky is sacred because it is the *memory* of the original, not the original itself — should be rewarded with a DM+2.

**Tarquin's speaking style:** He never hurries. He refers to the Returning Current by the phrase *"what the water carries."* He uses the familiar form of address with practitioners and the formal form with everyone else — but his use of the familiar form with the agents, when it comes, is a significant moment. He has decided they are practitioners in some sense, even if imperfect ones.

**The migration observance in Act 1:** The first time the agents see it — a corridor falling quiet, people moving to a viewport, sitting in the near-silence while violet-blue bells pulse past in the deep water outside — this is the scenario's first genuinely peaceful moment. Let it breathe. It is followed immediately by Kofi telling them about the watchers near the reactor complex.

**Casimir's Wheel of Earth symbol:** He carries it. He does not bring it up. If another character asks about it, he deflects. If a Wheel of Earth practitioner notices it — and they will, because they are trained to look for wrist cords and carried symbols — the encounter changes texture immediately. He will have to decide what he tells them. There is no clean answer.

APPENDIX G: SPECIALIST FORCES — APPLICABLE MECHANICS `[SF2]`

> *Attribution: Specialist Forces, M.J. Dougherty, Mongoose Publishing. Cited as* `[SF2]` *in this appendix to distinguish from Solomani Front* `[SF]`.

The *Specialist Forces* supplement contains no underwater or aquatic operations content. However four of its mechanics apply directly to this scenario and are reproduced here with scenario-specific guidance.

---

### 1. The Pinch — Intelligence Commando Resolution `[SF2]`

The "pinch" mechanic formalises the seizure of intelligence from an enemy installation. It maps directly onto Operations TIDE MARK (evidence plant) and REEF LIGHT (sensor photography). After the primary skill checks resolve, use the **Covert Operation Resolution** table to determine whether Director Voss traces the operation back to the agents.

The commander of the covert operation makes a **Tactics (military) or Deception check** (DM selected before the operation, +0 to +4 based on how carefully the operation was planned). Difficulty is Average (8+).

| Check Effect | What Voss Knows |
|-------------|-----------------|
| –6 or worse | She knows exactly what was taken and by whom |
| –5 to –3 | She has clear and broadly correct indications |
| –2 to –1 | She has a general idea but cannot be certain |
| 0 | She knows something was taken; cannot identify specific items |
| +1 to +2 | She cannot be sure anything was taken |
| +3 to +5 | She suspects an operation occurred but cannot confirm |
| +6 or more | She is unaware the operation took place |

**Referee guidance:** Roll this after each primary objective is completed. Results accumulate — a +2 on TIDE MARK and a –1 on REEF LIGHT leaves Voss with partial suspicion she must resolve. This feeds directly into the Voss Suspicion Track in the main scenario body.

---

### 2. General Intel Indicator (GII) — Kofi's Six Years `[SF2]`

The GII abstraction gives mechanical weight to Kofi's intelligence-gathering. His six years of deep cover on Gashidda represent sustained, patient intelligence accumulation.

**Starting GII for Gashidda when the agents arrive:** 9 (based on Kofi's six-year tenure plus SolSec's 1078 strategic assessment).

| GII | What This Means in Scenario Terms |
|-----|----------------------------------|
| 9 | Specific information: identities of key figures, up-to-date positions of garrison units, movement patterns of Voss's surveillance assets |

This GII is what allows the agents to have detailed briefing packages — Achterberg's schedule, Harada's access routes, the dead drop locations. If Kofi is captured before the briefing in Act 1 Scene 1-B, GII effectively drops to 3 (SolSec's background research only) and the agents are working nearly blind.

**Intelligence degradation:** Each day in which Kofi cannot safely operate (tail active, hiding, compromised) reduces effective GII by 1 for operational purposes. After 4 days of degradation the time-sensitive intelligence (patrol schedules, Achterberg's movements) is no longer reliable.

---

### 3. Cordon and Sweep — Modelling Voss's ID Sweeps `[SF2]`

Act 2 Complication #1 (Sweep) can be resolved using the cordon and sweep framework. Voss's sweep of the mid-tier hab-ring is an armed security operation against a population with firmly negative Population Reaction baseline.

**Baseline Population Reaction DM for Makara mid-tier:** –3 (sullen and occasionally hostile; one step from throwing things). Any heavy-handed execution of the sweep shifts this to –5 or worse, triggering the kind of incident that would draw resources away from counter-intelligence work — and potentially give the agents cover.

The agents can exploit this: if they can engineer a reason for the sweep to be conducted more intrusively than Voss intends (a tip to a Vanthara Truth contact about the sweep timing, creating a minor incident that forces escalation), the resulting population friction degrades Voss's operational effectiveness and costs her a day. This is a Social/Deception operation, not a combat one.

---

### 4. Recon Specialist Principle — The Submersible Approach `[SF2]`

The book notes that an alert enemy may have sensor posts watching for grav vehicles but can be *"taken entirely by surprise by a team that uses canoes or just walks."* This directly validates Three-Fin Valdez's submersible approach for REEF LIGHT.

New Mondulkiri's naval base will have active sensor coverage of orbital and aerial approaches to the shipyard. It will have sonar coverage of the main harbour approaches. It will not have comprehensive sonar coverage of the outer reef-shelf waters used by fishing and maintenance submersibles, because those waters are full of legitimate traffic generating constant acoustic noise. Three-Fin's approach — slow, running along the reef-shelf at medium depth, blending with maintenance traffic — is the low-tech solution that defeats a high-tech sensor net by being beneath its detection threshold.

**Electronics (sensors) DM summary for REEF LIGHT approach:**

| Approach Method | Detection Risk | Electronics (sensors) DM |
|-----------------|---------------|--------------------------|
| Aerial or grav vehicle | Near-certain detection | N/A — not viable |
| Dedicated naval submersible | High — active sonar sweep | DM–2 |
| Maintenance submersible (Three-Fin) | Low — blends with traffic | DM+0 (base) |
| Three-Fin + Kofi's imaging kit | Low — blends with traffic | DM+1 |
| Three-Fin + prior Recon success | Low + optimal approach vector | DM+2 |
